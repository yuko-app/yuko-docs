You can also open a support ticket from your **Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty**.

## What to include in a support request

To speed up the back-and-forth, please share:

- Your Shopify store URL.
- The Yuko area you're working in (Loyalty, Referral, Memberships, Workflows, etc.).
- A short description of what you expected vs what you saw.
- A screenshot if relevant.

Our support specialists will get back to you quickly with the guidance you need.
