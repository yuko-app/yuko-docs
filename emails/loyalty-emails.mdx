<Note>
**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Emails
</Note>

Yuko has 11 built-in loyalty emails. They all start **inactive**. Switch on the ones you want in **Loyalty → Emails**, and edit the content in the [Template Editor](/emails/template-editor).

| Email | Sent when | Default subject | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Points earned](#points-earned) | A customer earns points | You've Got `{{ points_value }}` `{{ points_label }}` | **Days after earned reward** |
| [Points redeemed](#points-redeemed) | A customer redeems points for a reward | You unlocked a reward! | **Days after redeemed reward** |
| [Tier unlocked](#tier-unlocked) | A customer enters their first VIP tier | You've unlocked a new tier! | **Days after tier changed** |
| [Point Expiry Reminder](#point-expiry-reminder) | Points are about to expire | Your `{{ points_label }}` going to expire! | 3 days before expiry |
| [Points expired](#points-expired) | Points have expired | Your `{{ points_label }}` have expired | **Days after points expired** |
| [Reward expiry reminder](#reward-expiry-reminder) | A redeemed reward is about to expire | Your reward is expiring in `{{days_until_expiry}}` days! | 3 days before expiry |
| [Reward expired](#reward-expired) | A redeemed reward has expired | Your reward has expired | **Days after reward expired** |
| [Birthday Reminder](#birthday-reminder) | A customer's birthday is coming up | Celebrate Your Birthday With Us! | 3 days before the birthday |
| [Birthday award](#birthday-award) | Birthday points are awarded | Happy Birthday, `{{ first_name }}`! | **Days after birthday award** |
| [Anniversary Reminder](#anniversary-reminder) | A customer's anniversary is coming up | Celebrate Your Anniversary With Us! | 3 days before the anniversary |
| [Anniversary award](#anniversary-award) | Anniversary points are awarded | Happy Anniversary, `{{ first_name }}`! | **Days after anniversary award** |

Settings in **bold** are delay fields in the email editor. Set them to **0** to send immediately (the default). The four reminders use a fixed number of days before the date and have no timing field in the editor.

## Points earned

**Sent when:** a customer earns points from any [way to earn](/loyalty/ways-to-earn/overview), including points credited by a workflow.

**Default subject:** *You've Got `{{ points_value }}` `{{ points_label }}`*

**Default message:** Greeting → *"Congratulations! You've earned `{{ points_value }}` `{{ points_label }}` from `{{earn_program_name}}`"* → thank-you line.

**Delay:** **Days after earned reward**.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ first_name }}`, `{{ points_value }}`, `{{ points_label }}`, `{{ earn_program_name }}`, `{{ points_balance }}`, `{{ points_to_next_reward }}`, `{{ redemption_link }}`.

<Tip>**Lead with the number.** "You've Got 50 Points" gets more opens than "Thanks for your order".</Tip>

## Points redeemed

**Sent when:** a customer redeems points for a [reward](/loyalty/ways-to-redeem/overview).

**Default subject:** *You unlocked a reward!*

**Default message:** Greeting → *"You redeemed the following reward `{{ reward_code }}`"* → thank-you line.

**Delay:** **Days after redeemed reward**.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ reward_name }}`, `{{ reward_code }}`, `{{ reward_value }}`, `{{ reward_expire_at }}`, `{{ points_spent }}`, `{{ points_balance }}`.

<Tip>**Put the code front and centre.** Most customers open this email on their phone at checkout.</Tip>

---

## Tier unlocked

**Sent when:** a customer qualifies for a [VIP tier](/vip-tiers/overview) for the first time, moving from no tier into a tier.

**Default subject:** *You've unlocked a new tier!*

**Default message:** Greeting → *"You've unlocked the following tier `{{ current_tier_name }}`"* → thank-you line.

**Delay:** **Days after tier changed**.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ current_tier_name }}`, `{{ next_tier_name }}`, `{{ points_balance }}`, `{{ total_earned_points }}`.

<Note>This email doesn't send when a customer moves **up** from one tier to a higher one, or **down**. For those moments, build a [workflow](/workflows/triggers-and-conditions) on the **VIP Tier Upgrade** or **VIP Tier Downgrade** trigger.</Note>

## Point Expiry Reminder

**Sent when:** some of a customer's points are about to expire. It's listed as **Point Expiry Reminder** in the Emails list.

**Default subject:** *Your `{{ points_label }}` going to expire!*

**Default message:** Greeting → *"Your `{{ points_label }}` balance of `{{ expiring_points }}` will expire in `{{ days_until_expiry }}` days."*

**Timing:** 3 days before the points expire.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ expiring_points }}`, `{{ points_expiry_at }}`, `{{ days_until_expiry }}`, `{{ expiring_points_as_cash }}`, `{{ redemption_link }}`.

<Tip>**Only turn this on if your points expire.** Points expiry is set per earn rule. See [Ways to Earn](/loyalty/ways-to-earn/overview).</Tip>

## Points expired

**Sent when:** a customer's points have expired.

**Default subject:** *Your `{{ points_label }}` have expired*

**Default message:** *"`{{ expired_points }}` `{{ points_label }}` from your account have expired. Your current balance is `{{ points_balance }}` `{{ points_label }}`. Keep shopping to earn more!"*

**Delay:** **Days after points expired**.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ expired_points }}`, `{{ expired_points_as_cash }}`, `{{ points_balance }}`, `{{ points_to_next_reward }}`.

---

## Reward expiry reminder

**Sent when:** a reward the customer redeemed (their discount code) is about to expire.

**Default subject:** *Your reward is expiring in `{{days_until_expiry}}` days!*

**Default message:** Greeting → *"Your reward `{{ reward_code }}` is expiring in `{{days_until_expiry}}` days."*

**Timing:** 3 days before the reward expires.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ reward_name }}`, `{{ reward_code }}`, `{{ reward_value }}`, `{{ reward_expire_at }}`, `{{ days_until_expiry }}`.

---

## Reward expired

**Sent when:** a reward the customer redeemed has expired without being used.

**Default subject:** *Your reward has expired*

**Default message:** *"Your reward `{{ reward_name }}` (`{{ reward_code }}`) has expired. You still have `{{ points_balance }}` points to redeem for a new reward!"*

**Delay:** **Days after reward expired**.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ reward_name }}`, `{{ reward_code }}`, `{{ points_balance }}`, `{{ redemption_link }}`.

---

## Birthday Reminder

**Sent when:** a customer's birthday is coming up and your [Birthday](/loyalty/ways-to-earn/birthday) earn rule is active.

**Default subject:** *Celebrate Your Birthday With Us!*

**Default message:** Greeting → *"As a token of our appreciation, we're offering you `{{ points_value }}` `{{ points_label }}` on `{{birthday_date}}`"*.

**Timing:** 3 days before the birthday.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ points_value }}`, `{{ points_label }}`, `{{ birthday_date }}`, `{{ points_balance }}`.

---

## Birthday award

**Sent when:** Yuko awards a customer's birthday points.

**Default subject:** *Happy Birthday, `{{ first_name }}`!*

**Default message:** *"Happy Birthday! To celebrate, we've added `{{ points_value }}` `{{ points_label }}` to your account."*

**Delay:** **Days after birthday award**.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ points_value }}`, `{{ points_balance }}`, `{{ points_balance_as_cash }}`, `{{ redemption_link }}`.

---

## Anniversary Reminder

**Sent when:** a customer's anniversary is coming up and your [Anniversary](/loyalty/ways-to-earn/anniversary) earn rule is active.

**Default subject:** *Celebrate Your Anniversary With Us!*

**Default message:** Greeting → *"As a token of our appreciation, we're offering you `{{ points_value }}` `{{ points_label }}` on `{{anniversary_date}}`"*.

**Timing:** 3 days before the anniversary.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ points_value }}`, `{{ anniversary_date }}`, `{{ customer_years }}`, `{{ customer_year_label }}`.

## Anniversary award

**Sent when:** Yuko awards a customer's anniversary points.

**Default subject:** *Happy Anniversary, `{{ first_name }}`!*

**Default message:** *"Happy `{{ customer_years }}` `{{ customer_year_label }}` with us! To celebrate, we've added `{{ points_value }}` `{{ points_label }}` to your account."*

**Delay:** **Days after anniversary award**.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ customer_years }}`, `{{ customer_year_label }}`, `{{ points_value }}`, `{{ points_balance }}`.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Start with Points earned, Points redeemed and Tier unlocked.** These three cover the moments customers care about most.</Tip>
<Tip>**Pair reminders with a redemption link.** Add `{{ redemption_link }}` as the CTA URL in expiry reminders so customers can spend their points straight away.</Tip>
<Tip>**Need different timing or extra steps?** Build a [workflow](/workflows/overview) on the same trigger (for example **Points Expiring Reminder** with a **Days Before** filter) and leave the built-in email off.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change when the expiry and birthday reminders are sent?">
    Not from the email editor. All four built-in reminders go out 3 days before the date (points expiry, reward expiry, birthday or anniversary). For other timing, use a workflow with the matching trigger and a **Days Before** trigger filter.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why didn't a customer get the Tier unlocked email after an upgrade?">
    Tier unlocked only sends when a customer enters their first tier. Use a workflow on **VIP Tier Upgrade** for upgrades.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do the birthday emails need the customer's birthday?">
    Yes. Yuko can only send them to customers with a birthday on file, and only while the Birthday earn rule is active.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
