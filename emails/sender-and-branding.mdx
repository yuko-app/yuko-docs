## Introduction

**Email Settings** control who Yuko's emails come from. The settings apply to every email: loyalty, referral and membership emails, and Send Email nodes in workflows. You set them once and don't need to repeat them per template.

By default, emails go out from **Yuko's shared email address** with your From name. To send from your own domain (for example `rewards@send.yourstore.com`), turn on **Custom Domain Emails** and verify the domain.

The **Email Settings** page has two tabs:

- **General**: sender identity, default From address and custom domains (this page).
- **Email Blacklist**: addresses that never receive Yuko emails. See [Email Blacklist](/account/email-blacklist).

## Setup guide

### 1. Set your sender identity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Email Settings">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Account → Email Settings → General**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a From Name">
    In **Sender Identity**, set **From Name**, which is *"the default 'From' name for emails"*. Use a name customers recognise, such as your store or rewards program name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a Reply Email">
    Set **Reply Email**, the default Reply-To address. Customer replies go here, so use an inbox your team reads.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** in the Sender Identity card. Both fields are required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 2. Send from your own domain (optional)

**Custom Domain Emails** is available on select plans.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Custom Domain Emails">
    In the **Custom Domain Emails** card (*"Send emails from your own domain instead of the default Yuko shared email"*), switch the toggle on. The badge changes from **Disabled** to **Enabled** and the **Mail Domains** section appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your domain">
    Click **Add Domain**, enter the **Domain Name** (for example `send.yourdomain.com`) and click **Create Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the DNS records">
    Click **View DNS** next to the domain. Yuko lists the **DKIM**, **SPF** and **CNAME** records, each with its **Type**, **Name** and **Value** and a copy button. Add each record at your DNS provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the domain">
    Back in the DNS dialog, click **Verify Domain**. Each record shows **Verified** or **Unverified**. DNS changes can take a while to propagate. If a record is still unverified, wait and click **Verify Domain** again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a From address">
    Once a domain is verified, the **From Addresses** section appears. Click **Add From Address** and enter a **From Email Address** on your verified domain (for example `rewards@send.yourdomain.com`). Addresses on a verified domain are verified automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 3. Choose the default From address

In **Default Email Addresses**, pick the **Default From Address**:

- **Yuko shared email**: the default, which needs no setup.
- Any verified From address you added.

Click **Save**. Every Yuko email now uses this From address. To go back to the shared address, select **Yuko shared email** and save.

## Logo

The logo in your emails comes from **Account → General → Logo** (*"Display logo in emails and other communications"*). In the [Advanced Editor](/emails/template-editor#advanced-editor) you can also place it with the **Logo** block.

## How it works

- **From name and Reply-To** from Sender Identity are used on every email.
- **From address** is the Default From Address. If none is set, or you delete the address that was the default, Yuko falls back to the shared system address.
- **Custom domains** prove to inbox providers that Yuko is allowed to send for your domain (through DKIM and SPF), which helps deliverability and shows your own domain to customers.
- **Monthly limit:** if your store has a monthly sending limit, a banner on each module's Emails tab shows it. Contact the Yuko team to raise it.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Use a subdomain for sending,** such as `send.yourdomain.com`. It keeps loyalty email reputation separate from your main domain.</Tip>
<Tip>**Use a Reply Email someone reads.** Customers reply to points and reward emails with questions.</Tip>
<Tip>**Avoid `noreply@` From addresses.** A real-looking address like `rewards@` builds more trust.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a custom domain?">
    No. Emails send from Yuko's shared address out of the box. A custom domain is optional and is available on select plans.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My domain stays Unverified. What should I check?">
    Make sure every DKIM, SPF and CNAME record matches the **Name** and **Value** shown in **View DNS** exactly (use the copy buttons). Some DNS providers add your domain to the end of the name automatically, so check for duplicates. Then wait for DNS to propagate and click **Verify Domain** again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I add a From address?">
    You need at least one verified domain first. Until then, Yuko shows *"No Verified Domains Available"*. The address must also use your verified domain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I set a different sender per email?">
    No. The From name, Reply-To and From address apply to all Yuko emails.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I stop emails going to certain addresses?">
    Use the **Email Blacklist** tab next to General. See [Email Blacklist](/account/email-blacklist).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
