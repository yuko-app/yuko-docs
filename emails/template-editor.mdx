</Note>

## Introduction

Every Yuko email opens in a simple **form editor**: you fill in the subject, title, message and footer, and the preview updates beside it. For full control over layout, switch to the **Advanced Editor**, a drag-and-drop email builder (available on select plans).

## The form editor

The left panel holds the email's settings and content fields. The right panel shows **Stats** and a live preview.

### Email fields

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Email Subject** | The subject line. *"Displayed to customers in their inbox."* |
| **Preview Text** | *"Text that appears next to the subject line in inboxes."* |
| **Email Title** | The heading at the top of the email. Rich text. |
| **Email Message** | The main body. Rich text. |
| **Email Footer** | Footer text below the message. Rich text. |
| **CTA Button** | Tick **Use a CTA Button** to add a button, then fill in **CTA Button Text** (up to 60 characters) and **CTA URL**. The URL field already shows your store's address, so enter only the page path, for example `collections/all`. You can also enter a single shortcode such as `{{ redemption_link }}` as the whole link. Untick the box to remove the button. |
| **Unsubscribe Text** | The unsubscribe link text, for example *"Unsubscribe"*. |
| **Unsubscribe Description** | Text shown with the unsubscribe link. |

You can use [shortcodes](/emails/shortcodes) such as `{{ first_name }}`, `{{ points_label }}` or `{{ wallet_balance }}` in the subject, preview text and content fields. The **Available Template Variables** card at the bottom of the panel lists every shortcode for this email. Click the copy icon next to one and paste it into a field. If the list is long, click the **Show … more** button at the bottom of the card to see all of it.

### Delay settings

Most built-in emails have a delay setting at the top of the panel, so you can send the email some days after the event instead of immediately. Enter **0** to send right away.

| Email | Setting |
|---|---|
| Points earned | **Days after earned reward** |
| Points redeemed | **Days after redeemed reward** |
| Tier unlocked | **Days after tier changed** |
| Points expired | **Days after points expired** |
| Reward expired | **Days after reward expired** |
| Birthday award | **Days after birthday award** |
| Anniversary award | **Days after anniversary award** |
| Referrer welcome / Friend reward unlocked / Referrer reward issued | **Days after Referrer welcome** (and so on) |
| Membership created / upgraded / downgraded / cancelled | **Days after membership created** (and so on) |

<Note>The four reminder emails (**Point Expiry Reminder**, **Reward expiry reminder**, **Birthday Reminder** and **Anniversary Reminder**) have no delay setting in the editor. They go out a set number of days *before* the date. See [Loyalty Emails](/emails/loyalty-emails) for the defaults.</Note>

### Send a test

Click **Send Test Email**, then **Send**. The test goes to **your account email** (the address shown in the dialog). You can't choose a different recipient. Test emails use sample data for the shortcodes.

### Save

Click **Save** at the top of the page. Saving also stores the delay setting.

## Advanced Editor

Click **Advanced Editor** at the top of the form editor to open the drag-and-drop builder. It's **available on select plans**. If your plan doesn't include it, the button shows an upgrade icon.

In the Advanced Editor you can:

- Drag in layout blocks (one to four columns, mixed columns) and content blocks such as **Text**, **Heading 1–3**, **Button**, **Image**, **Divider**, **Spacer**, **Logo**, **Unsubscribe** and **Coupon**.
- Switch between **Desktop** and **Mobile** views, and undo or redo changes.
- **Import** MJML (paste it into **MJML Content**) or **Export** the current design as MJML.
- **Clear canvas** to start over (this removes all content).
- **Preview** the email, **Send Test**, browse **Shortcodes**, open **Analytics** and **Save**.

Once you save an email in the Advanced Editor, it shows an **Advanced** badge in the Emails list and opens in the Advanced Editor next time.

<Tip>**Show a workflow coupon in an email.** In a workflow, add a [Coupon node](/workflows/action-nodes#coupon) before the Send Email node. In the Advanced Editor, drag in the **Coupon** block and pick that node's identifier. The customer's unique code appears in the email.</Tip>

## Best practices

<Tip>**Keep the subject short and specific.** *"You've Got 50 Points"* beats a generic *"An update from our store"*.</Tip>
<Tip>**Use the preview text.** It's free space next to the subject in most inboxes.</Tip>
<Tip>**Always send a test** after changing shortcodes. Some values, like `{{ next_tier_name }}`, are empty for customers already in the top tier.</Tip>
<Tip>**Point the CTA to a page that helps.** Your rewards page or a collection works better than the homepage.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I send the test email to a colleague?">
    No. **Send Test Email** always goes to the email address on your Yuko account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I save my CTA URL?">
    The CTA URL must be a page on your store. The editor shows your store's address above the field, so enter only the path, such as `pages/rewards`. Links to other sites aren't allowed. If you don't want a button, untick **Use a CTA Button**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the CTA URL field greyed out?">
    Your store isn't connected yet, so Yuko doesn't know which domain to use. The field shows *"Connect your store to enable CTA links."*
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I translate an email into several languages?">
    Each email has one version. Write it in the language most of your customers use.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where does the logo come from?">
    From **Account → General → Logo** (*"Display logo in emails and other communications"*). In the Advanced Editor you can also place it with the **Logo** block.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the reminder timing for expiry, birthday and anniversary emails?">
    Not from the email editor. These reminders use a fixed number of days before the date. See [Loyalty Emails](/emails/loyalty-emails).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
