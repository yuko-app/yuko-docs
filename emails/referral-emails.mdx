<Note>
**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Referral → Emails
</Note>

Your [referral program](/referrals/overview) has three built-in emails. They start **inactive**. Switch them on in **Referral → Emails** and edit them in the [Template Editor](/emails/template-editor).

| Email | Sent to | Sent when | Default subject |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Referrer welcome](#referrer-welcome) | The referrer | They join the referral program | Welcome to our referral program |
| [Friend reward unlocked](#friend-reward-unlocked) | The referred friend | They claim their friend reward | Your referral discount is ready |
| [Referrer reward issued](#referrer-reward-issued) | The referrer | Their reward is issued for a successful referral | Your referral advocate reward is ready |

Each email has a delay setting (**Days after Referrer welcome**, **Days after Friend reward unlocked**, **Days after Referrer reward issued**). Leave it at **0** to send immediately.

## Referrer welcome

**Sent when:** a customer joins your referral program as a referrer.

**Default subject:** *Welcome to our referral program*

**Default message:** Greeting → thanks for joining → *"Here is your personal referral link: `{{ referral_link }}`"* → share the link, and when friends complete their first purchase you may earn rewards.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ first_name }}`, `{{ referral_link }}`, `{{ referral_code }}`, `{{ advocate_name }}`, `{{ expiry }}`.

<Tip>**Make the link the hero.** This email is what gets referrers sharing, so put `{{ referral_link }}` in a button, not just the body text.</Tip>

---

## Friend reward unlocked

**Sent when:** a referred friend claims their friend reward, for example through the [Friend Welcome Popup](/referrals/widgets/friend-welcome-popup). It goes to the **friend**.

**Default subject:** *Your referral discount is ready*

**Default message:** Greeting → *"You've just unlocked a special discount through a referral. Here is your discount code: `{{ discount_code }}`"* → enter it at checkout.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ discount_code }}`, `{{ reward_value }}`, `{{ reward_type }}`, `{{ expires_at }}`, `{{ referred_by }}`, `{{ referral_code_used }}`.

<Tip>**Mention who sent them.** `{{ referred_by }}` makes the email feel personal and builds trust with a first-time shopper.</Tip>

---

## Referrer reward issued

**Sent when:** a referrer earns their reward because a friend they referred completed a qualifying order. It goes to the **referrer**.

**Default subject:** *Your referral advocate reward is ready*

**Default message:** Greeting → *"You've just unlocked a special reward through a referral. Here is your reward code/ value: `{{ discount_code }}`"*.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ discount_code }}`, `{{ reward_value }}`, `{{ reward_type }}`, `{{ friend_first_name }}`, `{{ total_referrals }}`, `{{ points_balance }}`.

<Tip>**Show their progress.** `{{ total_referrals }}` gives your best referrers a reason to keep sharing.</Tip>

## Best practices

<Tip>**Turn on all three together.** They cover both sides of the referral loop: the referrer gets their link, the friend gets their code, and the referrer hears when it paid off.</Tip>
<Tip>**Test with a real referral.** Join as a referrer, open your link in a private window, claim the friend reward and place a test order. This checks all three emails.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I send a milestone email, for example after 5 referrals?">
    Not as a separate email. Workflow conditions can't filter on referral count, so the simplest option is to mention the count in Referrer reward issued with `{{ total_referrals }}`. For other follow-ups, build a [workflow](/workflows/overview) on the **Referrer Rewarded** trigger.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there an email when a referral is completed?">
    There's no built-in one, but you can build a workflow on the **Referral Completed** trigger.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
