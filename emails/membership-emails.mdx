<Note>
**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Membership → Emails
</Note>

Paid [memberships](/memberships/overview) have four built-in emails, one for each change to a member's subscription. They start **inactive**. Switch them on in **Membership → Emails** and edit them in the [Template Editor](/emails/template-editor).

| Email | Sent when | Default subject | Delay setting |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Membership created](#membership-created) | A customer subscribes to a membership for the first time | Welcome to `{{ membership_name }}`, `{{ first_name }}`! | **Days after membership created** |
| [Membership upgraded](#membership-upgraded) | A member switches to a higher plan | You've upgraded to `{{ membership_name }}`, `{{ first_name }}`! | **Days after membership upgraded** |
| [Membership downgraded](#membership-downgraded) | A member switches to a lower plan | Your membership plan has changed, `{{ first_name }}` | **Days after membership downgraded** |
| [Membership cancelled](#membership-cancelled) | A member's subscription is cancelled | Your `{{ membership_name }}` membership has been cancelled, `{{ first_name }}` | **Days after membership cancelled** |

Leave the delay at **0** to send immediately.

## Membership created

**Sent when:** a new subscription contract is created, meaning the customer has just bought a membership plan.

**Default subject:** *Welcome to `{{ membership_name }}`, `{{ first_name }}`!*

**Default title:** *Your membership is now active*

**Default message:** Welcome → the `{{ membership_name }}` membership is active → overview of the perks → visit your account to explore them.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ membership_name }}`, `{{ membership_benefits }}`, `{{ benefit_1_name }}`, `{{ membership_price }}`, `{{ billing_interval }}`, `{{ next_billing_date }}`.

<Tip>**Lead with the benefits, not the price.** The member has just paid, so remind them what they get.</Tip>

---

## Membership upgraded

**Sent when:** a member switches to a plan that comes later in your plan order on the [Plans](/memberships/plans) tab. Yuko uses that order, not the price, to decide what counts as an upgrade.

**Default subject:** *You've upgraded to `{{ membership_name }}`, `{{ first_name }}`!*

**Default title:** *Your membership upgrade is active*

**Default message:** The membership is now `{{ membership_name }}` → the new benefits are live today.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ membership_name }}`, `{{ previous_price }}`, `{{ membership_price }}`, `{{ membership_benefits }}`.

---

## Membership downgraded

**Sent when:** a member switches to a plan that comes earlier in your plan order on the [Plans](/memberships/plans) tab.

**Default subject:** *Your membership plan has changed, `{{ first_name }}`*

**Default title:** *Your membership plan has been updated*

**Default message:** The membership has changed to `{{ membership_name }}` → the member keeps that plan's benefits → they can change plan from their account.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ membership_name }}`, `{{ previous_price }}`, `{{ benefits_lost }}`, `{{ benefits_retained }}`.

<Tip>**Keep the tone neutral.** Spell out what they keep (`{{ benefits_retained }}`) before what they lose.</Tip>

---

## Membership cancelled

**Sent when:** a member's subscription contract is cancelled.

**Default subject:** *Your `{{ membership_name }}` membership has been cancelled, `{{ first_name }}`*

**Default title:** *Your membership has been cancelled*

**Default message:** Confirms the cancellation → benefits are no longer active → thanks them, and they're welcome back anytime.

**Useful shortcodes:** `{{ membership_name }}`, `{{ cancellation_reason }}`, `{{ member_since }}`, `{{ subscription_status }}`.

<Tip>**Leave the door open.** Add a CTA URL to your membership page so customers can rejoin in one click.</Tip>

## Best practices

<Tip>**Use the benefit slots for a clean list.** `{{ benefit_1_name }}` to `{{ benefit_10_name }}` (and the matching `_description` shortcodes) let you lay out benefits exactly how you want. Empty slots print nothing.</Tip>
<Tip>**Turn on Membership created first.** It's the email every new member gets.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there an email when a membership is paused or reactivated?">
    There's no built-in email for those. Build a [workflow](/workflows/overview) on the **Membership Paused** or **Membership Reactivated** trigger.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do benefit names come from?">
    From the benefits on the membership [plan](/memberships/plans). Each benefit fills one numbered slot.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
