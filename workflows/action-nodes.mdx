## Introduction

Action nodes are the steps that actually do something when a customer reaches them. The **Add Node** dialog lists these actions:

| Node | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Send Email](#send-email) | *"An email will be sent to the contact when they reach this step."* |
| [Coupon](#coupon) | *"Generate a unique coupon code for the contact at this step."* |
| [Points Credit](#points-credit) | *"Credit loyalty points to the contact when they reach this step."* |
| [Points Debit](#points-debit) | *"Deduct loyalty points from the contact when they reach this step."* |
| [WhatsApp](/workflows/whatsapp-node) | Send a WhatsApp template through a connected provider |
| [Engati](/workflows/partner-nodes) / [kwikEngage](/workflows/partner-nodes) | Send a template message through Engati or Kwik Engage |

Timing and logic nodes (**Time Delay**, **If / Else**) are covered in [Time Delay & Exit](/workflows/wait-and-exit) and [Triggers & Conditions](/workflows/triggers-and-conditions).

Click any node on the canvas to open its settings panel. Save the panel to apply your changes.

## Send Email

Sends an email to the customer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the node">
    Pick **Send Email** in **Add Node**. A new email node starts with the subject *New Email*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Click the node. The panel shows a preview of the email (switch between **Desktop view** and **Mobile view**). Click **Edit** to open the [Template Editor](/emails/template-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the email">
    Set the subject, preview text, title, message, CTA and footer. Use [shortcodes](/emails/shortcodes). The **Available Template Variables** card lists the shortcodes for this workflow's trigger, plus the Redemption group. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- The node card shows **Emails sent** so far.
- Emails use your sender settings from [Email Settings](/emails/sender-and-branding).
- The [Advanced Editor](/emails/template-editor#advanced-editor) is available on select plans.

## Coupon

Creates a unique Shopify discount code for the customer at this step. Show it in a later Send Email node with the **Coupon** block in the Advanced Editor.

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| **Coupon identifier** | A random identifier for this node, with a copy button (*"Unique identifier for this coupon node."*). Email coupon blocks use it to find this coupon. |
| **Discount Type** | **Percentage (%)** (default, 10), **Amount** or **Free Shipping** |
| **Value** | The percentage or amount (not shown for Free Shipping) |
| **Set validity** | Toggle on and enter **Valid upto (in days)** to make the code expire |
| **Minimum purchase requirements** | **No minimum requirements**, **Minimum purchase amount** or **Minimum quantity of items** |
| **Product Restrictions** | **Applies to**: **All products**, **Specific collections** or **Specific products**. For Amount discounts, **Only apply discount once per order**. |
| **Shipping Restrictions** (Free Shipping) | **All countries** or **Selected countries**, and **Exclude shipping rates over a certain amount** with a **Maximum shipping rate** |
| **Combinations** | Whether the code combines with **Product discounts**, **Order discounts** and **Shipping discounts** |

<Tip>**Pair Coupon with Send Email.** Put the Coupon node first, then add the **Coupon** block in the email and pick this node's identifier, so the customer sees their code.</Tip>

## Points Credit

Adds points to the customer's balance. Available on Shopify stores.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Method** | **Fixed amount of points**: the same number of points for everyone. **Incremental points**: points based on the order amount. |
| **Points to earn** | Fixed: the number of points to credit. Incremental: the points earned *for every* **Amount spent**. |
| **Amount spent** | Incremental only. For example, 1 point for every 10 spent gives 25 points on an order with a subtotal of 250. |
| **Credited reason** | *"Store why these points were credited for support and audit logs."* Shown in the customer's points history. |
| **Enable points expiry for this credit** | Expire these points after **Expire after** N **Days**, **Weeks**, **Months** or **Years**. |
| **Enable on-hold period before crediting points** | Hold the points as pending for **Hold points for** N **Days**, **Weeks**, **Months** or **Years** before they become available. |

<Note>**Incremental points** use the order subtotal, so they need an order-based trigger such as **Order Paid** or **Order Delivered**. On a trigger with no order, the step fails with *"Order not found"* and the customer's run stops there.</Note>

Points credited by a workflow count as earned points. Without an on-hold period, they fire the **Earn Points** event right away, which also sends the built-in **Points earned** email if it's active.

## Points Debit

Removes points from the customer's balance. Available on Shopify stores.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Points to debit** | The number of points to remove |
| **Debited reason** | *"Store why this debit happened for support and audit logs."* |

If the customer doesn't have enough points, the step fails with *"Insufficient point balance"* and nothing is deducted. The customer doesn't continue to the next steps, and their subscriber status becomes **Failed**. You can see the reason in [Analytics](/workflows/analytics).

<Tip>To avoid failed runs, put an **If / Else** with **Points Balance** *is at least* your debit amount before the Points Debit node (available on loyalty, VIP and booster triggers).</Tip>

## Examples

**1. Welcome bonus.** Trigger **Joined Loyalty Program** → Points Credit (Fixed, 100, reason *Welcome bonus*) → Send Email.

**2. Downgrade win-back.** Trigger **VIP Tier Downgrade** → Coupon (Percentage, 15, valid 14 days) → Send Email with the Coupon block.

**3. Extra points on delivered orders.** Trigger **Order Delivered** → Points Credit (Incremental, 1 point for every 20 spent, hold for 14 days).

## Best practices

<Tip>**Always fill in the reason.** Support teams rely on **Credited reason** and **Debited reason** to explain balance changes.</Tip>
<Tip>**Use the on-hold period for order-based credits.** It gives returns time to happen before the points can be spent.</Tip>
<Tip>**Give coupons a validity.** An expiry date creates urgency and keeps old codes from piling up.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a Coupon node give a free product?">
    No. The Coupon node supports Percentage, Amount and Free Shipping discounts. Free-product rewards are available as a [way to redeem](/loyalty/ways-to-redeem/free-product).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Points Credit apply VIP tier multipliers?">
    The node credits exactly the points you set (or the incremental calculation).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will a credit in my workflow re-trigger the same workflow?">
    A credit fires **Earn Points**. If your workflow's trigger is also Earn Points, use **No re-entry** or a **Has Not in This Flow** profile filter so customers don't loop through it again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
