## What we'll build

A simple win-back workflow:

1. **Trigger** — customer hasn't placed an order in 60 days.
2. **Condition** — they have a points balance > 0.
3. **Wait** — 1 day.
4. **Action** — send a *“We miss you”* email.
5. **Exit**.

## Walkthrough

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new workflow">
    In Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Workflows** and click **Create workflow**. Give it a name like *“60-day win-back”*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the trigger">
    Click the placeholder **Trigger** node. Pick **Customer inactive for X days** and set X to 60.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a condition">
    Click the **+** below the trigger and pick **Condition (binary)**. Set the rule to **Customer has points balance > 0**. The node now has two branches — *Yes* and *No*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a wait">
    On the *Yes* branch, click **+** and pick **Wait**. Set duration to 1 day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an action">
    After the wait, click **+** and pick **Send Email**. Choose a template (or create a new one in Email Notifications first).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an exit">
    Click **+** at the end and pick **Exit**. Both the *Yes* and *No* branches need an exit — add one to the *No* branch too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Click **Activate** at the top right. Yuko starts evaluating the trigger from this point forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a test customer">
    Pick a customer with a points balance and last-order date > 60 days ago.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Analytics">
    Open the workflow's **[Analytics](/workflows/analytics)** tab. After the trigger fires (typically within an hour), the customer should appear as an entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the email lands">
    A day later, check the test customer's inbox. The email should arrive.
  </Step>
</Steps>
