## Introduction

In this tutorial you'll build a **VIP upgrade bonus** workflow:

```
VIP Tier Upgrade  ──→  Points Credit (200 points)  ──→  Send Email  ──→  Exit
```

When a customer moves up to a higher [VIP tier](/vip-tiers/overview), they get 200 bonus points and an email congratulating them on the new tier. The built-in [Tier unlocked](/emails/loyalty-emails#tier-unlocked) email only covers a customer's first tier, so this workflow fills the gap for upgrades.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the workflow">
    Go to **Workflows** and click **Create Workflow** → **Create from Scratch**. The new workflow opens on the canvas as a **Draft**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Click the title at the top and rename it, for example *VIP upgrade bonus*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the trigger">
    Click the **Trigger** node. In the trigger list (grouped by **Loyalty**, **VIP Tiers**, **Referral**, **Membership** and **Orders**), search for and select **VIP Tier Upgrade**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set re-entry">
    In the trigger panel under **Re-entry criteria**, choose **Allow re-entry**, so a customer who upgrades again later (Silver → Gold → Platinum) gets the bonus each time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: limit it to one tier">
    Under **Trigger filters**, click **Add**, choose the **Current Tier Name** field and set it to, for example, *Gold*. Only upgrades into Gold will enter the workflow. Skip this step to reward every upgrade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Points Credit node">
    Click the **+** on the line below the trigger. In **Add Node**, pick **Points Credit**. Set **Method** to **Fixed amount of points**, **Points to earn** to *200* and **Credited reason** to *VIP upgrade bonus*. Save the node.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Send Email node">
    Click the **+** below Points Credit and pick **Send Email**. In the node panel, click **Edit** to open the [Template Editor](/emails/template-editor). Write a subject such as *Welcome to `{{ current_tier_name }}`, `{{ first_name }}`!* and a message that mentions the bonus and the new balance with `{{ points_balance }}`. Click **Save**, then go back to the workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Exit">
    Every path already ends in an **Exit** node (*"The automation will stop here."*). There's nothing to configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send yourself a test">
    In the Template Editor, click **Send Test Email** to see the email in your inbox with sample data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go Live">
    Changes save automatically (you'll see **Saved** at the top). Click **Turn on workflow**. The status changes from **Draft** to **Live**. Running workflows Live is available on select plans.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. A customer's tier is re-evaluated and they move to a higher tier. Yuko fires **VIP Tier Upgrade**.
2. If the customer passes your trigger filters and re-entry rule, they become a **subscriber** of the workflow.
3. **Points Credit** adds 200 points with the reason *VIP upgrade bonus*. You'll see it in the customer's activity on the [Customers](/loyalty/customers) page.
4. **Send Email** sends your email. Shortcodes like `{{ current_tier_name }}` are filled in for this customer.
5. The subscriber reaches **Exit** and is marked **Completed** in [Analytics](/workflows/analytics).

## Variations

- **Wait before emailing:** add a [Time Delay](/workflows/wait-and-exit) of 1 Day(s) before Send Email, with **Set a delay until a specific time of day** at 10:00.
- **WhatsApp instead of email:** swap Send Email for the [WhatsApp node](/workflows/whatsapp-node).
- **Different bonus per tier:** add an **If / Else** node after the trigger with **Current Tier Name** equals *Platinum*. Give the **Yes** path a bigger Points Credit.
- **Downgrade win-back:** build a second workflow on **VIP Tier Downgrade** with a [Coupon](/workflows/action-nodes#coupon) and a Send Email.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Use a clear Credited reason.** It appears in the customer's points history, so support can see where the points came from.</Tip>
<Tip>**Mind the order.** Put Points Credit before Send Email so `{{ points_balance }}` already includes the bonus.</Tip>
<Tip>**Watch the first few subscribers.** Open **Analytics** after going Live and check that subscribers reach **Completed**.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the trigger after choosing it?">
    Pick the trigger carefully. Once a trigger is set, the trigger panel shows its filters and re-entry settings rather than the trigger list. To use a different trigger, create a new workflow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I click Turn on workflow?">
    Running workflows Live is available on select plans. You can still build and save workflows as Draft.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will the bonus points trigger my Points earned email?">
    Yes. Points credited by a workflow count as earned points, so the built-in **Points earned** email (if active) also sends. Turn it off, or rely on it instead of a separate Send Email node.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
