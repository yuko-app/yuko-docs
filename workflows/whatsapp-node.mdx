## Introduction

The WhatsApp node sends one WhatsApp template message to the customer when the workflow reaches it. It uses a WhatsApp provider you've connected under **Integrations**, and fills the template's parameters with [shortcodes](/emails/shortcodes) such as `{{first_name}}` or `{{points_balance}}`.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the node">
    Click the **+** where the message should go and choose **WhatsApp**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a provider">
    The panel shows every provider as a card with a **Connected** or **Disconnected** badge. Disconnected providers can't be selected. Click the provider you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the template details">
    Enter the fields your provider needs (see the table below), for example **Template Name** and **Language Code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map the parameters">
    Set the parameter count to match your template, then fill each parameter in order. Type plain text or insert a shortcode, for example `{{first_name}}` for the first variable and `{{points_balance}}` for the second.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the node. If a field is missing, the panel shows what to fix.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Shortcodes

The shortcode picker in each parameter field lists the shortcodes for your workflow's **trigger**, the same set a Send Email node gets, plus `{{redemption_link}}`. For example, a **VIP Tier Upgrade** workflow offers `{{current_tier_name}}`, while a **Redeem Points** workflow offers `{{reward_code}}`. See the [Shortcodes Reference](/emails/shortcodes) for what each one means.

## How it works

1. A customer reaches the WhatsApp node.
2. Yuko uses the phone number on the customer's record. If there's no phone number, the step is marked **failed** with *"Customer phone number not found"* and the workflow moves on.
3. Yuko fills the template parameters with the customer's data and sends the template through the provider's API.
4. The step is marked **completed**, or **failed** with the provider's error. Either way, the workflow continues to the next node.

You can see each step's result and failure reason in [Analytics](/workflows/analytics).

### Limitations

- One template per node. To send a second message, add another WhatsApp node, ideally after a Time Delay.
- Templates must be created and approved in your provider's dashboard. Yuko sends them but doesn't edit their wording.
- Delivery, read and reply status stay in your provider's dashboard. Yuko tracks completed or failed.
- The provider must be included in your plan. Otherwise the step fails with a message asking you to upgrade.

## Examples

**1. Tier upgrade celebration.** **VIP Tier Upgrade** → WhatsApp: *"Welcome to `{{current_tier_name}}`, `{{first_name}}`! Your balance is `{{points_balance}}` points."*

**2. Points expiry nudge.** **Points Expiring Reminder** → WhatsApp: *"`{{first_name}}`, `{{expiring_points}}` points expire in `{{days_until_expiry}}` days."*

**3. One-time campaign announcement.** **One-time Campaign Points Awarded** with a **Campaign Identifier** filter → WhatsApp: *"We've added bonus points to your account. Redeem here: `{{redemption_link}}`"*.

**4. Friend reward.** **Friend reward unlocked** → WhatsApp with `{{discount_code}}`.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Get templates approved first.** Unapproved templates fail at send time.</Tip>
<Tip>**Back up with email.** WhatsApp fails when there's no phone number. Use an **If / Else** on **Phone number** to send an email to customers without one.</Tip>
<Tip>**Space out messages.** Put a **Time Delay** between an email and a WhatsApp so they don't arrive in the same minute.</Tip>
<Tip>**Test with your own customer record.** Make sure it has a phone number, and trigger the event yourself before going Live.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is a provider greyed out?">
    It shows **Disconnected**. Connect it under **Integrations** (see the provider's guide above), then reopen the node.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if the customer has no phone number?">
    The step fails with *"Customer phone number not found"* and the workflow continues to the next node.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the message wording here?">
    No. The wording is fixed in the approved template in your provider's dashboard. Yuko only fills the parameters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I use this node or the Engati / kwikEngage nodes?">
    Use the WhatsApp node. It supports Engati and Kwik Engage as providers, plus 11 others. See [Partner Nodes](/workflows/partner-nodes).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
