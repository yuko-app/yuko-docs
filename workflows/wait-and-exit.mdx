**Where to find this:** Inside any workflow, click the **+** on a connecting line → **Add Node** → **Timing** → **Time Delay**
</Note>

## Time Delay

A **Time Delay** node holds each customer for a set time before they move on to the next step. In the picker it's described as: *"The workflow will wait for this duration before moving to the next step."*

### Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Duration** | How long to wait. Must be at least 1. |
| **Unit** | **Minute(s)**, **Hour(s)** or **Day(s)** |
| **Set a delay until a specific time of day** | After the duration, wait until a set **Time** in a chosen **Time Zone**. Only available with **Day(s)**. |
| **Set a delay until chosen day(s) of the week** | After the duration, wait until one of the weekdays you select. Pick at least one day. Only available with **Day(s)**. |

### Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the node">
    Click the **+** where you want the pause, then choose **Time Delay**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the duration">
    Enter a **Duration** and pick the **Unit**, for example *3* **Day(s)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: pick a send window">
    With **Day(s)** selected, turn on **Set a delay until a specific time of day** and choose the **Time** and **Time Zone**. You can also turn on **Set a delay until chosen day(s) of the week** and select the days.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the panel. The node on the canvas shows the delay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### When to use

- **Space out messages:** Send Email → Time Delay 3 Day(s) → WhatsApp.
- **Give a coupon time to work:** Coupon → Send Email → Time Delay 5 Day(s) → reminder email.
- **Send at a friendly hour:** Time Delay 1 Day(s) until 10:00 in your store's time zone.

## Exit

Every path in a workflow ends at an **Exit** node: *"The automation will stop here."* Yuko adds Exit nodes for you, including one at the end of each **If / Else** branch. There's nothing to configure.

When a customer reaches Exit, their subscriber status in [Analytics](/workflows/analytics) becomes **Completed**.

## How it works

1. A customer reaches the Time Delay node and their status stays **In Progress**.
2. Yuko waits for the duration, then, if set, until the next matching time of day or weekday.
3. The customer moves to the next node.
4. At the end of the path they reach Exit and are marked **Completed**.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Avoid back-to-back messages.** Put a Time Delay between two sends on different channels, so customers don't get an email and a WhatsApp in the same minute.</Tip>
<Tip>**Use a time of day for multi-day delays.** "1 Day(s) until 10:00" lands in inboxes in the morning instead of whenever the trigger happened.</Tip>
<Tip>**Remember re-entry.** If re-entry is allowed, a customer can be in the workflow more than once at the same time, each waiting separately.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I wait in weeks or until a specific date?">
    No. Use **Day(s)** (for example 14 days for two weeks). There's no option to wait until a calendar date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I turn on the time-of-day option?">
    **Set a delay until a specific time of day** and the weekday option only work when the unit is **Day(s)**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add an exit reason?">
    No. Exit has no settings. It simply ends the path.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I check a condition after the wait?">
    Yes. Add an **If / Else** node after the Time Delay. See [Triggers & Conditions](/workflows/triggers-and-conditions).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
