---
title: "List booster campaigns"
---

Retrieves a paginated list of your organisation's **active** booster campaigns. Results are sorted by `created_at` in descending order. Draft, completed, cancelled and disabled campaigns are not returned. The list is filtered by status only, not by date, so check `starts_at`, `ends_at` and `schedule` to see whether a campaign is running right now.

**GET /public/booster-campaigns**

#### **Authorizations**

**Authorization**

**string**

**header**

**required**

Bearer authentication header of the form `Bearer <token>`, where `<token>` is your REST API token.

#### **Required Scope**

**`read:booster_campaigns`**

This endpoint requires the `booster_campaigns` scope with **Read Access** (or **Read/Write Access**). See [Scopes](/developer/api/scopes).

#### **Query Parameters**

**per_page**

**integer**

**default: 20**

The number of results per page. Values above 100 are capped at 100.

**page**

**integer**

**default: 1**

The page number to retrieve.

#### **Response**

**200 - application/json**

**data**

**object[]**

Hide child attributes

**data.uuid**

**string\<uuid\>**

Unique UUID identifier for the booster campaign.

Example:

`"5b1f2c9e-7d3a-4e8b-9c11-2f6a8d4e0b73"`

**data.name**

**string**

The campaign name.

Example:

`"Double points weekend"`

**data.description**

**string | null**

The campaign description.

**data.type**

**enum\<string\>**

The booster campaign type.

Available options:

`multiplier`,

`bonus_flat`,

`spending_goal`,

`order_frequency`,

`streak`,

`multi_action`,

`dormant_reactivation`,

`first_purchase_boost`,

`product_collection_multiplier`

Example:

`"multiplier"`

**data.status**

**string**

The campaign status. Always `active` for this endpoint.

Example:

`"active"`

**data.stackable**

**boolean**

Whether the campaign can stack with other booster campaigns.

Example:

`false`

**data.schedule**

**object | null**

When the campaign runs, for example `type` (`evergreen`, `one_time` or `recurring`), recurrence settings, `days_of_week`, and `active_hours_start` / `active_hours_end`.

**data.targeting**

**object | null**

Targeting settings, such as the `products` and `collections` the campaign applies to.

**data.product_scope**

**object | null**

The product scope the campaign applies to.

**data.display**

**object**

Storefront display settings, such as `show_in_widget` and `labels`. Always includes an `icon` object (or `null`) with `source`, `slug`, `id` and `url`, the same shape as a reward's icon.

**data.config**

**object | null**

Type-specific settings. Structure varies by `type`, for example `milestones` for spending goals and order frequency, or `actions` and `completion_logic` for multi-action challenges.

**data.starts_at**

**string\<date-time\> | null**

When the campaign starts.

Example:

`"2026-10-10T00:00:00+00:00"`

**data.ends_at**

**string\<date-time\> | null**

When the campaign ends, or `null` if it has no end date.

Example:

`"2026-10-12T23:59:59+00:00"`

**data.created_at**

**string\<date-time\> | null**

Example:

`"2026-10-01T09:00:00+00:00"`

**meta**

**object**

Hide child attributes

**meta.pagination**

**object**

Hide child attributes

**meta.pagination.current_page**

**integer**

Example:

`1`

**meta.pagination.per_page**

**integer**

Example:

`20`

**meta.pagination.total**

**integer**

Example:

`1`

**meta.pagination.last_page**

**integer**

Example:

`1`

**meta.pagination.has_more**

**boolean**

Example:

`false`
