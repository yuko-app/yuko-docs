<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Checkout Editor">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Settings → Checkout** and click **Customize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Yuko app block">
    Click **Add app block** in the section where you want the widget and choose the Yuko block for this widget (for example **Yuko Potential points earn** or **Yuko Redeem rewards**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Position and save">
    Drag the block to its final position in the checkout and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Checkout app blocks are only available on **Shopify Plus**. In Yuko, the Checkout Process widgets show "Available only on Shopify Plus plan" on other Shopify plans. See [Theme compatibility & requirements](/getting-started/theme-compatibility).
</Info>
