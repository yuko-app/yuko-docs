## What is the Yuko Loyalty Program

Your loyalty program is the centre of Yuko. It defines:

- **How customers earn** — [Ways to Earn](/loyalty/ways-to-earn/overview) like *Place an order*, *Sign Up*, *Birthday* or *Subscribe to SMS*.
- **How customers redeem** — [Ways to Redeem](/loyalty/ways-to-redeem/overview) like *Amount Discount*, *Free Product* or *Store Credit*, online and at Shopify POS. In-store redemption is covered in the [Shopify POS](/integrations/shopify-pos) section.
- **When to give extra** — [Bonus Campaigns](/loyalty/booster-campaigns/overview) and [One-time Campaigns](/loyalty/one-time-campaigns).
- **The program rules** — [Loyalty Settings](/loyalty/program-settings): point labels, which order statuses earn and revert points (including returns), and excluded customers.
- **What customers see** — loyalty [widgets](/onsite-widgets/overview) and [emails](/emails/loyalty-emails).

## Where things are

The **Loyalty** section of the Yuko app has four tabs:

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Programs** | **Loyalty Program Status** (turn the program on or off), **Quick Start**, **Setup Overview**, **Ways to earn**, **Ways to redeem**, **Bonus Campaigns** and **One-time Campaigns** |
| **Widgets** | The storefront loyalty widgets and branding |
| **Emails** | Loyalty email templates |
| **Settings** | Program-wide settings, Backfill points for past orders, and Reset customer activities |

[Customers](/loyalty/customers), [VIP Tiers](/vip-tiers/overview) (**VIP**) and [Analytics](/analytics/reports) have their own items in the Yuko sidebar, next to **Loyalty**.

**Setup Overview** appears once **Place an order** is active. It holds your **Points to cash conversion rate**, the value of 100 points in your store currency. The default is 1, shown as *100 points = $1* in your currency. Click **Edit** to change it. Setup Overview also shows:

- **Reward value rate** — how much value customers get back for what they spend, usually 1–10%.
- **Cost per point** — what each point costs you in your store currency.

Yuko uses the conversion rate to show customers what their balance is worth in notifications and emails. See [Points to cash conversion rate](/loyalty/program-settings#points-to-cash-conversion-rate).

## Who it's for

- Stores that want to drive **repeat purchases**.
- Brands that want to **own their customer relationship** instead of renting it from ad channels.
- Teams ready to reward loyalty with points instead of relying only on blanket discounts.

## How it fits together

```
  Customer action  ──→  Way to Earn  ──→  Points credited (× VIP multiplier)
                                                       │
                                                       ▼
  Customer redeems  ←──  Way to Redeem  ←──  Points balance
                                 │
                                 ▼
             Discount / free product / free shipping / store credit
```

Every page in this section documents one piece of that loop.

## Turning the program on or off

Use the **Loyalty Program Status** toggle at the top of the **Programs** tab. Disabling the program turns off all loyalty widgets and stops all loyalty activity until you enable it again.

## Get started in 15 minutes

Follow the [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart) to launch a working program: install Yuko, set up one way to earn and one reward, add the widgets to your theme and test with a real order.
