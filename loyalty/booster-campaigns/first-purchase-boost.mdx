**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs → Booster Campaigns → Create campaign → **First Purchase Boost**
</Note>

## Overview

A First Purchase Boost issues an extra reward when a customer places their first order, on top of the normal **Place an order** points. The reward can be bonus points, a discount for the next order, free shipping, a free product or store credit.

There are no type-specific settings: you choose the reward, and optionally who qualifies.

## Why you need it?

The first order is the most fragile moment in the relationship; the customer is still deciding whether your brand is worth a second visit.

A standard program rewards the first order like any other. A First Purchase Boost makes the program *immediately* tangible: *"they already gave me 500 points and I haven't even unboxed yet."* That tangibility is what drives the second order.

## When to use it?

This is another booster worth running all the time.

- First-to-second order conversion is your key retention metric.
- You're launching your loyalty program and want new customers to see the value right away.
- You run paid memberships and want joining to feel worth it on day one.

## Before you start

<Check>The **Place an order** Way to Earn is enabled. If it's off, this type is greyed out in the **Create Booster Campaign** picker.</Check>

## How to configure it?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Booster Campaigns">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In the **Booster Campaigns** section, click **Create campaign** and select **First Purchase Boost**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Campaign Details">
    Enter a **Campaign Name** (internal) and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the reward">
    Click **Add Reward** and choose **Amount discount**, **% off discount**, **Free shipping**, **Free product**, **Bonus points** or **Store credit** (Shopify only). You can add more than one reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set targeting (optional)">
    Add **Targeting Rules**, for example **Customer Tags** or **Membership**, or **Products** / **Collections** rules to require the first order to contain certain items. Set **Max participants** if you want to cap the campaign. Each customer can only complete it once, so there is no **Max completions per customer** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule">
    **Evergreen (always on)** is the usual choice. Use **One-time** for a launch period (for example, a bigger first-order reward during your program launch month).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review display, stacking, publishing and icon">
    The **Body text** widget label defaults to *"Place your first order to unlock [reward]."* Tokens: `[title]`, `[reward]`, `[points_label]`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Status** is enabled by default. Click **Save**. The boost applies to first orders placed from now on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. A targeted customer places an order while the campaign is active.
2. Yuko checks it is the customer's first order (their only order that hasn't been refunded).
3. If so, the reward is issued and the customer is marked **Completed** in the **Participants** tab.
4. The boost fires only once per customer.

## Use cases / Examples

**1. Flat onboarding bonus.** +500 **Bonus points** on the first order. The customer knows the exact reward before checking out.

**2. Second-order hook.** An **Amount discount** ($10 off) issued on the first order, to use on the second. It gives the customer a concrete reason to come back.

**3. Member welcome.** A **Membership** rule with **Free shipping** as the reward, so paid members feel the benefit straight away.

**4. Category starter.** A **Collections** rule so only first orders containing your starter-kit collection qualify.

## How to promote it

The boost rewards a specific moment, so non-customers need to see it.

- **Storefront:** with **Show in loyalty widget** checked, the **[Loyalty Nudges](/onsite-widgets/loyalty-nudges)** widget, Floating Launcher and customer account page show the offer to signed-in customers.
- **Email:** mention the first-order reward in your welcome series. Yuko syncs loyalty data to **[Klaviyo](/integrations/klaviyo)** so you can target customers who signed up but haven't ordered.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can the boost multiply the first order's points?">
    No. Multiplier rewards are only available for Multiplier and Product & Collection Multiplier campaigns. Use **Bonus points** for a fixed extra amount.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What counts as a first order?">
    The customer's first order in your store that hasn't been refunded. Customers who already ordered before the campaign started don't qualify.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I target customers by signup source?">
    There's no signup-source rule. Use **Customer Tags** if your signup flow tags customers.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
