## Overview

An Order Booster counts the orders a customer places while the campaign runs and rewards them at each milestone, for example **3 orders → 500 bonus points, 5 orders → free shipping**. Set up to 20 milestones (each up to 100 orders), and give each milestone one or more rewards of any type.

<Info>Order Booster was previously called *Order Frequency*.</Info>

## Why you need it?

Lifetime value is basket size times frequency. Multipliers and spending goals push basket size; an Order Booster closes the loop on frequency.

It also builds habit. A customer who places three orders in a month has a buying rhythm with your brand, and is far more likely to keep it after the campaign ends.

## When to use it?

- You want to drive purchase frequency over a defined window.
- You sell repeat-purchase categories (consumables, fashion drops, replenishment).
- You want a "virtual subscription" nudge without a subscription product.
- You're running a holiday campaign and want to capture multi-order gift shopping.

## Before you start

<Check>The **Place an order** Way to Earn is enabled. If it's off, this type is greyed out in the **Create Booster Campaign** picker.</Check>

## How to configure it?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Booster Campaigns">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In the **Booster Campaigns** section, click **Create campaign** and select **Order Booster**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Campaign Details">
    Enter a **Campaign Name** (internal) and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add milestones">
    Under **Milestones**, enter the **Number of Orders** for **Milestone 1** (the target number of orders to unlock it, 1–100). Click **Add milestone** for more, up to 20. Each milestone must be higher than the one before.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add rewards to each milestone">
    In each milestone, click **Add reward** and choose **Amount discount**, **% off discount**, **Free shipping**, **Free product**, **Bonus points** or **Store credit** (Shopify only). Every milestone needs at least one reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a minimum order amount (optional)">
    Under **Order Booster Goal**, check **Set a minimum order amount** and enter the **Minimum Order Amount**. Only orders above this amount count toward each milestone, so the campaign can't be gamed with tiny orders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set targeting and limits (optional)">
    Add **Targeting Rules**, including **Products** or **Collections** rules. Set **Max participants** and **Max completions per customer** if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule">
    The schedule is the window in which orders count: **One-time**, **Recurring** (by weeks or months, with progress tracked per cycle) or **Evergreen (always on)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review display, stacking, publishing and icon">
    Two widget labels apply: **Call-to-action above progress bar** (default *"Place [remaining_orders] more order(s) to unlock [next_reward]"*) and **Status text under progress bar** (default *"Order [current_orders] of [total_orders]"*). Other tokens: `[title]`, `[reward]`, `[points_label]`, `[milestones_done]`, `[milestones_total]`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Status** is enabled by default. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. Each qualifying order a targeted customer places while the campaign is active adds 1 to their order count.
2. When the count reaches a milestone, that milestone's rewards are issued.
3. When every milestone is reached, the customer has completed the campaign. If **Max completions per customer** allows it, their next order starts a fresh run.
4. On a recurring schedule, progress is tracked separately for each cycle.

## Use cases / Examples

**1. Loyalty sprint.** One-time, 30 days: 3 orders → 500 bonus points. Run it mid-quarter when frequency dips.

**2. Virtual subscription.** One-time, 60 days: 2 orders → free shipping, 4 orders → 1,000 bonus points. Creates a replenishment cadence without a recurring charge.

**3. Holiday cadence push.** One-time, Nov 1 – Dec 31: 3 orders → a free product. Captures gift shopping across several occasions.

**4. Quarterly ladder.** Recurring, **Months**, **Every quarter**: 2 orders → 200 points, 4 orders → $15 off, in each active month.

## How to promote it

Frequency campaigns sell themselves once the customer sees their progress.

- **Storefront:** with **Show in loyalty widget** checked, the **[Loyalty Nudges](/onsite-widgets/loyalty-nudges)** widget, Floating Launcher and customer account page show the customer's order count and next reward.
- **Email:** state the ladder explicitly in the launch email (*"3 orders = 500 points. 5 orders = free shipping."*). With **[Klaviyo](/integrations/klaviyo)**, follow up after each order.
- **WhatsApp:** if you've connected a WhatsApp provider, nudge customers who are one order away as the window closes.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do orders placed before the campaign count?">
    No. Only orders placed while the campaign is active count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I set the time window?">
    Use the **Schedule**. A one-time schedule sets a fixed window; a recurring schedule repeats it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can one milestone have several rewards?">
    Yes. Click **Add reward** again inside the milestone.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
