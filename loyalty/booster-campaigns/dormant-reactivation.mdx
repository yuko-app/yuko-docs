<Note>
**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs → Booster Campaigns → Create campaign → **Dormant Reactivation**
</Note>

## Overview

When a customer who has been inactive for a set number of days comes back and places an order (or writes a review), they automatically get a reward. For example: **no order in 60 days → 200 bonus points on the return order**. The campaign catches every returning customer who matches the inactivity threshold.

## Why you need it?

Most winback campaigns are email-driven: segment, write, send, hope. They work, but they don't always reach the customer when they're ready to buy.

Dormant Reactivation handles the reward side automatically. The customer returns, the reward lands, and your *"welcome back"* message can quote exactly what they earned.

## When to use it?

This is one of the few boosters worth running all the time. The main decisions are the inactivity threshold and the reward size.

- You have a meaningful base of customers who have drifted away.
- You have high-value customers worth recovering.
- You sell replenishment categories where a missed reorder is an early sign of churn.

## How to configure it?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Booster Campaigns">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In the **Booster Campaigns** section, click **Create campaign** and select **Dormant Reactivation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Campaign Details">
    Enter a **Campaign Name** (internal) and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the qualifying action">
    Under **Qualifying action** (what triggers the reactivation reward), pick **Place an order** or **Write a review**. For **Place an order**, you can check **Set a minimum order amount**; only orders above this amount trigger the reward. The qualifying action can't be changed after the campaign is saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the inactivity threshold">
    **Inactivity Threshold** is the number of days (14–365) the customer must have been inactive to qualify. Common values:
    - **30 days** — early lapsers
    - **60–90 days** — noticeable disengagement
    - **180–365 days** — deep sleepers worth an aggressive offer
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the reward">
    Click **Add Reward** and choose **Amount discount**, **% off discount**, **Free shipping**, **Free product**, **Bonus points** or **Store credit** (Shopify only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set targeting and limits (optional)">
    Add **Targeting Rules** (for example **Min lifetime spend** to focus on valuable customers). With **Place an order**, **Products** and **Collections** rules are also available. Set **Max participants** and **Max completions per customer** if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule">
    **Evergreen (always on)** is the usual choice. **One-time** and **Recurring** (by weeks or months) are also available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review display, stacking, publishing and icon">
    The **Body text** widget label defaults to *"Welcome back — place an order to earn [reward]."* Tokens: `[title]`, `[reward]`, `[points_label]`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Status** is enabled by default. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. A targeted customer completes the qualifying action while the campaign is active.
2. Yuko looks up their previous order (or previous review, if the qualifying action is **Write a review**) and counts the days since then.
3. If the gap is at least the **Inactivity Threshold**, the reward is issued. The **Participants** tab shows how many days the customer was idle.
4. Customers with no previous order (or review) don't qualify, because there's no earlier activity to measure from. Use [First Purchase Boost](/loyalty/booster-campaigns/first-purchase-boost) for first-time buyers.

## Use cases / Examples

**1. Early lapser net.** 30 days, **Place an order** → 200 bonus points. Catches customers who skipped a routine purchase before they fully disengage.

**2. Quarterly winback.** 90 days → $10 off the next order.

**3. Annual reactivation.** 365 days with a **Min lifetime spend** rule → a free product. Reserved for valuable customers who have been away a long time.

**4. Review comeback.** **Write a review**, 60 days → 100 bonus points, to bring quiet reviewers back.

<Tip>
**Layer campaigns at different thresholds** (for example 30, 90 and 365 days) with escalating rewards. A long-absent customer meets several thresholds at once, so uncheck **Stackable with other campaigns** on these campaigns and order the list with the most generous one at the top if you want only one reward to apply.
</Tip>

## How to promote it

The reward is automatic, but customers still need a reason to come back.

- **Email:** run a winback flow through your ESP that mentions the reward. Yuko syncs loyalty data to **[Klaviyo](/integrations/klaviyo)**, so you can segment by points balance and activity.
- **WhatsApp:** if you've connected a WhatsApp provider, a short personal message to long-lapsed, high-value customers works well.
- **Storefront:** with **Show in loyalty widget** checked, returning customers see the campaign in the **[Loyalty Nudges](/onsite-widgets/loyalty-nudges)** widget, the **[Floating Rewards](/onsite-widgets/floating-launcher)** launcher and customer account page.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What's the shortest threshold I can set?">
    14 days. The maximum is 365 days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a new customer's first order count?">
    No. Dormant Reactivation needs a previous order (or review) to measure inactivity from.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the same customer be reactivated more than once?">
    Yes, if **Max completions per customer** is blank or higher than 1 and they go inactive again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
