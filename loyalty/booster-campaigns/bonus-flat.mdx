title: Bonus Flat Booster

<Note>
**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs → Booster Campaigns → Create campaign → **Bonus Flat**
</Note>

## Overview

Add a fixed bonus to each qualifying action while the campaign runs, for example **+50 points on every order this weekend**. The customer's normal points still apply; the flat bonus sits on top.

## Why you need it?

Multipliers ask the customer to do mental math. A flat bonus is a single number: *"+50 points on this order"* is concrete and fits an email subject line. When your message needs to convert at a glance, flat usually beats multiplier.

## When to use it?

- Your campaign copy needs a headline number (*"+100 points this week"*).
- You want the bonus to be the same regardless of basket size.
- Your base earning rate is small (for example 1 point per $1), so multipliers feel underwhelming.
- You want to give paid members an always-on bonus on top of regular earning.
- You want a predictable points cost per action.

## How to configure it?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Booster Campaigns">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In the **Booster Campaigns** section, click **Create campaign** and select **Bonus Flat**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Campaign Details">
    Enter a **Campaign Name** (internal) and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the earning action">
    Under **Earning action**, pick the Way to Earn that earns the bonus (for example **Place an order**). Only enabled Ways to Earn are listed. The earning action can't be changed after the campaign is saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Bonus points reward">
    Click **Add Reward**. Bonus Flat only accepts **Bonus points**: enter a **Name** (visible on the storefront), the **Number of points** (at least 1) and an optional expiry for the bonus points.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set targeting and limits (optional)">
    Add **Targeting Rules** such as **Customer Tags**, **Membership** or **Min lifetime orders**. If the earning action is **Place an order**, you can also add **Products** or **Collections** rules. Set **Max participants** and **Max completions per customer** if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule">
    **One-time**, **Recurring** (by days, weeks or months) or **Evergreen (always on)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review stacking, display, publishing and icon">
    Keep **Stackable with other campaigns** checked or uncheck it to make the campaign exclusive. Edit the **Body text** widget label if needed (tokens: `[title]`, `[reward]`, `[points_label]`, `[action]`). Keep **Online store** selected under **Publishing** and choose an **Icon**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Status** is enabled by default. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. While the schedule is active, a targeted customer completes the earning action.
2. They earn their normal points for that action, and Yuko credits the bonus points from the reward.
3. Each qualifying action counts as one completion, up to **Max completions per customer** if you set one.

## Use cases / Examples

### One-time uses

**1. Black Friday flat boost.** **+200 points per order** during BFCM. Customers know exactly what they're getting, and the number works in an email subject line.

**2. Email blast pairing.** Send *"+100 points on your next order this week"* and run a one-week Bonus Flat to match. The number in the email is the number on the receipt.

**3. Launch-week bonus.** +75 points per order during a product launch, with a **Collections** rule so only orders containing the new range qualify.

**4. Review push.** +50 points on **Write a review** for two weeks after a launch.

### Recurring uses

**5. Fortnightly bonus week.** Recurring, **Weeks**, **Every 2 weeks**: +25 points per order every other week.

### Always-on uses

**6. Member-only baseline.** Evergreen +10 points on every order with a **Membership** rule. It signals that membership pays off every time they buy.

## How to promote it

The big advantage of a flat bonus is that the headline writes itself.

- **Storefront:** the **[Loyalty Nudges](/onsite-widgets/loyalty-nudges)** widget, the Floating Launcher and the customer account page show active campaigns when **Show in loyalty widget** is checked.
- **Email:** make the number the subject (*"+100 points on every order this week"*). Yuko syncs loyalty data to **[Klaviyo](/integrations/klaviyo)** so you can segment by tier or balance.
- **WhatsApp:** if you've connected a WhatsApp provider, use it for the last-call message in the final 24 hours.

For always-on member bonuses, announce once in the membership welcome email and let the widgets surface the bonus from there.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I give a discount instead of points?">
    Not with Bonus Flat; it only accepts **Bonus points**. To reward with a discount or other reward, use a goal-based booster such as Spending Goal, Order Booster or Multi-Action Challenge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Max completions per customer missing?">
    It's hidden when the earning action can only be earned once per customer, such as **Sign Up**, **Instagram Follow** or **Subscribe to Newsletter**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
