<Note>
**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs → Booster Campaigns → Create campaign → **Streak**
</Note>

## Overview

A Streak rewards customers for repeating a qualifying action in consecutive periods (weeks, months or quarters). For example: **order at least once a month for 3 consecutive months → 1,000 bonus points**. If the customer misses a period, the streak resets.

## Why you need it?

Most loyalty programs reward transactions. Streaks reward **consistency**.

Consistency compounds into habit. A customer who is two months into a three-month streak protects it, and will place an order sooner than they normally would to keep it alive.

## When to use it?

- You sell repeat-purchase categories where a weekly or monthly cadence is natural.
- You want to reward customers for *not pausing*, not just for buying more.
- You're trying to build a buying rhythm rather than a one-off spike.
- You want to keep reviews flowing steadily (with **Write a review** as the qualifying action).

## How to configure it?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Booster Campaigns">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In the **Booster Campaigns** section, click **Create campaign** and select **Streak**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Campaign Details">
    Enter a **Campaign Name** (internal) and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the qualifying action">
    Under **Qualifying action**, pick **Place an order** or **Write a review** (only enabled Ways to Earn are listed). For **Place an order**, you can also check **Set a minimum order amount**; only orders above this amount count toward the streak. The qualifying action can't be changed after the campaign is saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the streak">
    - **Period** — **Week**, **Month** or **Quarter**: the unit of time for each streak interval.
    - **Consecutive Periods** — how many periods in a row the customer must keep up (2–24).
    - **Min Actions Per Period** — the minimum number of times the qualifying action must happen in each period (at least 1).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the reward">
    Click **Add Reward** and choose **Amount discount**, **% off discount**, **Free shipping**, **Free product**, **Bonus points** or **Store credit** (Shopify only). The reward is issued when the streak is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set targeting and limits (optional)">
    Add **Targeting Rules**. With **Place an order** as the qualifying action, **Products** and **Collections** rules are also available. Set **Max participants** and **Max completions per customer** if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule">
    **One-time**, **Recurring** (by weeks or months) or **Evergreen (always on)**. Streaks usually run evergreen, because the streak itself defines the time frame.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review display, stacking, publishing and icon">
    Streak has four widget labels: **Call-to-action above progress bar**, **Status text under progress bar**, **Reward banner — locked** and **Reward banner — unlocked**. Tokens include `[current]`, `[target]`, `[remaining]`, `[period]` and `[period_plural]`, plus `[title]`, `[reward]` and `[points_label]`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Status** is enabled by default. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. The first qualifying action starts the customer's current period.
2. A period counts toward the streak when the customer reaches **Min Actions Per Period** in it.
3. If a whole period passes without the action, the streak resets to zero.
4. When the customer has kept the streak for **Consecutive Periods** in a row, the reward is issued and the participant is marked **Completed**.

You can follow each customer's streak in the campaign's **Participants** tab, which shows the periods completed and the actions in the current period.

## Use cases / Examples

**1. Monthly buyer streak.** Period **Month**, 3 consecutive periods, 1 action per period, **Place an order** → 500 bonus points. Subscription-like behaviour without a recurring charge.

**2. Weekly replenishment streak.** Period **Week**, 4 consecutive periods, **Place an order** with a minimum order amount → free shipping on the next order.

**3. Quarterly loyalist.** Period **Quarter**, 4 consecutive periods → a free product. A year of steady buying earns a meaningful reward.

**4. Steady reviewer.** Period **Month**, 3 consecutive periods, **Write a review** → 300 bonus points.

## How to promote it

- **Storefront:** with **Show in loyalty widget** checked, the **[Loyalty Nudges](/onsite-widgets/loyalty-nudges)** widget, Floating Launcher and customer account page show the customer's current streak and the reward banner.
- **Email:** at the start of each period, remind customers on a streak that they need to act again to keep it. With **[Klaviyo](/integrations/klaviyo)**, you can segment by loyalty activity.
- **WhatsApp:** if you've connected a WhatsApp provider, use it for *"your streak ends this week"* nudges.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I run a daily streak?">
    No. The **Period** options are **Week**, **Month** and **Quarter**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which actions can drive a streak?">
    **Place an order** and **Write a review**. The action's Way to Earn must be enabled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I have different rewards for a 3- and a 6-period streak?">
    Create two Streak campaigns with different **Consecutive Periods**, each with its own reward.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
