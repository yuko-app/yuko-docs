**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Booster Campaigns → New booster → **Multi-Action Challenge**
</Note>

## Overview

The customer earns a bonus when they complete a set of diverse actions within a window — e.g., **Order + Submit a review + Refer a friend = 500 bonus points**. Mix any 2–10 actions across purchase, content, referral, and social.

## Why you need it?

A customer who only buys is fragile. A customer who buys, reviews, refers, and follows on social is **sticky** — they've put multiple touchpoints into your brand, and switching has a real cost.

Most loyalty programs reward each of those actions independently with small per-action points. The multi-action challenge bundles them into a single goal with a chunky bonus, making the breadth of engagement worth chasing.

## When to use it?

- Onboarding new customers across the channels you care about.
- Driving UGC during a launch (orders + reviews + social shares).
- Building a brand-ambassador program where the customer's total activity matters.
- Running a seasonal multi-touch campaign (holiday challenge with five themed actions).
- Engaging your most active customers in a layered campaign that simple multipliers can't match.

## How to configure it?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Booster Campaigns">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Booster Campaigns** and click **New booster**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Multi-Action Challenge">
    Select the **Multi-Action Challenge** booster type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the actions (2–10)">
    Choose from any Way to Earn: *Place an Order*, *Write a Review*, *Refer a Friend*, *Subscribe to Newsletter*, *Facebook Like*, *Instagram Follow*, etc.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set completion logic">
    - **All** — customer must complete every action in the list.
    - **Any N** — customer must complete any N actions from the list (e.g., “any 3 of 5”).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the bonus">
    The points awarded when the challenge is completed. Multi-action bonuses are typically chunky (500–2,000 points) because the customer is doing several things.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the time window">
    The period the customer has to complete the challenge (e.g., 14 days, 30 days, a full quarter).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set schedule, targeting, product scope">
    One-time, recurring, or always-on. Multi-action onboarding challenges are usually always-on with cohort targeting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use cases / Examples

### One-time uses

**1. Welcome challenge**

For new customers in their first 14 days: Order + Write a review + Refer a friend = 500 points. A single campaign drives three of the four most important new-customer behaviours.

**2. Full-funnel engagement**

Order + Subscribe to newsletter + Follow on social = bonus. Captures multi-channel customers in one campaign — the ones who are most likely to become long-term advocates.

**3. Brand ambassador challenge**

Refer + Share on social + Submit a photo review = elevated bonus (e.g., 1,500 points). Targets your top customers and gives them a structured way to evangelise. Combine with influencer outreach for outsized lift.

**4. Holiday challenge**

November: complete any 3 of 5 holiday-themed actions (gift purchase, refer a friend, share on social, write a review, subscribe to gift-guide newsletter) → 1,000 points. Builds multi-touch seasonal engagement.

### Recurring uses

**5. Monthly engagement bingo**

Each month, customers pick any 3 of 5 actions to complete and earn 250 points. Self-paced, low-friction, and resets monthly so it stays fresh. Best customers stack month after month.

### Always-on uses

**6. Onboarding combo (first 30 days)**

New customers who complete Order + Write a review during their first 30 days get a bonus. Always-on for the new-customer cohort. Reduces churn at the most fragile point in the lifecycle.

## How to promote it

Multi-action challenges have a lot of moving parts. Your promotion strategy should be **progress-led** — the customer needs to see how close they are to the bonus at every step.

### Storefront — Loyalty Nudges surfaces the checklist

The **[Loyalty Nudges](/onsite-widgets/loyalty-nudges)** widget surfaces the multi-action challenge progress on every storefront page — a clear *“2 of 3 done”* checklist with which actions are still outstanding, visible in the drawer from any page. The Customer Account hub also shows it for logged-in customers.

Pair with a homepage banner during the launch window for non-logged-in visitors.

### Email — launch and per-action progress

Pair the challenge with email through your ESP. **Klaviyo** is the strongest pairing because Yuko pushes events for each individual action (Order placed, Review submitted, Referral completed) and live profile properties (see [Klaviyo integration](/integrations/klaviyo)) — build a Klaviyo flow that fires after each completed action with updated progress.

- **Launch email:** list the actions clearly. *“Complete any 3 of: order, review, refer, follow on Instagram, subscribe to newsletter. Earn 500 points.”*
- **Action-complete email (Klaviyo flow):** fires after each completed action. *“1 of 3 done. Keep going.”* Use the corresponding event's properties to call out which action they just completed.
- **Last-step email:** when the customer is one action away from completing the bonus, with a clear *“finish the challenge”* CTA.

### WhatsApp — final-step nudge

If you've connected a WhatsApp provider, reserve WhatsApp for the *“one action to go”* moment, especially if the remaining action is reviewing or referring. WhatsApp shines for the action that needs the customer to *do something specific right now*.

Example: *“You've done 2 of 3. Refer a friend by Friday to earn the 500-point bonus: [referral_link]”*

### Reminder cadence

- **Day 0** — enable Loyalty Nudges + launch email with the action list
- **After each completed action** — Klaviyo flow with status update
- **One action away, window closing** — WhatsApp + email
- **Window closes, customer didn't finish** — *(optional)* a "so close" follow-up email to soften the miss and seed the next challenge
