<Note>
**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs → Booster Campaigns → Create campaign → **Multi-Action Challenge**
</Note>

## Overview

The customer earns a reward when they complete a set of actions, for example **Place an order + Write a review + Instagram Follow = 500 bonus points**. Pick 2 to 10 actions from your enabled Ways to Earn, and choose whether customers must complete all of them or any one.

## Why you need it?

A customer who only buys is fragile. A customer who buys, reviews and follows you on social is **sticky**: they've invested in several touchpoints with your brand, and switching has a real cost.

Most programs reward each of those actions separately with small per-action points. A challenge bundles them into one goal with a bigger reward, so the breadth of engagement is worth chasing.

## When to use it?

- Onboarding new customers across the channels you care about.
- Driving reviews and social engagement during a launch.
- Running a seasonal challenge with several themed actions.
- Engaging your most active customers with something richer than a multiplier.

## How to configure it?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Booster Campaigns">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In the **Booster Campaigns** section, click **Create campaign** and select **Multi-Action Challenge**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Campaign Details">
    Enter a **Campaign Name** (internal) and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the actions">
    Under **Actions Required**, click **Add action** and pick an earning action. Only enabled Ways to Earn are listed, and each action can be used once. Add between 2 and 10 actions. For **Place an order**, you can check **Set a minimum order amount** and enter a **Min order amount**; the order must meet it to count toward this action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the completion logic">
    - **Match all** — the customer must complete every action.
    - **Match any** — completing any one action unlocks the reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a time window (optional)">
    **Time Window** (days): customers must complete the actions within this many days. Leave blank for no time limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the reward">
    Click **Add Reward** and choose **Amount discount**, **% off discount**, **Free shipping**, **Free product**, **Bonus points** or **Store credit** (Shopify only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set targeting and limits (optional)">
    Add **Targeting Rules** and **Max participants**. Each customer can complete a challenge once, so there is no **Max completions per customer** field. **Products** and **Collections** rules aren't available for this type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule">
    **One-time**, **Recurring** (by weeks or months) or **Evergreen (always on)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review display, stacking, publishing and icon">
    Widget labels: **Call-to-action above progress bar**, **Reward banner — locked** and **Reward banner — unlocked**. The defaults change with the completion logic (for example *"Complete all [total_count] steps to earn:"* or *"Complete any 1 step to earn:"*). Tokens include `[done_count]`, `[total_count]` and `[remaining_count]`, plus `[title]`, `[reward]` and `[points_label]`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Status** is enabled by default. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. Each time a targeted customer completes one of the challenge actions while the campaign is active, it's ticked off on their progress.
2. With **Match all**, the reward is issued once every action is done. With **Match any**, it's issued after the first action.
3. With a **Time Window**, the clock starts at the customer's first completed action. If they don't finish within the window, their progress expires (shown as **Expired** in **Participants**) and they start again from zero.

## Use cases / Examples

**1. Welcome challenge.** Evergreen: **Sign Up** + **Place an order** + **Write a review**, **Match all**, 30-day **Time Window** → 500 bonus points.

**2. Social starter.** **Instagram Follow** + **Facebook Like** + **Subscribe to Newsletter**, **Match all** → free shipping on the next order.

**3. Launch buzz.** One-time, launch month: **Place an order** (with a minimum order amount) + **Write a review**, **Match all** → $10 off.

**4. Any-step nudge.** **Match any** across several social actions → 50 bonus points. A low-effort entry point for less engaged customers.

## How to promote it

- **Storefront:** with **Show in loyalty widget** checked, the **[Loyalty Nudges](/onsite-widgets/loyalty-nudges)** widget, Floating Launcher and customer account page show the challenge as a checklist with the reward banner.
- **Email:** list the steps and the reward clearly in a launch email, then follow up with customers who have completed some but not all steps.
- **WhatsApp:** if you've connected a WhatsApp provider, nudge customers who are one step away before the window closes.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I require any 2 of 5 actions?">
    No. The options are **Match all** (every action) or **Match any** (any one action).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I add a certain action?">
    The action's Way to Earn must be enabled, and each action can only be added once per challenge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when the time window runs out?">
    The customer's progress is marked **Expired** and resets. Their next qualifying action starts a new attempt.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
