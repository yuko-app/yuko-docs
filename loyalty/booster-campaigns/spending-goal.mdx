## Overview

A Spending Goal tracks how much a customer spends while the campaign runs and rewards them each time their total crosses a milestone. For example: **spend $150 → 500 bonus points, spend $300 → $20 off**. The spend can come from one large order or several smaller ones.

You can set up to 20 milestones, and each milestone can have one or more rewards of any type.

## Why you need it?

Multipliers and flat bonuses reward whatever the customer spends. Spending goals make the customer aim higher.

When a customer knows there's a reward for crossing $150, they add the extra item to reach it. *"I was going to spend $90"* becomes *"$150 because I want the reward"*. Basket size grows without a discount.

## When to use it?

- You want to lift average order value over a defined period.
- You're running a launch and want bigger baskets, not just more customers.
- You want a recurring stretch goal for your best customers.
- You want a ladder of rewards that keeps customers climbing.

## Before you start

<Check>The **Place an order** Way to Earn is enabled. If it's off, this type is greyed out in the **Create Booster Campaign** picker.</Check>

## How to configure it?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Booster Campaigns">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In the **Booster Campaigns** section, click **Create campaign** and select **Spending Goal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Campaign Details">
    Enter a **Campaign Name** (internal) and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add milestones">
    Under **Milestones**, set the **Spending Amount** for **Milestone 1**: the amount the customer must spend to unlock it. Click **Add milestone** for more (up to 20). Each milestone must be higher than the one before.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add rewards to each milestone">
    In each milestone, click **Add reward** and choose **Amount discount**, **% off discount**, **Free shipping**, **Free product**, **Bonus points** or **Store credit** (Shopify only). Every milestone needs at least one reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a minimum order amount (optional)">
    Under **Spending Booster Goal**, check **Set a minimum order amount** and enter the **Minimum Order Amount**. Only orders above this amount count toward each milestone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set targeting and limits (optional)">
    Add **Targeting Rules**, including **Products** or **Collections** rules. Set **Max participants** and **Max completions per customer** if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule">
    The schedule defines the window in which spend counts: **One-time** (start and end date), **Recurring** (by weeks or months, with progress tracked per cycle) or **Evergreen (always on)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review display, stacking, publishing and icon">
    Two widget labels apply: **Call-to-action above progress bar** (default *"Spend [remaining] more to unlock [next_reward]"*) and **Status text under progress bar** (default *"[current] / [target]"*). Other tokens: `[title]`, `[reward]`, `[points_label]`, `[milestones_done]`, `[milestones_total]`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Status** is enabled by default. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. Each order a targeted customer places while the campaign is active adds its order total to their running spend (orders below the minimum order amount are skipped).
2. When the running spend reaches a milestone, that milestone's rewards are issued. One large order can unlock several milestones at once.
3. Once every milestone is reached, the customer has completed the campaign. If **Max completions per customer** allows it, their next order starts a fresh run.
4. On a recurring schedule, progress is tracked separately for each cycle.

Track customers' progress in the campaign's **Participants** tab and issued rewards in the **Activity** tab.

## Use cases / Examples

**1. Launch-week ladder.** One-time, launch week: $100 → 200 bonus points, $200 → free shipping, $300 → $25 off.

**2. Quarterly stretch goal.** Recurring, **Months**, **Every quarter**: spend $300 in the active month → 1,000 bonus points.

**3. Collection-focused spend.** Add a **Collections** rule so only customers buying from a specific range qualify, with milestones at $150 and $250.

**4. Holiday gifting goal.** One-time, Dec 1–15: spend $250 → 1,000 points. Gift baskets are bigger anyway; the reward tilts the customer's full gift list toward you.

**5. VIP-only stretch.** Evergreen with a **Customer Tags** or **Min lifetime spend** rule, and a free product at the top milestone.

## How to promote it

Spending goals work best when the customer can see their progress.

- **Storefront:** with **Show in loyalty widget** checked, the **[Loyalty Nudges](/onsite-widgets/loyalty-nudges)** widget, Floating Launcher and customer account page show a progress bar using your widget labels.
- **Email:** announce the milestones and the window through your ESP. With **[Klaviyo](/integrations/klaviyo)**, you can trigger a follow-up after each order to remind customers how close they are.
- **WhatsApp:** if you've connected a WhatsApp provider, save it for customers who are close to a milestone as the window closes.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where did the time-window setting go?">
    The window is the campaign's **Schedule**. Use a one-time schedule for a fixed window, or a recurring schedule for a repeating one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does one order count twice?">
    No. Each order is counted once per cycle.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a milestone have more than one reward?">
    Yes. Click **Add reward** again inside the milestone.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
