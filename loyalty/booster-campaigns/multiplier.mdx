**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs → Booster Campaigns → Create campaign → **Multiplier**
</Note>

## Overview

While the campaign runs, every time a customer completes the chosen earning action they earn a multiple (for example 2× or 3×) of the points that Way to Earn normally gives. The customer pays the same price and simply earns more.

The multiplier works on any enabled Way to Earn, not just orders. To boost only certain products or collections, use the [Product & Collection Multiplier](/loyalty/booster-campaigns/product-collection-multiplier) instead.

## Why you need it?

When sales are flat, the default reflex is to discount. Discounting trains customers to wait for the next promotion, eats into margin and slowly erodes the perceived value of your products.

A points multiplier creates the same urgency without cutting price. The customer sees an obvious reason to buy now, your margin holds, and loyal customers feel rewarded rather than pushed into a sale.

## When to use it?

- You want a near-term lift in revenue without discounting.
- You're entering a slow week and need a lever that doesn't condition customers to expect markdowns.
- You want an alternative to a competitor's discount campaign during a high-stakes period.
- You want to give a VIP tier or paid members an always-on earning advantage.
- You want to boost a non-purchase action for a while, such as reviews.

## How to configure it?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Booster Campaigns">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In the **Booster Campaigns** section, click **Create campaign** and select **Multiplier**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Campaign Details">
    Enter a **Campaign Name** (internal) and an optional **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the earning action">
    Under **Earning action**, pick the Way to Earn this campaign applies to (for example **Place an order**). Only enabled Ways to Earn are listed. The earning action can't be changed after the campaign is saved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Multiplier reward">
    Click **Add Reward**. Enter a **Name** (visible on the storefront) and the **Multiplier**, a value between 1.1 and 10.0. A Multiplier campaign has exactly one reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set targeting and limits (optional)">
    Add **Targeting Rules** (for example **VIP Tiers** or **Customer Tags**). If the earning action is **Place an order**, you can also add **Products** or **Collections** rules. Set **Max participants** and **Max completions per customer** if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule">
    **One-time** (start and end date), **Recurring** (by days, weeks or months) or **Evergreen (always on)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set stacking">
    **Stackable with other campaigns** is checked by default. Uncheck it to make the campaign exclusive. Check **Ignore if customer earned VIP tier multiplier** to skip the boost when the customer's VIP tier already gave them a bonus on the same action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review display, publishing and icon">
    Edit the **Body text** widget label if you want custom copy (tokens: `[title]`, `[reward]`, `[points_label]`, `[action]`), keep **Online store** selected under **Publishing**, and pick an **Icon**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **Status** is enabled by default. Click **Save**. The boost applies from the moment the schedule is active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. The customer completes the earning action and earns their normal points (plus any VIP tier bonus).
2. Yuko credits the boost as a separate bonus entry: **base points × (multiplier − 1)**. With a 2× multiplier, a 100-point order earns 100 extra points.
3. The bonus is calculated on the base points only, so it doesn't compound with the VIP tier bonus. The bonus also inherits the base points' pending period and expiry.
4. If **Ignore if customer earned VIP tier multiplier** is checked and a VIP tier bonus was awarded for that same action, no boost is added.

## Use cases / Examples

### One-time uses

**1. Slow week rescue.** Numbers are flat on Monday morning, so you run a 3-day 2× event on **Place an order** instead of reaching for a discount code.

**2. BFCM alternative.** Instead of joining the discount race in November, run a 3× points event. Same urgency, no price cut.

**3. Review drive.** 2× on **Write a review** for two weeks after a big launch to collect feedback faster.

**4. Store anniversary.** A natural reason to run a celebration event. Feels earned, not manufactured.

### Recurring uses

**5. Double-points month every quarter.** Recurring, **Months**, **Every quarter**: one month in every three runs at 2×. Customers learn the rhythm and plan purchases around it.

**6. Fortnightly boost week.** Recurring, **Weeks**, **Every 2 weeks**: a 2× week, then a normal week, repeating.

### Always-on uses

**7. VIP tier accelerator.** Evergreen 2× with a **VIP Tiers** rule for your top tier. The points program becomes a real reason to climb.

**8. Member perk.** Evergreen 1.5× with a **Membership** rule so paid members always earn more.

## How to promote it

### Storefront

The **[Loyalty Nudges](/onsite-widgets/loyalty-nudges)** widget shows active campaigns in a pinned strip and drawer on every storefront page. The **[Floating Rewards](/onsite-widgets/floating-launcher)** launcher and the customer account page also list active boosters when **Show in loyalty widget** is checked.

### Email and WhatsApp

Announce the multiplier through your ESP. Yuko syncs loyalty data such as `yuko_points_balance` and `yuko_vip_tier` to **[Klaviyo](/integrations/klaviyo)**, so you can segment the send.

- **Subject line:** lead with the multiplier and the urgency, for example *"3× points — this weekend only"*.
- **Body:** state the multiplier, the exact dates and what to buy.
- **Last call:** if you've connected a WhatsApp provider, save WhatsApp for the last 24 hours of the campaign.

For always-on multipliers (VIP tier, membership), promote once at the moment of qualification, then let the widgets do the rest.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I add a collection multiplier on top?">
    Not inside a Multiplier campaign; it accepts one Multiplier reward. Create a separate [Product & Collection Multiplier](/loyalty/booster-campaigns/product-collection-multiplier) campaign. If both are stackable, both apply to matching orders.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the multiplier apply to points that are pending?">
    Yes. The bonus copies the base points' pending period and expiry, so it becomes available at the same time as the base points.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Max completions per customer missing?">
    It's hidden when the earning action can only be earned once per customer, such as **Sign Up**, **Instagram Follow** or **Subscribe to Newsletter**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
