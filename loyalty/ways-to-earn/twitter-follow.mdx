## Introduction

Award points when customers follow your X (formerly Twitter) account from the loyalty widget.

## What problem this solves

For brands with a Twitter/X presence, organic follower growth is slow. A points-incentivised follow turns existing customers into followers in seconds.

## When to use

- You have an active X account.
- Your audience uses X.
- You want a one-time low-friction earning action.

## Overview

- One-time reward per customer.
- Configured with your X username.
- Optional points expiry.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Ways to Earn → Twitter Follow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set details and points">
    Enter campaign name, description and points value (e.g., `10`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your X username">
    Enter your handle (without the @).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure expiry (optional)">
    Set expiry if desired.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- The action appears in the loyalty widget.
- Customer is sent to your X profile.
- Points are credited on intent.

### Limitations

- Verification is intent-based.
- One-time per customer.

## Examples

- 10 points for an X follow, no expiry.
