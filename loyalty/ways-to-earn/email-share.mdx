## Introduction

Award points when customers share your store via email.

## What problem this solves

Email shares have lower volume than social, but high intent — customers email people they think will buy. A small reward turns this into a low-cost referral channel.

## When to use

- You sell B2B or considered-purchase items where customers email recommendations.
- You want a complementary share channel beyond social.

## Overview

- Points per share with optional cadence cap.
- Pre-configurable subject and body text.
- Optional points expiry.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Ways to Earn → Email Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set details and points">
    Enter campaign name and points value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure subject and body">
    Pre-fill what opens in the customer's email client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure expiry (optional)">
    Set expiry if desired.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- The share action surfaces in the widget.
- Clicking opens the customer's default email client with prefilled content.
- Points are credited on share intent.

### Limitations

- Verification is intent-based.
- Cadence cap recommended to prevent spam.

## Examples

- 15 points per email share, capped at 1 per week.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Match the email tone to the channel** — customers won't forward marketing-heavy copy.</Tip>
