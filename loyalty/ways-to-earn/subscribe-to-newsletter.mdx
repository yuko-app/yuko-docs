## Introduction

Award points when a customer subscribes to your email newsletter. A direct way to convert anonymous browsers into a marketable list.

## What problem this solves

Free shipping and 10%-off popups have lost their punch. A points-based opt-in incentive feels like ongoing value rather than a one-shot discount.

## When to use

- You're growing an email list.
- You want to convert email captures into loyalty members from day one.
- You're running Klaviyo, Omnisend, Shopify Email or similar.

## Overview

- One-time reward when a customer opts in.
- Triggered by Shopify customer email-marketing-consent or via integration with your ESP.
- Optional expiry on awarded points.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Ways to Earn → Subscribe to Newsletter**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set details and points">
    Enter campaign name and points value (e.g., `30`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- Yuko detects email-marketing-consent on the Shopify customer record.
- Points are credited once per customer.
- If the customer unsubscribes, the points stay; they don't reverse.

### Decision guidance

- **Calibrate the bonus** so it doesn't outweigh other one-time campaigns.
- **Surface the bonus on your popup or signup form** — customers need to know it exists.

### Limitations

- Customers who were already opted in before installing Yuko don't earn historically.
- Re-subscribing after unsubscribing doesn't re-earn.

## Examples

- 30 points for newsletter subscription, paired with a popup that says *“Join our list. Get 30 points.”*

## Best practices

<Tip>**Mention the points reward in your popup copy.** Conversion lifts noticeably.</Tip>
