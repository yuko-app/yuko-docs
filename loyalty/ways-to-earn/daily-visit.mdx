## Introduction

Award a small bonus to customers who come back to your store regularly. Effective for stores with content beyond products, such as lookbooks, blogs, restock pages or drop calendars.

## What problem this solves

It's easy to focus on the first purchase and forget about repeat visits. Daily Visit rewards customers for coming back between orders.

## When to use

- You publish content beyond products (blogs, lookbooks, drop calendars).
- You run regular product drops or restocks.
- You want customers visiting between purchases.

## Overview

- Reward a visit once per **Earning interval**: Daily, Weekly or Monthly.
- Optional **URL** to only count visits to a specific page.
- Points are awarded automatically; the customer doesn't need to click anything.
- Optional points expiry and VIP tier multipliers.
- Available on select plans.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the way to earn">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In **Ways to earn**, click **Add another way** and pick **Daily Visit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Basic Details">
    - **Campaign Name** — shown to customers on the storefront. You can use `[earn_value]` and `[points_label]`, for example `Earn [earn_value] [points_label]`.
    - **Description** — short copy shown with the way to earn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the points">
    Enter **Points to earn on daily visit** (a whole number, at least 1).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the URL and Earning interval">
    - **URL** (optional) — a store path where customers must visit to earn, for example `/pages/about-us` or `/products/example`. Leave it empty to allow earning on any page.
    - **Earning interval** — how often customers can earn: **Daily** (the default), **Weekly** or **Monthly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Expiry (optional)">
    Turn on **Expiry**, then set **Expire after** and a **Period** (Day, Week, Month or Year). Expiry is available on select plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the side panel">
    - **Status** — on by default.
    - **Icon** — **Default**, or **Custom** to upload your own (JPG, PNG, GIF or WEBP, under 1 MB).
    - **Tiers** — turn on to apply VIP tier multipliers to this way to earn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- When a logged-in customer loads a storefront page, the Yuko theme app embed checks whether Daily Visit is active.
- If you set a URL, the visit only counts on that path or pages under it. The launcher also shows a **Visit** button that links to it.
- If the customer hasn't earned yet in the current interval, the points are credited automatically.
- **Daily** resets each day, **Weekly** each calendar week (Monday start), **Monthly** each calendar month.

With **Tiers** on and [VIP tiers](/vip-tiers/overview) enabled, customers in a tier earn the tier's multiplier for this way to earn, plus any bonus points the tier sets for it. The extra points appear as a separate tier bonus entry.

### Decision guidance

- **Keep the value tiny** — 5 points a day, not 50. This shouldn't dominate your points economy.
- **Use a URL** when you have a page worth visiting, like a drops calendar.
- **Add a short expiry** so daily points don't pile up.

### Limitations

- Customers must be logged in. Anonymous visits don't earn.
- The [Theme App Embed](/installation-and-setup/theme-app-embed) must be enabled.
- Reloading or opening more tabs doesn't add points; one credit per interval.
- You can have one Daily Visit way to earn.

## Examples

- A streetwear brand awards 5 points per day for visiting `/pages/drops`, with a 14-day expiry.
- A content-led store awards 10 points weekly for any logged-in visit.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Use a URL whenever you can.** It rewards visiting a page that matters, not just any page.</Tip>
<Tip>**Pair it with content worth visiting.** Daily Visit works best when there's something new to see.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can guests earn this?">
    No. The customer must be logged in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if the customer opens several tabs?">
    Only one credit is awarded per interval per customer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the customer need to click anything?">
    No. Points are awarded automatically when a logged-in customer loads a matching page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does mobile count?">
    Yes. Any device counts as long as the customer is logged in and the theme app embed is enabled.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
