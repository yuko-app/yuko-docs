## Introduction

Award a small bonus to customers who return to your store on a regular cadence. Effective for stores with content beyond products — lookbooks, blogs, restock pages, or pre-order announcements.

## What problem this solves

It's easy to optimise for the *first* purchase and forget about repeat traffic. Daily Visit gently nudges customers to come back and treats engagement as worth rewarding.

## When to use

- You publish content beyond products (blogs, lookbooks, drop calendars).
- You run regular product drops or restocks.
- You want to keep customers visiting between purchases.

## Overview

- Reward customers per visit on a configurable cadence.
- Cadence options: daily, weekly or monthly.
- Optional URL restriction (only count visits to a specific page).
- Optional points expiry.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    Go to **Loyalty → Ways to Earn → Daily Visit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the points value">
    Keep it small (e.g., 5–10 points). The cadence does the work, not the size.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the earning interval">
    Choose **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restrict by URL (optional)">
    Limit earning to visits to a specific path (e.g., a blog or a drop page).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure expiry (optional)">
    Recommended: short expiry (e.g., 30 days) to keep the loyalty economy clean.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- The widget runtime tracks the logged-in customer's visit per session.
- A visit awards points only if the cadence window has elapsed since the last credit.
- Activity is recorded in the customer's transaction log.

### Decision guidance

- **Keep the value tiny** — 5 points daily, not 50. This campaign should not dominate the points economy.
- **Use URL restriction** when you have content destinations worth rewarding (avoids gaming via bot traffic).
- **Add a short expiry** — daily-visit points should burn quickly so they don't pile up.

### Limitations

- Requires the customer to be logged in. Anonymous visits don't earn.
- The widget runtime must be loaded — ensure the [Theme App Embed](/installation-and-setup/theme-app-embed) is enabled.
- Refreshing the page repeatedly does not stack points; only one credit per cadence window.

## Examples

- A streetwear brand awards 5 points per daily visit to its drops calendar page, expiring in 14 days.
- A media-led store awards 10 points weekly for any logged-in visit to keep customers in the habit of checking back.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Restrict by URL whenever possible.** It encodes intent: visit *that* page, not just any page.</Tip>
<Tip>**Don't run this in isolation.** It only works when paired with content worth visiting.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can guests earn this?">
    No — the customer must be logged in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if the customer opens multiple tabs?">
    Only one credit is awarded per cadence window per customer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does mobile count?">
    Yes, any device with the storefront loaded counts — as long as the embed is enabled.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
