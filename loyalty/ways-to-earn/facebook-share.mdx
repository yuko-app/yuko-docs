## Introduction

Award customers a one-time points bonus when they share a link to your store or a product on Facebook from your loyalty widgets.

## What problem this solves

Word of mouth is a high-converting acquisition channel and hard to create on demand. A points reward gives customers a nudge to share.

## When to use

- Your audience is on Facebook.
- You have a page worth sharing, such as your homepage, a launch or a bestseller.
- You want a low-effort, one-time way to earn.

## Overview

- One-time reward per customer.
- Configured with a **Share URL**.
- Points are credited when the customer clicks **Share**.
- Optional points expiry and VIP tier multipliers.
- Available on select plans.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the way to earn">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In **Ways to earn**, click **Add another way** and pick **Facebook Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Basic Details">
    - **Campaign Name** — shown to customers on the storefront. You can use `[earn_value]` and `[points_label]`, for example `Earn [earn_value] [points_label]`.
    - **Description** — short copy shown with the way to earn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the points">
    Enter **Points to earn on Facebook share** (a whole number, at least 1).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Share URL">
    Enter the complete **Share URL** customers will share, for example `https://yourwebsite.com/product`. It must be a valid URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Expiry (optional)">
    Turn on **Expiry**, then set **Expire after** and a **Period** (Day, Week, Month or Year). Expiry is available on select plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the side panel">
    - **Status** — on by default.
    - **Icon** — **Default**, or **Custom** to upload your own (JPG, PNG, GIF or WEBP, under 1 MB).
    - **Tiers** — turn on to apply VIP tier multipliers to this way to earn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. The way to earn appears in the **Ways to earn** list of your loyalty widgets (for example the [Floating Rewards](/onsite-widgets/floating-launcher) launcher, [rewards landing page](/onsite-widgets/rewards-landing-page) and [customer account page](/onsite-widgets/customer-account-page)) with a **Share** button.
2. A logged-in customer clicks **Share**. Facebook's share dialog opens with your Share URL.
3. Yuko credits the points as soon as the customer clicks. Social networks don't let apps confirm the action, so earning is based on the click.
4. The way to earn then shows as completed for that customer.

With **Tiers** on and [VIP tiers](/vip-tiers/overview) enabled, customers in a tier earn the tier's multiplier for this way to earn, plus any bonus points the tier sets for it. The extra points appear as a separate tier bonus entry.

### Decision guidance

- **Share a page worth sharing.** A launch page or bestseller gets more clicks from friends than a generic homepage.
- **Keep the reward modest.** It's a quick one-time action.

### Limitations

- Earning is based on the click; Facebook doesn't let apps confirm that a post was published.
- One reward per customer. Sharing again doesn't earn more.
- Customers must be logged in to earn.
- You can have one Facebook Share way to earn, with one Share URL.

## Examples

- 15 points for sharing a new collection page on Facebook during a launch.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Update the Share URL for big moments.** Point it at your current launch or sale page.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can customers earn multiple times?">
    No. Facebook Share is a one-time reward per customer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the shared link later?">
    Yes. Edit the **Share URL** and save. Customers who already earned won't earn again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is the share verified?">
    It isn't. Yuko credits points when the customer clicks **Share** and the share dialog opens.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
