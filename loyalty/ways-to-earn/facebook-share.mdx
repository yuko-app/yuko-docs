## Introduction

Award points when customers share your store on Facebook from the loyalty widget.

## What problem this solves

Word-of-mouth is the highest-converting acquisition channel and the hardest to manufacture. A points reward for shares is one of the few ways to nudge it on demand.

## When to use

- Your audience is on Facebook.
- You want a measurable lift in shares around a launch or promo.

## Overview

- Points awarded per share, with optional cap per customer.
- Configurable share link.
- Optional points expiry.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Ways to Earn → Facebook Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set details and points">
    Enter campaign name, description and points value (e.g., `15`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure share link (if applicable)">
    Set the URL or product page that gets shared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure expiry (optional)">
    Set expiry to keep balances clean.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- The share action surfaces in the loyalty widget.
- Clicking opens Facebook's share dialog.
- Points are credited on share intent.

### Decision guidance

- **Cap the action per cadence** to prevent spam shares.
- **Pair with a meaningful share asset** — a product launch, a campaign page, etc.

### Limitations

- Verification is intent-based; Facebook does not expose share confirmation programmatically.

## Examples

- 15 points per Facebook Share around a Black Friday launch, capped at 1 per customer per week.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Don't reward generic shares** — give customers a focal page worth sharing.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can customers earn multiple times?">
    Depends on your cap settings. Without a cap, share spam is possible.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
