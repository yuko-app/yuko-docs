</Note>

## Introduction

Award points to customers who leave a review after buying. Reviews are one of the highest-leverage forms of UGC, and a small points bonus consistently lifts submission rates.

## What problem this solves

Most customers don't write reviews unless asked. Even fewer write them with photos or detail. A points reward turns a one-time post-purchase action into a measurable conversion.

## When to use

- You're running Yuko Reviews (or another reviews app integrated with Yuko).
- You want to lift review submission rate or photo-review rate.
- You're launching a new product and need fresh social proof.

## Overview

- Award points per submitted review.
- Optional **media bonus** — extra points if the review includes a photo or video.
- One-time per review per customer per product.
- Optional points expiry.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Ways to Earn → Write a Review**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set details and points">
    Enter campaign name and base points value (e.g., `20`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure media bonus (optional)">
    Add extra points awarded when a review includes a photo or video (e.g., +30).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure expiry (optional)">
    A 30-day expiry encourages prompt redemption.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- Yuko awards points when a review is submitted and approved.
- If the review is rejected or removed, the points are reversed.
- The bonus is paid once per review; editing a review doesn't re-trigger it.

### Decision guidance

- **Set a higher media bonus** — photo reviews convert dramatically better than text-only.
- **Pair with a post-purchase review request email** to maximise opt-in.
- **Don't reward fake reviews** — use moderation in your reviews tool to gate approvals.

### Limitations

- The campaign relies on review approval. Auto-approval awards points immediately; moderated approval awards on approval.
- Customers can only earn once per product, even if they submit multiple reviews.

## Examples

- 20 points per review + 30 bonus for a photo → 50 points for a media review. At 100 pts = $1, that's $0.50 reward.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Set the media bonus higher than the base reward.** Photos drive conversion; pay for them.</Tip>
<Tip>**Always moderate reviews** before they earn — stops fake-review farming.</Tip>
