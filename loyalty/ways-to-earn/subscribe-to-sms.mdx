## Introduction

Award customers a one-time points bonus when they subscribe to your SMS marketing. Points are awarded automatically when Shopify records their SMS consent.

## What problem this solves

SMS has high open rates, but customers are cautious about sharing their number. A points reward makes the opt-in feel worth it.

## When to use

- You send SMS marketing.
- You want to grow your SMS subscriber list.

## Overview

- One-time reward per customer.
- Triggered when Shopify records the customer's SMS marketing consent as subscribed.
- Optional points expiry and VIP tier multipliers.
- Available on Shopify stores, on select plans.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the way to earn">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In **Ways to earn**, click **Add another way** and pick **Subscribe to SMS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Basic Details">
    - **Campaign Name** — shown to customers on the storefront. You can use `[earn_value]` and `[points_label]`, for example `Earn [earn_value] [points_label]`.
    - **Description** — short copy shown with the way to earn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the points">
    Enter **Points to earn for SMS subscription** (a whole number, at least 1).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Expiry (optional)">
    Turn on **Expiry**, then set **Expire after** and a **Period** (Day, Week, Month or Year). Expiry is available on select plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the side panel">
    - **Status** — on by default.
    - **Icon** — **Default**, or **Custom** to upload your own (JPG, PNG, GIF or WEBP, under 1 MB).
    - **Tiers** — turn on to apply VIP tier multipliers to this way to earn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- Shopify notifies Yuko when a customer's SMS marketing consent changes.
- If the customer is now subscribed, Yuko awards the points.
- It doesn't matter where the customer subscribed (for example your storefront, checkout, or an app that updates consent in Shopify), as long as Shopify records the consent.
- Each customer earns once. Unsubscribing doesn't remove the points, and subscribing again doesn't earn more.

With **Tiers** on and [VIP tiers](/vip-tiers/overview) enabled, customers in a tier earn the tier's multiplier for this way to earn, plus any bonus points the tier sets for it. The extra points appear as a separate tier bonus entry.

### Decision guidance

- **Calibrate the bonus** so it doesn't outweigh your other one-time ways to earn.
- **Tell customers about the reward** wherever you ask them to subscribe.

### Limitations

- Shopify stores only. It doesn't appear on other platforms.
- Customers who were already subscribed before you enabled it don't earn for past consent.
- One reward per customer.
- You can have one Subscribe to SMS way to earn.

## Examples

- 50 points for subscribing to SMS, promoted at checkout next to the SMS consent box.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Offer more than for email.** Customers value their phone number more, so a slightly bigger reward helps.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens if a customer unsubscribes?">
    The points stay. They aren't reversed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do existing subscribers earn?">
    Not for consent given before you enabled it. Only new consent updates recorded by Shopify trigger the reward.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
