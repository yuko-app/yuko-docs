## Introduction

Award points when customers follow your Instagram account through the loyalty widget.

## What problem this solves

Instagram remains a key channel for visual-first brands. A points reward gets existing customers to follow on a network where you'll surface again and again.

## When to use

- Your audience is on Instagram.
- You're growing your Instagram presence.
- You want a low-friction one-time earning action.

## Overview

- One-time reward per customer.
- Configured with your Instagram username.
- Optional points expiry.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Ways to Earn → Instagram Follow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set details and points">
    Enter campaign name, description and points value (e.g., `10`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your Instagram username">
    Enter your handle (without the @).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure expiry (optional)">
    Set expiry if desired.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- The action appears in the loyalty widget.
- The customer is sent to your Instagram profile.
- Points are credited on intent.

### Decision guidance

- **Keep the bonus small.** This is a low-effort action.

### Limitations

- Instagram doesn't expose follow verification; intent-based crediting only.
- One-time per customer.

## Examples

- 10 points for an Instagram Follow, expiring in 30 days.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Audit your handle** — a wrong handle silently breaks the campaign.</Tip>
