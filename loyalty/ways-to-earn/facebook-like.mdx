## Introduction

Award customers a one-time bonus when they like your Facebook page through the loyalty widget.

## What problem this solves

Growing a Facebook page audience organically is hard. A small points reward gets your existing customers to take a 2-second action that compounds your social proof.

## When to use

- You have an active Facebook page.
- Your audience is on Facebook in non-trivial numbers.
- You want a low-friction earning action with a one-time cap.

## Overview

- One-time reward per customer.
- Configured with your Facebook page URL.
- Verification is intent-based (the customer clicks through to like).
- Optional points expiry.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Ways to Earn → Facebook Like**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set details and points">
    Enter campaign name, description and points value (e.g., `10`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your Facebook page URL">
    Paste the full URL to your Facebook page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure expiry (optional)">
    Set expiry if desired.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- The action appears in the loyalty widget on the storefront.
- The customer clicks through to your Facebook page in a new tab.
- They click Like on Facebook.
- Yuko credits the points (intent-based verification — see Limitations).

### Decision guidance

- **Keep the bonus modest** (5–15 points). It's a low-friction one-time action.
- **Don't run all social campaigns at once** — pick the 2–3 networks where your audience actually engages.

### Limitations

- Facebook restricts programmatic verification of likes; verification is intent-based.
- One-time per customer; subsequent likes don't earn.

## Examples

- A beauty brand awards 10 points for a Facebook Like, expiring in 30 days.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Audit the audience match before running.** If your customers aren't on Facebook, this campaign yields little.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is the like verified?">
    Verification is intent-based: the customer is sent to your page and the points are credited. Yuko cannot programmatically check Facebook's like state.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a customer earn this twice?">
    No — one-time per customer.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
