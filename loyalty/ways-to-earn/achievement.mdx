## Introduction

Achievements are custom milestones that reward specific behavioural patterns — a customer's 5th order, completing their profile, hitting a streak, etc. They're a building block for gamified loyalty experiences.

## What problem this solves

Linear earning rewards every action equally. Achievements call out specific milestones, making customers feel recognised for behaviour you actually care about.

## When to use

- You want to call out specific behavioural milestones.
- You're building a gamified loyalty experience (badges, streaks).
- You want to encourage behaviours not covered by other campaigns.

## Overview

- Configurable trigger event and threshold.
- One-time per achievement per customer.
- Triggers on specific Shopify events (Nth order, profile completion, etc.).

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Ways to Earn → Achievement**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the achievement type">
    Choose the trigger (e.g., *Nth order*, *Profile complete*, *Custom event*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set threshold and points">
    For threshold-based achievements, set the value (e.g., 5 orders) and the bonus (e.g., 250 points).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure expiry (optional)">
    Set an expiry on the bonus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- Yuko evaluates the achievement criteria when the underlying event fires.
- The bonus is awarded once per customer per achievement.
- Activity entries label the achievement clearly so customers see *what* they earned for.

### Decision guidance

- **Use it sparingly.** Achievements lose impact if there are too many.
- **Pair with a notification email** so customers see the achievement, not just the points.

### Limitations

- Custom event triggers require the corresponding Shopify or Yuko event to be wired up.
- Refunds and cancellations may invalidate order-count achievements; behaviour depends on configuration.

## Examples

- *"5th order"* → 250 points — marks loyal repeat purchasers.
- *"Profile complete"* → 50 points — nudges customers to fill in birthday and preferences.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Tie achievements to a story** — each one should feel like a chapter, not a transaction.</Tip>
