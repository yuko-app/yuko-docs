## Introduction

Give customers a points bonus on their birthday. It's a low-cost, high-emotion gesture that consistently drives high engagement and unprompted purchases.

## What problem this solves

Birthday emails are everywhere. A points bonus stands out because it's an actual gift the customer can spend, not a generic *Happy Birthday!* email.

## When to use

- You collect (or are willing to collect) birthdays from customers.
- You want a recurring engagement moment that doesn't feel promotional.
- You want to reward your most loyal customers with something their inbox doesn't already do.

## Overview

- Yearly bonus on the customer's birthday.
- Requires the customer's birthdate — collected via account profile, popup, or imported.
- Optional points expiry to encourage prompt use.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    Go to **Loyalty → Ways to Earn → Birthday**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set details and points">
    Enter a campaign name, description and points value (e.g., `200`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set expiry (optional)">
    A 30-day expiry is a healthy default — it tells customers the gift is for now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- Yuko runs a daily check at the start of each customer's local day.
- If today matches the customer's stored birthdate (year ignored), the points are credited.
- The customer can be notified via the **Points earned** email if enabled.

### Decision guidance

- **Pick a generous bonus** — birthdays only happen once a year. 200–500 points is a common range.
- **Add an expiry** so the gift creates urgency to come back.

### Limitations

- Customers without a stored birthdate don't earn (silent skip).
- Year-of-birth is ignored; only month + day are matched.

## Examples

- A homeware brand awards 300 points on birthday with 30-day expiry. Customers redeem on a self-treat purchase.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Collect birthdates at signup**. Adding a single optional field captures most of the data you need.</Tip>
<Tip>**Pair with a birthday email**. The points bonus + a celebratory email outperforms either alone.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do customers add their birthday?">
    Through the Yuko account block on your storefront, or through your signup form if you collect it there. You can also import birthdates via CSV.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if a customer changes their birthday after earning?">
    Yuko prevents earning more than once per calendar year for the same customer. Edits don't re-trigger the bonus in the same year.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
