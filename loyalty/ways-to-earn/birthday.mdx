## Introduction

Give customers a points bonus on their birthday. It's a low-cost, high-emotion gesture that brings customers back to your store.

## What problem this solves

Birthday emails are everywhere. A points bonus stands out because it's a gift the customer can actually spend.

## When to use

- You collect (or are willing to collect) birthdays from customers.
- You want a yearly engagement moment that doesn't feel promotional.
- You want to reward customers with something their inbox doesn't already offer.

## Overview

- Yearly bonus on the customer's birthday.
- Needs the customer's birth date, which customers can add in your loyalty widgets.
- Optional points expiry and VIP tier multipliers.
- Available on select plans.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the way to earn">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In **Ways to earn**, click **Add another way** and pick **Birthday**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Basic Details">
    - **Campaign Name** — shown to customers on the storefront. You can use `[earn_value]` and `[points_label]`, for example `Earn [earn_value] [points_label]`.
    - **Description** — short copy shown with the way to earn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the points">
    Enter **Points to earn on birthday** (a whole number, at least 1).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Expiry (optional)">
    Turn on **Expiry**, then set **Expire after** and a **Period** (Day, Week, Month or Year). Expiry is available on select plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the side panel">
    - **Status** — on by default.
    - **Icon** — **Default**, or **Custom** to upload your own (JPG, PNG, GIF or WEBP, under 1 MB).
    - **Tiers** — turn on to apply VIP tier multipliers to this way to earn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- Yuko runs a daily check for customers whose birthday is today (month and day; the year is ignored).
- Matching customers with an active Birthday way to earn get the points.
- Each customer earns once per year.
- Customers born on 29 February are rewarded on 1 March in non-leap years.
- You can also send the [Birthday Reminder](/emails/loyalty-emails) email and use the Birthday Reminder trigger in [Shopify Flow](/integrations/shopify-flow).

With **Tiers** on and [VIP tiers](/vip-tiers/overview) enabled, customers in a tier earn the tier's multiplier for this way to earn, plus any bonus points the tier sets for it. The extra points appear as a separate tier bonus entry.

### Decision guidance

- **Pick a generous bonus.** Birthdays happen once a year; 200–500 points is a common range.
- **Add an expiry** so the gift creates a reason to come back soon.

### Limitations

- Customers without a birth date don't earn.
- Excluded customers don't earn.
- Birthday points are awarded to Shopify customers.
- You can have one Birthday way to earn.

## Examples

- A homeware brand awards 300 points on birthdays with a 30-day expiry. Customers use them on a self-treat purchase.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Ask for birthdays early.** Prompt customers to add their birthday when they join.</Tip>
<Tip>**Pair with a birthday email.** Points plus a celebratory message work better than either alone.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do customers add their birthday?">
    Through the birthday section in your loyalty widgets, such as the [floating launcher](/onsite-widgets/floating-launcher). You can also see each customer's birthday on the [Customers](/loyalty/customers) list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can customers change their birthday?">
    Not from the storefront. Once a birthday is saved through the loyalty widget, the customer can't change it there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a customer earn more than once a year?">
    No. Customers earn the Birthday reward on their birthday, once a year.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
