## Introduction

Reward customers each year on the anniversary of joining your store. It's a simple way to thank customers for sticking around.

## What problem this solves

Most stores never thank customers for staying loyal. Anniversary points create a yearly moment of appreciation tied to a date you already have.

## When to use

- You have customers who've been with you for at least a year.
- You want a yearly engagement moment that doesn't depend on collecting birthdays.
- You want to reward long-term customers without setting up VIP tiers.

## Overview

- Yearly bonus on the anniversary of the customer's account creation.
- Based on the date the customer account was created in Shopify.
- Optional points expiry and VIP tier multipliers.
- Available on select plans.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the way to earn">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In **Ways to earn**, click **Add another way** and pick **Anniversary**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Basic Details">
    - **Campaign Name** — shown to customers on the storefront. You can use `[earn_value]` and `[points_label]`, for example `Earn [earn_value] [points_label]`.
    - **Description** — short copy shown with the way to earn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the points">
    Enter **Points to earn on anniversary** (a whole number, at least 1).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Expiry (optional)">
    Turn on **Expiry**, then set **Expire after** and a **Period** (Day, Week, Month or Year). Expiry is available on select plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the side panel">
    - **Status** — on by default.
    - **Icon** — **Default**, or **Custom** to upload your own (JPG, PNG, GIF or WEBP, under 1 MB).
    - **Tiers** — turn on to apply VIP tier multipliers to this way to earn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- Yuko runs a daily check for customers whose account creation date (month and day) is today.
- Customers earn from their first anniversary onward; nothing is awarded in the year the account was created.
- Each customer earns once per year.
- Accounts created on 29 February are rewarded on 1 March in non-leap years.
- You can also send the [Anniversary Reminder](/emails/loyalty-emails) email and use the Anniversary Reminder trigger in [Shopify Flow](/integrations/shopify-flow).

With **Tiers** on and [VIP tiers](/vip-tiers/overview) enabled, customers in a tier earn the tier's multiplier for this way to earn, plus any bonus points the tier sets for it. The extra points appear as a separate tier bonus entry.

### Decision guidance

- **Turn on Tiers** so VIP customers get a bigger anniversary bonus.
- **Combine with an email** acknowledging the milestone ("3 years with us!").

### Limitations

- Based on the Shopify account creation date, not the first order date.
- No points for anniversaries that passed before you enabled it.
- Excluded customers don't earn.
- You can have one Anniversary way to earn.

## Examples

- An apparel brand awards 200 anniversary points with a 60-day expiry, alongside a thank-you email.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Send a thank-you email alongside.** The points are the gift; the email makes the moment.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What date is the anniversary based on?">
    The date the customer's account was created in Shopify.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will customers earn for past anniversaries?">
    No. Customers earn on their next anniversary after you enable it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
