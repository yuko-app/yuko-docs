## Introduction

Award points when customers share your store on X.

## What problem this solves

A small reward turns customers into a distribution channel during product launches and announcements.

## When to use

- Your audience is active on X.
- You're launching something worth tweeting about.

## Overview

- Points per share with optional cap.
- Pre-configurable tweet text and link.
- Optional points expiry.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Ways to Earn → Twitter Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set details and points">
    Enter campaign name, description and points value (e.g., `15`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure tweet text and link">
    Pre-fill what gets shared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure expiry (optional)">
    Set expiry if desired.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- The share action surfaces in the widget.
- Customer is taken to X with the pre-filled tweet.
- Points are credited on share intent.

### Decision guidance

- **Cap per cadence** to prevent spam.

### Limitations

- Verification is intent-based.

## Examples

- 15 points per share of a launch announcement, capped at 1 per customer per week.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can customers spam shares?">
