## Introduction

Award customers a one-time points bonus when they share a link to your store or a product on Twitter (X) from your loyalty widgets. You can pre-fill the post with your own message.

## What problem this solves

Word of mouth is a high-converting acquisition channel and hard to create on demand. A points reward plus a ready-made message makes sharing quick.

## When to use

- Your audience is active on X.
- You have a page worth sharing, such as a launch or a bestseller.
- You want to suggest the wording customers post.

## Overview

- One-time reward per customer.
- Configured with a **Share URL** and an optional **Share Message**.
- Points are credited when the customer clicks **Share**.
- Optional points expiry and VIP tier multipliers.
- Available on select plans.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the way to earn">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In **Ways to earn**, click **Add another way** and pick **Twitter Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Basic Details">
    - **Campaign Name** — shown to customers on the storefront. You can use `[earn_value]` and `[points_label]`, for example `Earn [earn_value] [points_label]`.
    - **Description** — short copy shown with the way to earn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the points">
    Enter **Points to earn** (a whole number, at least 1).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Share URL and message">
    - **Share URL** — the complete URL customers will share, for example `https://yourwebsite.com/product`. Required.
    - **Share Message** — optional text included with the post, for example *Check out this amazing product!*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Expiry (optional)">
    Turn on **Expiry**, then set **Expire after** and a **Period** (Day, Week, Month or Year). Expiry is available on select plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the side panel">
    - **Status** — on by default.
    - **Icon** — **Default**, or **Custom** to upload your own (JPG, PNG, GIF or WEBP, under 1 MB).
    - **Tiers** — turn on to apply VIP tier multipliers to this way to earn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. The way to earn appears in the **Ways to earn** list of your loyalty widgets (for example the [Floating Rewards](/onsite-widgets/floating-launcher) launcher, [rewards landing page](/onsite-widgets/rewards-landing-page) and [customer account page](/onsite-widgets/customer-account-page)) with a **Share** button.
2. A logged-in customer clicks **Share**. A new X post opens with your Share URL and Share Message filled in.
3. Yuko credits the points as soon as the customer clicks. Social networks don't let apps confirm the action, so earning is based on the click.
4. The way to earn then shows as completed for that customer.

With **Tiers** on and [VIP tiers](/vip-tiers/overview) enabled, customers in a tier earn the tier's multiplier for this way to earn, plus any bonus points the tier sets for it. The extra points appear as a separate tier bonus entry.

### Decision guidance

- **Write a message customers would actually post.** Short and specific beats salesy.
- **Keep the reward modest.** It's a quick one-time action.

### Limitations

- Earning is based on the click; X doesn't let apps confirm that the post was published.
- One reward per customer.
- Customers must be logged in to earn.
- You can have one Twitter Share way to earn.

## Examples

- 15 points for sharing a launch page, with the message *Just found my new favourite sneakers*.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Keep the Share Message short.** Leave room for the link and for customers to add their own words.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can customers edit the message before posting?">
    Yes. The message is only pre-filled; customers can change it before posting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can customers earn multiple times?">
    No. Twitter Share is a one-time reward per customer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is the share verified?">
    It isn't. Yuko credits points when the customer clicks **Share**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
