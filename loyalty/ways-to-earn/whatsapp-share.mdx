## Introduction

Award points when customers share your store via WhatsApp — typically the most personal and high-converting share channel.

## What problem this solves

WhatsApp shares carry strong intent because they go to a friend, not a feed. A points reward turns this into a measurable acquisition channel, especially in regions where WhatsApp is the primary messaging app.

## When to use

- Your customers are heavy WhatsApp users (LATAM, MENA, India, SEA).
- You want a high-conversion share channel.

## Overview

- Points per share with optional cadence cap.
- Pre-configurable message text and link.
- Optional points expiry.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Ways to Earn → WhatsApp Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set details and points">
    Enter campaign name and points value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure message text and link">
    Pre-fill the message that opens in WhatsApp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure expiry (optional)">
    Set expiry if desired.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- The share action surfaces in the widget.
- Clicking opens WhatsApp's share intent on the customer's device.
- Points are credited on share intent.

### Decision guidance

- **Run this in WhatsApp-heavy regions** for outsized impact.
- **Cap per cadence** to prevent abuse.

### Limitations

- WhatsApp's share intent works on devices with WhatsApp installed.
- Verification is intent-based.

## Examples

- 20 points per WhatsApp share for an Indian DTC brand, capped at 2 per week.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Pre-fill personal-feeling copy.** WhatsApp messages are personal; the default text should sound conversational.</Tip>
