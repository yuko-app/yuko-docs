<Note>
**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs → Ways to earn
</Note>

Ways to Earn (also called **campaigns**) are the rules that award points. Each type maps to one customer action, and you can have one way to earn of each type.

To add one, click **Add another way** in the **Ways to earn** section (or **Create campaign** if you have none yet) and pick a type. Types you've already added don't appear in the picker, and types that aren't on your plan are marked with an upgrade badge.

## Settings every way to earn shares

- **Campaign Name** and **Description** — shown to customers on the storefront. The name supports `[earn_value]` and `[points_label]` (Place an order also supports `[earn_from_amount]`).
- **Points** — a whole number of at least 1.
- **Expiry** — optional; set **Expire after** and a **Period** (Day, Week, Month or Year). Available on select plans.
- **Status** — turn the way to earn on or off. New ways to earn are on by default.
- **Icon** — **Default**, or **Custom** to upload your own (JPG, PNG, GIF or WEBP, under 1 MB).
- **Tiers** — apply [VIP tier](/vip-tiers/overview) multipliers and bonus points to this way to earn.

## How ways to earn work

1. Yuko listens for the customer action (order reaches your Earning Status, account created, consent recorded, widget button clicked, and so on).
2. It checks the customer isn't excluded from the program and hasn't already earned within the way to earn's limit.
3. It calculates the points and rounds them using the **Rounding Type** in [Loyalty Settings](/loyalty/program-settings).
4. If **Tiers** is on, it adds the tier's multiplier and bonus points as a separate tier bonus entry.
5. It credits the customer's balance (or holds the points first, for Place an order with a holding period) and records the activity.

How often a customer can earn depends on the type:

| Type | How often |
|---|---|
| Place an order | Once per order |
| Write a review | Once per product |
| Birthday, Anniversary | Once a year |
| Daily Visit | Once per Earning interval (Daily, Weekly or Monthly) |
| Visit URL | Once, or once per day, week or month (Frequency) |
| All other types | Once per customer |

## Managing ways to earn

- **Reorder** ways to earn by dragging them in the list.
- **Disable** a way to earn with its **Status** toggle to stop earning without losing its settings.
- A way to earn used as the earning action by a [booster campaign](/loyalty/booster-campaigns/overview) can't be disabled or deleted. Disable or archive those booster campaigns first.

## Decision guidance

- Start with **Place an order** and **Sign Up**. Those two cover the first account and the next purchase.
- Add **Birthday** and **Anniversary** for engagement; they're personal, infrequent and feel generous.
- Add **Write a review** if you collect reviews. It lifts submission rates.
- Add **Subscribe to Newsletter**, **SMS** or **WhatsApp** to grow your owned channels.
- Add **social** ways to earn only on networks where your customers actually spend time.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Don't overstuff the program.** Four or five well-tuned ways to earn beat fifteen that customers never notice.</Tip>
<Tip>**Use VIP tiers to reward top customers more.** Turn on **Tiers** instead of creating bigger rewards for everyone.</Tip>
<Tip>**Watch the activity feed** in [Customers](/loyalty/customers) for the first 30 days to see how customers actually earn.</Tip>
