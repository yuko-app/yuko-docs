## Introduction

The *Refer a Friend* earning rule lets you award points to the **referrer** when a friend completes a qualifying action through their referral link. It's the loyalty-side counterpart to the dedicated **Referral** module, where the referral program (link generation, fraud prevention, friend rewards) is configured end-to-end.

<Info>
Full referral program setup, dual-sided rewards, and fraud controls are documented in the **Referrals** section. This page focuses only on the points-earning side.
</Info>

## What problem this solves

Referred customers convert at a higher rate than paid traffic. Awarding points to referrers — not just to the friends — turns existing customers into a reliable acquisition channel.

## When to use

- You have at least a few hundred active customers willing to share.
- You want a points-based reward for referrers (alternative to discount-only programs).
- You want referral and loyalty to feel like one program, not two.

## Overview

- Award points to the referrer when a friend completes a qualifying action.
- Qualifying action defined in the Referrals module (typically *first order placed*).
- Stacks with referrer rewards configured in the Referrals module.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Ways to Earn → Refer a Friend**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set details and points">
    Enter campaign name and points value (e.g., `500`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure expiry (optional)">
    Set an expiry to push redemption.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. A customer shares their referral link from the Referrals widget.
2. A friend clicks through and completes the qualifying action.
3. Yuko awards the configured points to the referrer.
4. The activity entry links to the referral record.

### Decision guidance

- **Pair with a discount-based friend reward** — friends typically want a price break, referrers typically want points.
- **Calibrate the points** so the referrer reward feels worth sharing for (often 250–1000 points).

### Limitations

- Customers self-referring (same email, same address) are blocked by the Referrals module's fraud rules.
- Points are awarded after the friend's qualifying action, not on click.

## Examples

- 500 points to the referrer when the friend places their first order.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Make referring frictionless** — a single share link in the Customer Account hub plus the Referrals widget at checkout.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is this different from the Referral Program?">
    The Referral Program defines the friend reward, the link, and the fraud rules. This earning rule defines the referrer's points reward. Both work together.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
