</Note>

## Introduction

Award a milestone bonus when a customer crosses a configured spending threshold. Useful for celebrating progression without standing up full VIP tiers.

## What problem this solves

Flat per-order earning rewards every order equally. Spending milestones reward *cumulative* value — the customer who's spent $1,000 with you gets recognised differently than the customer at $50.

## When to use

- You're not ready to launch full VIP tiers but want milestone-style recognition.
- You want to gamify progression toward higher spend.
- You want to surprise-and-delight high spenders without committing to ongoing perks.

## Overview

- Single-threshold milestone (e.g., spend $500 lifetime → get 500 points).
- Optional period scoping: lifetime, rolling year, or calendar year.
- One-time per threshold per customer.
- Stackable with VIP tiers.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Ways to Earn → Reward Based on Spending**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set details">
    Name the campaign and enter the spend threshold (e.g., `$500`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the points bonus">
    Enter the points awarded when the threshold is crossed (e.g., `500`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the calculation period">
    Lifetime, rolling year or calendar year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- Yuko tracks the customer's cumulative spend in the chosen period.
- When spend crosses the threshold (after order paid), the bonus is awarded once.
- The threshold is sticky per customer per period.

### Decision guidance

- **Use lifetime** for permanent recognition.
- **Use rolling year** if you want milestones to feel renewable.
- **Run multiple campaigns** for layered milestones (e.g., $500, $1000, $2500).

### Limitations

- Refunds reduce cumulative spend; once awarded, the bonus is not clawed back unless the customer falls below the threshold within the same period.
- VIP tier multipliers do not stack on milestone bonuses by default.

## Examples

- *"Spend $500 lifetime, get 500 points"* (worth $5 at 100 pts = $1).
- *"Spend $1000 in a calendar year, get 1500 points"* renews each year.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Run 2–3 thresholds layered** to create a progression sense.</Tip>
<Tip>**Send a milestone email** when the bonus fires.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does this differ from VIP tiers?">
    Milestones are one-off bonuses. VIP tiers grant ongoing benefits (multipliers, perks). Use this for simple recognition; use VIP for sustained perks.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
