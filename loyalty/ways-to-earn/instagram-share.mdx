## Introduction

Award points when customers share your content or tag your store on Instagram.

## What problem this solves

Instagram tags from real customers are the cleanest UGC you can earn. A points reward makes them a reliable, repeatable input.

## When to use

- You're investing in Instagram-led brand building.
- You want UGC to feed your ads or social proof.

## Overview

- Points per share or per tag.
- Configurable hashtag or handle to tag.
- Optional cap per cadence.
- Optional points expiry.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Ways to Earn → Instagram Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set details and points">
    Enter campaign name, description and points value (e.g., `25`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add hashtag or handle">
    Specify what customers should tag for the share to count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure expiry (optional)">
    Set expiry on the bonus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & activate">
    Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- The share action appears in the loyalty widget.
- Customer is taken to Instagram with prefilled tag/copy.
- Points are credited on share intent.

### Decision guidance

- **Cap shares** to prevent spam.
- **Use a clear branded hashtag** so you can find the UGC later.

### Limitations

- Verification is intent-based.
- Instagram doesn't allow direct posting via URL on most browsers; the experience is share-link or copy-and-post.

## Examples

- 25 points per Instagram tag with `#YourBrandStyle`, capped at 4 per month.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Always include a hashtag** — it's the only way to actually find the UGC later.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I find shared posts?">
    Search Instagram for the hashtag you set in the campaign. Yuko does not pull posts back into the dashboard.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
