## Introduction

The **Customers** page is your day-to-day view of the loyalty program. It lists your store's customers with their loyalty status, points and tier, and opens a detail page for each one with their activity, rewards, orders and the actions you need for support.

<Tip>**Customers is a top-level item** in the Yuko sidebar, next to Loyalty, VIP, Referral and Membership. You don't need to be inside Loyalty to reach it.</Tip>

You can also see a customer's loyalty details and adjust their points without leaving Shopify, from the [Yuko Loyalty block](#in-shopify-admin) on the Shopify customer page.

## What problem this solves

When a customer asks *"where are my points?"* you need to answer quickly. The Customers page gives you the full history, and lets you fix balances or change a tier without touching anything else.

## When to use

- Answering support questions about missing or incorrect points.
- Giving a goodwill credit or removing points awarded in error.
- Placing a customer in a VIP tier manually.
- Removing a customer (for example a staff or test account) from the program.
- Moving point balances in from a previous loyalty app.

## Overview

- Searchable, sortable list with **Status**, **VIP Tier**, **Birthday**, **Membership**, point totals and **Referral Count**.
- Filters for status, points balance, total earned, total redeemed, VIP tier, membership and referral count.
- Customer detail page with **Activities**, **Active Rewards**, **Recent Orders**, a loyalty summary, VIP tier and referrals.
- **Adjust balance** to credit or debit points, with optional expiry on credits.
- **Assign tier** to override a customer's VIP tier.
- **Exclude from program**, optionally resetting the customer's points, discounts and tags.
- **Import customer points** from a CSV.
- A **Yuko Loyalty** block and an **Adjust balance** action on the Shopify admin customer page.

## The customer list

The list shows these columns: **Name** (with email), **Phone**, **Status**, **VIP Tier**, **Birthday**, **Membership**, **Point Balance**, **Point Earned**, **Point Redeemed** and **Referral Count**. VIP Tier, Membership and Referral Count appear only when those programs are enabled.

**Status** is one of:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Member** | A customer who has taken part in the program, for example by earning or redeeming points |
| **Guest** | A customer synced from your store who hasn't taken part in the program yet |
| **Excluded** | Excluded from the loyalty program with **Exclude from program** on the customer's page |

### Search, filter and sort

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the list">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Customers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type in the search box to search across customers, for example by name or email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter">
    Add any of these filters:
    - **Status** — All, Member, Guest or Excluded.
    - **Points Balance**, **Total Earned**, **Total Redeemed** — pick an operator (**Greater than or equal (≥)**, **Less than or equal (≤)** or **Equal (=)**) and enter a value.
    - **VIP Tier** and **Membership** — pick one or more.
    - **Referral Count** — operator and value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort">
    Sort by **Date Joined**, **Points Balance**, **Total Earned**, **Total Redeemed**, **Email** or **Referral Count**. The list is sorted by highest points balance first by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a customer">
    Click a row to open the customer's detail page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The customer detail page

The detail page shows the customer's name and status badge, and three main sections:

- **Activities** — every points credit, debit, redemption and tier change, newest first.
- **Active Rewards** — reward codes the customer has and hasn't used yet.
- **Recent Orders** — the customer's latest orders.

The side panel shows:

- **Overview** — the **Loyalty point balance**, with the **Adjust balance** button.
- **Loyalty summary** — **Points earned**, **Points spent**, **Points expired** and **Total orders**.
- **VIP tier** — the current tier (marked **Manual** if assigned by hand), **Tier multiplier**, **Next tier**, **Progress to next tier** and **Highest tier reached**, with **Assign tier** and **Clear override**.
- **Membership** — the customer's paid membership, if the Membership program is enabled.
- **Referrals** — referrals made, completed, who referred them, and their referral link.

From the page header you can also open **View on Shopify** and **Exclude from program**.

### Adjust balance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Adjust balance">
    On the customer's detail page, click **Adjust balance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Type">
    Pick **Credit (Add points)** or **Debit (Remove points)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Points and an Update reason">
    Enter the number of **Points** (a whole number, at least 1) and an optional **Update reason** for your team's records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Points Expiry (credits only, optional)">
    For a credit, set **Is expire enabled** to **Yes**, then choose **Expire after** and an **Expire period** (Day, Week, Month or Year). Points expiry is available on select plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Adjust points**. The balance updates straight away and the adjustment appears in **Activities**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A debit can't remove more points than the customer has. Excluded customers can't have their balance adjusted.

### Assign tier

Click **Assign tier**, then:

- **Tier** — the VIP tier to place the customer in.
- **Expires at (optional)** — leave empty for no expiry. This sets both the manual override expiry and the tier retention expiry.
- **Note (optional)** — the reason for the manual override.

The tier shows a **Manual** badge and, if set, **Override expires**. Click **Clear override** to return the customer to their earned tier. See [VIP Tiers](/vip-tiers/overview).

### Exclude from program

Click **Exclude from program** in the page header. In the confirmation:

- Leave **Reset all points, discounts, and tiers** unticked to exclude the customer without resetting their points, discounts or tags.
- Tick it to also reset their points and activities, remove their active discounts and clear their loyalty tags.

Click **Exclude**. The customer's status becomes **Excluded** and they stop earning. To bring them back, click **Add to program**.

## Import customer points

Use import to bring in balances from another loyalty app or a spreadsheet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the import">
    On the Customers page, click **Import customer points**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the customer sync">
    Yuko checks that your customers have finished syncing from your store. If not, click **Go to Integrations** and wait for the sync to complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your CSV">
    Drag in or browse to a CSV file of **500 KB or less**. The file needs these headers: `platform_customer_id`, `customer_email`, `phone`, `points`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the statistics">
    Yuko validates the file and shows **Import Statistics**: **Total Rows**, **Invalid Rows** and **Skipped Rows**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Continue to Import**, tick **I confirm and want to proceed with the import**, and click **Confirm & Import**. Customers who already exist are given the points. Customers who don't exist yet are added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>**Migrating from another loyalty app?** If you'd rather base starting balances on real Shopify order history, use [Backfill Points](/loyalty/backfill-points). It calculates points from past orders using your Place an order rule.</Tip>

## In Shopify admin

Yuko adds two extensions to the **customer page in your Shopify admin** (Shopify admin → Customers → a customer):

- **Yuko Loyalty block** — shows **Points balance**, **Points earned**, **Points redeemed**, **VIP tier** (when VIP tiers are enabled), **Referrals** and **Referral link**. When collapsed, it summarises the balance and tier. It has two buttons: **Adjust points balance** and **View in Yuko**, which opens the customer in the Yuko app.
- **Adjust balance action** — opens from the block's **Adjust points balance** button or from the customer page's **More actions** menu. Choose **Credit (Add points)** or **Debit (Remove points)**, enter **Points** and an optional **Update reason**, and click **Adjust points**.

Use these when your team works from Shopify's customer page, for example while handling an order or support request.

<Note>If you don't see the **Yuko Loyalty** block, check that it's added and visible in the app blocks on the Shopify customer page.</Note>

## How it works

- Customers sync from your Shopify store into Yuko. Every synced customer is listed, starting as a **Guest** until they take part in the program.
- Every adjustment and import is written to the customer's points ledger and appears in **Activities**.
- Adjustments are direct changes to the balance. They don't trigger Ways to Earn.
- Excluded customers stay on the list with the **Excluded** status but don't earn.
- Customers with a tag listed under **Exclude customers by tag** in [Loyalty Settings](/loyalty/program-settings) don't earn either, but they keep their **Member** or **Guest** status on this list.

### Decision guidance

- **Always add an Update reason** when adjusting points. It helps your team understand the history later.
- **Use import for one-off migrations.** For ongoing syncs from another system, use the [REST API](/integrations/rest-api).
- **Use Backfill Points instead of import** when you want balances to reflect actual order history.
- **Exclude rather than debit** staff or test accounts, so they stop earning altogether.

### Limitations

- There's no customer export from this page.
- Import files must be CSV, 500 KB or less, with the exact headers listed above.
- Import adds points; it doesn't overwrite existing balances.
- A debit can't take a balance below zero.

## Examples

- A support agent credits 200 points to a customer whose delivery was late, with the reason *Goodwill credit, delivery delay*.
- A store migrating from another app imports a CSV of 12,000 customers and their old balances.
- A merchant excludes staff accounts and ticks **Reset all points, discounts, and tiers** to clear what they'd earned.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Treat Activities as the source of truth.** It's the full record of what happened to a customer's points.</Tip>
<Tip>**Prefer manual tier assignment with an expiry** for temporary upgrades, such as a VIP trial, so customers return to their earned tier automatically.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does a customer show as Guest?">
    They're in your store's customer list but haven't taken part in the loyalty program yet. They become a **Member** once they take part, for example by earning or redeeming points.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I export the customer list?">
    Not from the Customers page. Use the [REST API](/integrations/rest-api) or an integration such as [Klaviyo](/integrations/klaviyo) to get loyalty data out of Yuko.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I adjust points from Shopify's customer page?">
    Yes. Use the **Adjust points balance** button on the **Yuko Loyalty** block, or the Adjust balance action in **More actions**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to customers in my CSV who aren't in Yuko yet?">
    They're added as new customers and given the points.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between Import and Backfill Points?">
    Import takes balances you supply in a CSV. [Backfill Points](/loyalty/backfill-points) calculates points from past Shopify orders using your Place an order rule, with no CSV needed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
