## Introduction

A POS Percentage Discount lets customers spend points in your physical store for a percentage off the sale (for example, `1,000 points = 10% off`). The cashier redeems it from the Yuko tile in Shopify POS.

## What problem this solves

In-store baskets vary a lot in size. A percentage discount scales with the sale, so customers making bigger in-store purchases get a bigger reward.

## When to use

- You sell in person with Shopify POS.
- Your in-store basket sizes vary widely.
- You already offer a percentage reward online and want the same in store.

## Overview

- Redeemable **only at Shopify POS**.
- One fixed exchange: **Points Required** for a **Percentage Discount** from 1% to 50%.
- Optional minimum purchase amount.
- Optional combinations with other discounts.
- No description, coupon prefix, maximum discount amount, product targeting or expiry settings.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the reward">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In **Ways to redeem**, click **Add another way** and pick **POS Percentage Discount** under **Shopify POS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the reward">
    Under **Basic Details**, set the **Reward Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the POS Reward Configuration">
    - **Points Required** — the points cost.
    - **Percentage Discount** — a whole number from 1 to 50.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Minimum Requirements (optional)">
    Choose **No minimum requirements** or **Minimum purchase amount** and enter a **Minimum Subtotal**. POS rewards don't support a minimum quantity of items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose combinations (optional)">
    Under **Combine with other discounts**, tick any of **Product discount**, **Order discount** and **Free shipping**. All three are ticked by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable and save">
    Turn on **Status** in the side panel and click **Save**. The reward shows a **POS** badge in the rewards list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. The cashier adds the customer and at least one product to the cart in Shopify POS.
2. They open the Yuko tile, go to the **Loyalty discounts** tab and tap **Redeem points**.
3. Under **Available rewards**, they tap **Redeem** on this reward and confirm.
4. Yuko deducts the points, creates a single-use percentage discount code for that customer and applies it to the cart.
5. If the cart is below the Minimum Subtotal, the reward stays disabled and POS shows how much more the customer needs to spend.

### Decision guidance

- **Match your online Percentage Discount** so customers get the same value in both channels.
- **Add a Minimum Subtotal** to keep percentage discounts for meaningful purchases.

### Limitations

- Only redeemable at Shopify POS.
- There's no Maximum Discount Amount for POS percentage rewards, so the percentage applies to the full eligible amount.
- Minimum requirement is subtotal only.
- Shopify applies only the best discount when discounts don't combine. POS warns the cashier before applying.

## Examples

- *Furniture showroom:* `2,000 pts = 10% off`, Minimum Subtotal $200.
- *Café:* `300 pts = 15% off`, no minimum, combines with product discounts.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Keep percentages modest.** Without a cap, a large in-store sale gets the full percentage off.</Tip>
<Tip>**Train cashiers to add the customer first.** Rewards only appear once a customer is on the cart.</Tip>

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can customers redeem this reward online?">
    No. POS rewards are only offered at Shopify POS.
