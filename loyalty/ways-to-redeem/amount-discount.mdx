## Introduction

Let customers spend points on a fixed-amount discount (for example, `500 points = $5 off`). It's the simplest, most predictable reward type and usually the one customers expect to see first.

## What problem this solves

Percentage discounts can feel abstract ("how much is 10% off?"). A flat amount is concrete: customers know exactly what they get before they redeem.

## When to use

- You want the simplest possible reward to launch the program.
- Your basket sizes are predictable, so a fixed amount maps cleanly to value.
- You want to discount only certain products or collections by a set amount.

## Overview

- Fixed amount off, in your store currency.
- **Fixed amount of points** (one price) or **Incremented points** (customers choose how many points to spend).
- Optional **Coupon Prefix**, **Purchase type** and product or collection targeting.
- Optional minimum purchase amount or minimum quantity of items.
- Optional combinations with other discounts and an optional expiry.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the reward">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In **Ways to redeem**, click **Add another way** and pick **Amount Discount** under **Online Store**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Basic Details">
    - **Reward Name** — customer-facing name. You can use `[reward_value]`, `[points_required]` and `[points_label]`, for example `[reward_value] off for [points_required] [points_label]`.
    - **Description** — short copy shown with the reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the Reward type">
    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Fixed amount of points">
        One price. Set **Points Required** and **Discount Amount**, for example 500 points for $5 off.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Incremented points">
        Customers choose how many points to spend, in steps.
        - **Point Increments** — points per discount unit (for example 100).
        - **Discount per Increment** — discount for each increment (for example $1).
        - **Minimum points to redeem** — the fewest points a customer can spend in one redemption.
        - **Maximum points to redeem** — the most points a customer can spend in one redemption. Leave it at 0 for no cap. If set, it must be at least the minimum.
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Reward Configuration">
    - **Coupon Prefix** — the start of every code this reward creates (up to 8 characters, default `yuko`). Codes look like `YUKO-AB1-C2D`.
    - **Purchase type** — **One-time purchase**, **Subscription** or **Both** (default). Subscription options only take effect if your store sells subscriptions; otherwise the discount applies to one-time purchases.
    - **Applies to specific products or collections** — leave unchecked to discount the whole order. When checked, choose **Specific products** or **Specific collections** and select at least one.
    - **Only apply discount once per order** — shown when you target specific products or collections, and checked by default. If unchecked, the amount is taken off each eligible item in the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Minimum Requirements (optional)">
    Under **Minimum purchase requirements**, choose **No minimum requirements**, **Minimum purchase amount** (enter a **Minimum Subtotal**) or **Minimum quantity of items** (enter a **Minimum Quantity**). Values must be at least 1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose combinations (optional)">
    Under **Combine with other discounts**, tick any of **Product discount**, **Order discount** and **Free shipping**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Expiry (optional)">
    Turn on **Expiry**, then set **Expire after** and a **Period** (Day, Week, Month or Year). The code stops working after that time. Expiry is available on select plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable and save">
    Turn on **Status** in the side panel and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. The customer opens a loyalty widget (for example the Cart Redeem widget or the customer account page) and redeems this reward. With incremented points, they also choose how many points to spend.
2. Yuko deducts the points and creates a Shopify discount code for that customer. The code can be used once.
3. The customer applies the code at checkout. Widgets that apply it to the cart do this for them.
4. Yuko logs the redemption in the customer's activity.

### Decision guidance

- **Start with a fixed amount of points.** It's the easiest to explain.
- **Switch to incremented points** when customers have large balances and want to spend more at once.
- **Set a minimum purchase** so the discount isn't used on a near-empty cart.
- **Leave combinations off** unless you deliberately want stacking with sales.

### Limitations

- The code is a Shopify discount, so Shopify's discount rules apply. If it doesn't combine with another discount in the cart, Shopify applies only one.
- The discount can't take an order below zero.

## Examples

- *Starter program:* fixed, `500 pts = $5 off`, Minimum Subtotal $25, expires after 30 days.
- *Flexible balance:* incremented, 100 points per $1, minimum 500 points, maximum 5,000 points.
- *Collection promo:* fixed, `300 pts = $3 off` items in a "Basics" collection, applied once per order.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Anchor your value clearly.** "500 pts = $5" is the easiest mental model. Avoid awkward ratios.</Tip>
<Tip>**Use a 30–60 day expiry** on the issued code to encourage prompt use.</Tip>
<Tip>**Use a recognisable Coupon Prefix** (for example `LOYAL`) so you can spot loyalty codes in Shopify reports.</Tip>
<Warning>Combining with site-wide percentage promos can stack in ways customers don't expect. Test combinations before launch.</Warning>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a customer use two amount discounts on one order?">
    Only if both discounts allow combining with an order discount in Shopify. Otherwise Shopify applies one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can someone else use the customer's code?">
    No. The code is restricted to the customer who redeemed it and can be used once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if the discount is larger than the cart?">
    Shopify caps the discount at the eligible amount. The customer doesn't get the difference back.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need Purchase type if I don't sell subscriptions?">
    No. Leave it on **Both**. Without subscriptions, the discount applies to one-time purchases.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
