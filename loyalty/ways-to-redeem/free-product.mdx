## Introduction

Let customers spend points on a specific product at no cost. It's useful for moving inventory, promoting a new launch, or giving loyal customers a tangible perk. Free Product is available on select plans.

## What problem this solves

A free product feels more generous than a discount of the same value. It also puts a specific item in customers' hands: a launch sample, a slow-moving product or a brand essential.

## When to use

- You're promoting a specific product or launch.
- You want a reward with high perceived value.
- You want to move slow-moving inventory through the loyalty program.

## Overview

- One product variant per reward. Create more Free Product rewards to offer more products.
- The reward is a discount code worth the selected variant's price, applied to that variant.
- Yuko adds the product to the cart when the code is applied, and removes it when the code is removed.
- Optional **Coupon Prefix**.
- Optional minimum purchase amount or minimum quantity of items.
- Optional combinations with other discounts and an optional expiry.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the reward">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In **Ways to redeem**, click **Add another way** and pick **Free Product** under **Online Store**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Basic Details">
    Set the **Reward Name** (shortcodes `[points_required]` and `[points_label]` are supported) and a **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the product">
    Under **Product Selection**, click **Search products** and choose the product in the Shopify picker. Pick the exact variant you want to give away. If you select a whole product, Yuko uses its first variant. A product is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Reward Configuration">
    - **Points Required** — the points cost.
    - **Coupon Prefix** — the start of every code (up to 8 characters, default `yuko`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Minimum Requirements (optional)">
    Choose **No minimum requirements**, **Minimum purchase amount** (**Minimum Subtotal**) or **Minimum quantity of items** (**Minimum Quantity**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose combinations (optional)">
    Under **Combine with other discounts**, tick any of **Product discount**, **Order discount** and **Free shipping**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Expiry (optional)">
    Turn on **Expiry** and set **Expire after** and a **Period** (Day, Week, Month or Year). Expiry is available on select plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an Instruction note (optional)">
    Under **Display Settings**, enter an **Instruction note** to show below the code in the customer's rewards, for example *Add the product to your cart and use this code at checkout to get it free.* Leave it empty to hide it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable and save">
    Turn on **Status** in the side panel and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. The customer redeems the reward from a loyalty widget.
2. Yuko deducts the points and creates a single-use Shopify discount code for that customer, worth the variant's current price and applied only to that variant.
3. In your storefront widgets, Yuko adds the product to the cart if it isn't there already, applies the code and copies it to the clipboard. The same happens when the customer clicks **Apply** on the reward later.
4. At checkout, the code makes that product free. Any minimum requirement must be met.

### Automatic add and remove

While a logged-in customer shops, Yuko keeps the free product in step with its code:

- **Code applied, product missing** — if the customer applies the code another way (for example in the cart's discount field), Yuko adds the product to the cart.
- **Code removed** — Yuko removes the free product it added.
- **Product removed by the customer** — Yuko doesn't add it back while the code stays applied.
- **Minimum requirement not met yet** — if the code doesn't apply to the cart, Yuko takes the product out again and tries once more after the customer adds items.

### Decision guidance

- **Set a minimum purchase** so the free product comes with a paid order.
- **Pair it with a launch announcement** so redeeming feels like a privilege.

### Limitations

- One product variant per reward.
- Shopify can't sell an out-of-stock product, so the customer can't check out with it until it's back in stock (unless you allow overselling).

## Examples

- *Sample drop:* 800 pts for a trial-size product, Minimum Subtotal $50, expires after 30 days.
- *Accessory add-on:* 1,500 pts for a tote bag, Minimum quantity of items 2.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Pick products that benefit from being tried.** Trials and discovery items turn into repeat purchases.</Tip>
<Tip>**Keep stock topped up.** A redeemed reward the customer can't use is worse than no reward.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens if the product goes out of stock after redemption?">
    The code stays valid until it expires, but Shopify won't let the customer buy an out-of-stock product. Restock it or help the customer directly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I offer several free products in one reward?">
    No. Create one Free Product reward per product.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did the free product appear in or disappear from the cart?">
    Yuko adds the free product when its reward code is applied and removes it when the code is removed. If the cart doesn't meet the reward's minimum requirement yet, the product is removed until the customer adds more items.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
