## Introduction

Let customers spend points to remove shipping costs from their order. It works especially well where shipping is a known reason carts are abandoned.

## What problem this solves

Many customers drop out of checkout when they see the shipping cost. A points-funded free-shipping reward turns your loyalty program into a direct fix for that.

## When to use

- Shipping is expensive relative to your average order value.
- You see a big drop-off between cart and checkout.
- You want a value reward that's easy to explain.

## Overview

- 100% off shipping.
- Optional **Shipping Restrictions**: limit it to selected countries or exclude expensive shipping rates.
- Optional **Coupon Prefix** and **Purchase type**.
- Optional minimum purchase amount or minimum quantity of items.
- Optional combinations with product and order discounts, and an optional expiry.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the reward">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs**. In **Ways to redeem**, click **Add another way** and pick **Free Shipping** under **Online Store**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Basic Details">
    Set the **Reward Name** (shortcodes `[points_required]` and `[points_label]` are supported) and a **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Reward Configuration">
    - **Points Required** — the points cost.
    - **Coupon Prefix** — the start of every code (up to 8 characters, default `yuko`).
    - **Purchase type** — **One-time purchase**, **Subscription** or **Both** (default). Subscription options only take effect if your store sells subscriptions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Shipping Restrictions (optional)">
    - **Countries** — **All countries** (default) or **Selected countries**. Selected countries must be within your store's shipping zones, and you must pick at least one.
    - **Shipping rates** — tick **Exclude shipping rates over a certain amount** and enter the maximum shipping rate. Rates above it aren't discounted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Minimum Requirements (optional)">
    Choose **No minimum requirements**, **Minimum purchase amount** (**Minimum Subtotal**) or **Minimum quantity of items** (**Minimum Quantity**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose combinations (optional)">
    Under **Combine with other discounts**, tick **Product discount** and/or **Order discount**. A free-shipping code can't combine with another shipping discount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Expiry (optional)">
    Turn on **Expiry** and set **Expire after** and a **Period** (Day, Week, Month or Year). Expiry is available on select plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable and save">
    Turn on **Status** in the side panel and click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. The customer redeems the reward from a loyalty widget.
2. Yuko deducts the points and creates a single-use Shopify free-shipping code for that customer.
3. At checkout, the code removes the shipping cost when the destination and shipping rate meet your restrictions and any minimum requirement is met.

### Decision guidance

- **Pair it with a minimum purchase** so you aren't shipping a very small order for free.
- **Use Selected countries** if you only want to absorb domestic or regional shipping.
- **Exclude expensive rates** so express or international upgrades aren't free.

### Limitations

- Selected countries must be in your Shopify shipping zones.
- If shipping is already free for the order, the code saves the customer nothing, but the points are still spent once they redeem.

## Examples

- *Domestic only:* 300 pts, Selected countries = United States, Minimum Subtotal $40, expires after 30 days.
- *Standard shipping only:* 500 pts, all countries, exclude shipping rates over $15.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Test checkout in each country you allow** before launch.</Tip>
<Tip>**Expect high redemption.** Free shipping is popular, so plan your points economy for it.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does it apply to every shipping method?">
    Yes, unless you tick **Exclude shipping rates over a certain amount**. Then rates above your maximum aren't discounted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I select a country?">
    Only countries within your store's shipping zones can be selected. Add the country to a shipping zone in Shopify first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can it combine with another free-shipping discount?">
    No. Yuko doesn't offer a shipping-discount combination for free-shipping codes. It can combine with product and order discounts if you allow it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
