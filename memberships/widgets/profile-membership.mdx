## Introduction

The **Profile: Membership** widget shows a customer's membership in their Shopify customer account and lets them manage it themselves. It's the **Yuko Profile membership management** customer account UI extension block, not a theme block.

## What problem this solves

Members want to know what they're paying for, when they renew, and how to change plans. A self-serve card answers those questions and handles plan changes without a support ticket.

## Where it appears

- The **Profile** page in Shopify customer accounts (`customer-account.profile.block.render`).
- The **Orders** page in Shopify customer accounts (`customer-account.order-index.block.render`).

## Overview

- Shows the current plan, price, renewal date and benefits.
- **View rewards** opens a modal with the rewards issued by the membership.
- **Manage** opens plan cards where the member can **Upgrade**, **Downgrade** (if allowed) or **Cancel**.
- Customers without a membership see the **No membership text**.
- Text fields only. The block uses Shopify's customer account styling.

## Before you start

<Check>At least one plan exists in **Membership → Plans**.</Check>
<Check>Your store uses Shopify's new customer accounts (**Settings → Customer accounts**).</Check>
<Check>To let members move to a lower plan, turn on **Show downgrade option to customers** in **Membership → Plans → Membership Settings**.</Check>

## Setup guide

### 1. Configure the widget in Yuko

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the widget">
    Go to **Membership → Widgets** and open **Profile: Membership**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage paid membership">
    | Field | Default |
    |---|---|
    | **Title** | `Your Membership` |
    | **No membership text** | `No active membership` |
    | **Subscription renews** | Use `[renewal_date]` for the membership renewal date, for example `Renews: [renewal_date]`. |
    | **Manage button** | `Manage` |
    | **Manage modal title** | `Manage membership` |
    | **Active badge label** | `Active` |
    | **Upgrade button** | `Upgrade` |
    | **Downgrade button** | `Downgrade` |
    | **Benefits list title** | `Benefits` |
    | **Benefits more label** | `more` (as in "+5 more") |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rewards">
    | Field | Default |
    |---|---|
    | **View rewards button** | `View rewards` |
    | **Rewards modal title** | `Your Rewards` |
    | **No rewards text** | `No active rewards found.` |
    | **Reward expires text** | `Expires on [discount_expiry_date]` |
    | **Load more button** | `Load more` |
    | **Cancel button** | `Cancel` |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pricing labels">
    **Monthly interval label** (`month`) and **Yearly interval label** (`year`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation and progress text">
    - **Cancel membership confirmation**: **Title** (`Cancel membership`), **Description** (`If you cancel your membership you will lose your benefits immediately. Canceling a membership cannot be undone.`), **Close button** (`Close`), **Cancel button** (`Cancel membership`).
    - **Upgrade membership confirmation**: **Title** (`Upgrade membership`), **Description** (`You are about to upgrade your membership.`).
    - **Downgrade membership confirmation**: **Title** (`Downgrade membership`), **Description** (`You are about to downgrade your membership.`).
    - **Action in progress**: **In progress text** (`Your membership change is being processed. This may take a few moments.`), shown while a purchase, cancellation, upgrade or downgrade is processed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and enable">
    Click **Save** and switch the widget on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. The block reads the customer's active membership. Without one, it shows the **No membership text**.
2. **Manage** lists your plans in the order set on the **Plans** tab:
   - Plans after the member's current plan show **Upgrade**.
   - Plans before it show **Downgrade**, but only if **Show downgrade option to customers** is on.
3. Upgrade and downgrade move the member's existing Shopify subscription to the new plan and its price.
4. **Cancel** cancels the subscription in Shopify. The membership tags are removed and benefits end.
5. While a change is processing, the block shows the **In progress text**.

### Limitations

- Works only with Shopify's new customer accounts.
- The look follows Shopify's customer account styling.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Order your plans from lowest to highest** on the Plans tab, so upgrade and downgrade labels make sense.</Tip>
<Tip>**Keep the cancel description honest.** It's the last thing a member reads before cancelling.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can members cancel on their own?">
    Yes. **Manage → Cancel** cancels their membership after a confirmation step. You can also cancel from **Membership → Members**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't members see a Downgrade option?">
    Turn on **Show downgrade option to customers** in **Membership → Plans → Membership Settings**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
