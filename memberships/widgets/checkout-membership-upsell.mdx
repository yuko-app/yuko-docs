## Introduction

The **Checkout: Membership upsell** widget shows your membership plan inside Shopify Checkout. It's the **Yuko Checkout Membership Upsell** checkout UI extension block.

## What problem this solves

If a customer didn't add the membership in the cart, checkout is your last chance. The block lets them add the plan to the order in one click.

## Where it appears

- Shopify Checkout (`purchase.checkout.block.render`). By default it sits in the order summary; you can move it in the checkout editor.

## Overview

- Promotes the plan chosen in **Membership → Plans → Membership Settings → Membership to upsell**.
- Text fields only. The block uses Shopify's checkout styling.
- Hidden from customers who already have a membership.
- Requires Shopify Plus. On other Shopify plans, the widget card shows a **Shopify Plus** badge and can't be turned on.

## Before you start

<Check>Your store is on Shopify Plus.</Check>
<Check>A plan is selected in **Membership to upsell**.</Check>

## Setup guide

### 1. Configure the widget in Yuko

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the widget">
    Go to **Membership → Widgets** and click **Customize** on **Checkout: Membership upsell**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the text">
    | Field | Default |
    |---|---|
    | **Title** | `Become a member and save` |
    | **Description** | `Save on all your future orders by becoming a member` |
    | **Shipping upsell description** | `Get FREE shipping on this and future orders by becoming a member` |
    | **Add button** | `Add` |
    | **Added button** | `Added` |
    | **View benefits button** | `View benefits` |
    | **Monthly interval label** | `month` |
    | **Yearly interval label** | `year` |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and enable">
    Click **Save**, then switch the widget on with the toggle on its card in **Membership → Widgets**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 2. Add the block in the checkout editor

Choose the **Yuko Checkout Membership Upsell** block.

### 3. Verify

Go through a test checkout as a customer without a membership. The block should show the plan and an **Add** button.

## How it works

1. Shopify renders the block in checkout.
2. The block checks that memberships and the widget are on, and that the customer has no membership.
3. It shows the upsell plan with its price, billing interval, and a **View benefits** button that expands the plan's benefits.
4. **Add** adds the plan's product, with its subscription option, to the order. The button then shows the **Added** label.

### Limitations

- Requires Shopify Plus.
- Promotes one plan: the **Membership to upsell**.
- Visual styling follows Shopify's checkout.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Use it alongside the cart upsell** as a second, lighter touchpoint.</Tip>
<Tip>**Test in the checkout editor preview and in a real checkout.** Shopify can cache checkout settings for a short time.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My checkout doesn't show the block">
    Check that your store is on Shopify Plus, the widget is switched on in Yuko, the block is added in the checkout editor, and you're testing as a customer without a membership.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if the customer is already a member?">
    The block hides itself.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
