</Note>

## Introduction

The **Cart membership upsell** widget shows a membership offer in the cart drawer or on the cart page, so customers can add the plan to their cart before checkout.

## What problem this solves

The cart is the highest-intent moment on your storefront. Showing the membership offer there turns one-time buyers into members just before they pay.

## Where it appears

- The cart drawer and the cart page.
- It's placed automatically through the **Yuko App** embed. You can also place it yourself with the **Yuko: Member Cart Upsell** theme app block, or point it at a specific element with a CSS selector.

## Overview

- Promotes the plan chosen in **Membership → Plans → Membership Settings → Membership to upsell**.
- **Configuration** and **Styles** tabs.
- Hidden from customers who already have a membership, and when the cart is empty.

## Before you start

<Check>At least one plan exists in **Membership → Plans**.</Check>
<Check>A plan is selected in **Membership to upsell** (in **Membership Settings** on the Plans tab).</Check>
<Check>The **Yuko App** embed is turned on in your theme. See [Theme App Embed](/installation-and-setup/theme-app-embed).</Check>

## Setup guide

### 1. Configure the widget in Yuko

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the widget">
    Go to **Membership → Widgets** and open **Cart membership upsell**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the text">
    | Field | Default |
    |---|---|
    | **Title** | `Save with membership` |
    | **Description** | `Save on all your future orders by becoming a member` |
    | **Monthly interval label** | `month` (as in "$9.99/month") |
    | **Yearly interval label** | `year` (as in "$99/year") |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the placement (optional)">
    Under **App embed placement**, enter a **CSS Selector** for the element the upsell should appear above, for example `.cart__footer`. Leave it empty to use the default placement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Style it">
    On the **Styles** tab, set **Border color**, **Background color**, **Title text color**, **Description text color**, **Padding** and optional **Custom CSS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and enable">
    Click **Save** and switch the widget on. If the Yuko App embed isn't on yet, click **Enable Theme Extension** to open the theme editor with the embed ready to turn on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 2. Place the block manually (optional)

To control exactly where the upsell appears, add the theme app block:

<SetupThemeBlock />

Open the **Cart** template and add **Yuko: Member Cart Upsell**. The block has no theme-editor settings; text and styles come from Yuko.

### 3. Verify

As a logged-out customer (or one without a membership), add a product to the cart and open the cart. The upsell should appear with your plan's price.

## How it works

1. When the cart changes, the Yuko App embed checks that memberships and the widget are on, the customer has no membership, a plan is set to upsell, and the cart isn't empty.
2. If all checks pass, the widget shows the upsell plan with its price and billing interval.
3. The customer adds the plan to the cart and becomes a member once they complete checkout.

### Limitations

- The widget promotes one plan: the **Membership to upsell**.
- Customers have to check out with the plan to start their membership.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Quote a concrete saving in the description**, for example "Members save 10% on every order".</Tip>
<Tip>**Test in your cart drawer on mobile.** Space is tight there, so use the CSS selector if the default spot doesn't work.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What if the customer is already a member?">
    The widget hides itself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I choose which plan is shown?">
    Set **Membership to upsell** in **Membership → Plans → Membership Settings**. The same plan is used by the checkout upsell, the rewards landing page and the Floating Rewards launcher.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The upsell appears in the wrong place">
    Enter a **CSS Selector** under **App embed placement**, or add the **Yuko: Member Cart Upsell** block exactly where you want it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
