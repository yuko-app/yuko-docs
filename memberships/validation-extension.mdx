---
title: Membership Validation at Checkout
description: A Shopify checkout rule that stops customers who already have an active membership from buying another one.
---

<Note>
**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Settings → Checkout → Checkout rules. This isn't a tab in the Yuko admin; it's a Shopify Function (**yuko-membership-validation**) that ships with the Yuko app.
</Note>

## Introduction

Membership Validation is a cart and checkout validation function. When the cart contains a membership product, it checks whether the customer already has an active membership. If they do, checkout is blocked with the message **"You already have an active membership"**.

## What problem this solves

Without it, an existing member could buy a membership product again and end up with two paid subscriptions. Members who want a different plan should switch plans from their customer account instead (see [Profile Membership](/memberships/widgets/profile-membership)).

## Where it appears

- Shopify checkout. The error only shows when the rule blocks the purchase.

## When to use

- You sell more than one plan, or you want to make sure no customer pays for two memberships at once.

## Overview

- Runs on every cart and checkout validation.
- Looks for products tagged `YUKO:Membership:Product`. Yuko adds this tag to each plan's linked product.
- Checks whether the customer has the `YUKO:Membership` tag. Yuko adds it to customers with an active membership and removes it when they no longer have one.
- There's nothing to configure in Yuko; the message is fixed.

## Before you start

<Check>At least one membership plan exists in **Membership → Plans**, so its linked product carries the `YUKO:Membership:Product` tag.</Check>

## Setup guide

### 1. Turn on the checkout rule in Shopify

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Checkout settings">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Settings → Checkout**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the rule">
    In **Checkout rules**, click **Add rule** and choose **yuko-membership-validation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Turn the rule on and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 2. Verify

<Steps>
  <Step title="Test as an existing member">
    Log in as a customer with an active membership, add a membership product to the cart and go to checkout. You should see **"You already have an active membership"** and be unable to complete the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test as a non-member">
    Repeat as a customer without a membership. Checkout should proceed normally.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

1. The rule reads the cart lines and the customer's tags.
2. If no line is a product tagged `YUKO:Membership:Product`, checkout continues.
3. If there is one and the customer has the `YUKO:Membership` tag, the rule returns an error and checkout is blocked.

### Limitations

- It only checks for an existing active membership. It doesn't restrict regular products to members.
- It relies on Shopify customer and product tags. Don't remove the `YUKO:Membership` or `YUKO:Membership:Product` tags manually.
- Guest checkouts without an identified customer can't be matched to a membership.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Turn on the profile widget too.** Members who want a different plan can upgrade or downgrade from their account instead of buying again.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use this to make products members-only?">
    No. The rule only stops existing members from buying a second membership.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the error message?">
    No. The message is set in the function.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
