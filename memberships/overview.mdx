## What is the Memberships module

Memberships let you sell paid plans. Customers pay a monthly or yearly fee and get benefits in return, such as discounts, free shipping, bonus points, store credit or a custom perk.

Memberships are different from [VIP tiers](/vip-tiers/overview), which customers *earn* through activity rather than buy. Memberships are a separate recurring revenue line tied to your most committed customers.

Memberships are available on select plans.

## Who it's for

- Brands with repeat purchases that would benefit from a paid membership layer.
- DTC brands that want a paid plan above their free loyalty program.
- Stores that want predictable recurring revenue from their best customers.

## The Membership menu

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Plans** | Turn memberships on, create and order plans, add members, and set **Membership Settings**. See [Membership Plans](/memberships/plans). |
| **Members** | See every customer's membership, change its status and next billing date, and review benefits. See [Members](/memberships/contracts). |
| **Widgets** | Configure the cart, checkout, customer profile and product-page widgets. See [Cart Membership Upsell](/memberships/widgets/cart-membership-upsell). |
| **Emails** | Edit the membership emails (created, upgraded, downgraded, cancelled). See [Membership emails](/emails/membership-emails). |

## How it fits together

```
Plan (Membership → Plans)
  │  Yuko creates a Shopify selling plan and a linked membership product
  ▼
Customer buys the product with the subscription option, or you add them manually
  │
  ▼
Shopify subscription contract → Member (Membership → Members)
  │  Yuko tags the customer and schedules the plan's benefits
  ▼
Benefits issued · Status shown in the profile widget · Renewals billed by Shopify
```

## Get started

The **Quick Start** card on the Plans tab walks you through these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a membership plan">
    Add a plan with a price, billing interval and at least one benefit. See [Membership Plans](/memberships/plans).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the cart upsell widget">
    Promote your plan in the cart. See [Cart Membership Upsell](/memberships/widgets/cart-membership-upsell).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the profile widget">
    Let members view and manage their membership from their customer account. See [Profile Membership](/memberships/widgets/profile-membership).
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also add the [Purchase Options](/memberships/widgets/purchase-options) widget to product pages, and turn on [Membership Validation](/memberships/validation-extension) to stop customers buying a second membership.
