<Note>
**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Membership → Members
</Note>

## Introduction

The **Members** tab lists every membership: one row per customer per plan, backed by a Shopify subscription contract. Each membership has its own status, billing frequency and next renewal date.

## What problem this solves

You need one place to see who is subscribed to what, and to act on individual memberships when customers contact support.

## When to use

- Answering a customer's question about their membership.
- Pausing, cancelling or reactivating a membership.
- Moving a member's next billing date.
- Checking which benefits a member has received or has coming up.

## Overview

- Status tabs: **All**, **Active**, **Paused**, **Cancelled**, **Expired**, **Failed**.
- Columns: **Membership**, **Customer**, **Billing frequency**, **Next renewal**, **Created at** and **Status**.
- **Search memberships** by customer, membership number or product, and sort by membership, customer, status or created date.
- A detail page per membership with plan details, subscriber details, **Earned benefits** and **Scheduled benefits**.

## Before you start

<Check>At least one plan exists in **Membership → Plans**. See [Membership Plans](/memberships/plans).</Check>

## Setup guide

### Find a membership

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    Go to **Membership → Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter and search">
    Pick a status tab, or type in **Search memberships**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the detail page">
    Click a row to open **Membership #&lt;id&gt;**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Read the detail page

- **Membership details**: **Plan name**, **Billing frequency**, **Price**, **Product**, **Next renewal date**, **Created at** and the originating **Order**.
- **Subscriber**: customer name, **Email** and **Phone**.
- **Earned benefits**: benefits already issued, with **Benefit**, **Value**, **Reward** and **Date**.
- **Scheduled benefits**: upcoming benefits, with **Benefit**, **Value** and **Scheduled at**. Recurring benefits are marked **Recurring**.

### Pause, cancel or activate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the status menu">
    On the Members list, click the status badge of the membership.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an action">
    - **Active** memberships offer **Pause** and **Cancel**.
    - **Paused** memberships offer **Activate** and **Cancel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm in the dialog. Yuko updates the subscription contract in Shopify.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Cancelled, expired and failed memberships have no actions.

### Change the next billing date

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the membership">
    Open an **Active** membership's detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Change">
    Next to **Next renewal date**, click **Change**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a date">
    Choose a **Next billing date** (tomorrow at the earliest) and click **Continue**. Future cycles are calculated from this date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Review the old and new dates and click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
The customer is charged the full membership price on the new billing date. No proration is applied.
</Warning>

## How it works

- A membership is created when a customer buys the plan's linked product with the subscription option, or when you add them from the plan page.
- Shopify bills each renewal. Yuko keeps the status and next renewal date in sync.
- When a membership becomes active, Yuko tags the customer and schedules the plan's benefits.
- When a membership ends, Yuko removes the plan's customer tag, and removes the general `YUKO:Membership` tag if the customer has no other active membership.

### Limitations

- Status changes are limited to Pause, Activate and Cancel. A cancelled membership can't be reactivated; the customer has to join again.
- Refunds aren't issued from Yuko. Refund the order in Shopify if needed.
- Search runs on the memberships loaded in the list.

## Examples

- A customer travelling for two months asks to stop payments: **Pause** the membership, then **Activate** it when they're back.
- A customer wants their billing to fall after payday: **Change** the next billing date to the 1st of the month.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Pause before you cancel.** If a customer wants a break, pausing keeps their membership so they don't have to sign up again.</Tip>
<Tip>**Tell customers about billing-date changes.** They're charged the full price on the new date.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can customers manage their own membership?">
    Yes. With the [Profile Membership](/memberships/widgets/profile-membership) widget, members can upgrade, cancel and (if you allow it) downgrade from their customer account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a customer rejoin after cancelling?">
    Yes. They buy the plan again, which creates a new membership.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I issue refunds?">
    Refund the order in Shopify.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
