## Introduction

A membership plan defines what members pay and what they get. When you save a plan, Yuko creates it in Shopify as a subscription selling plan with a linked membership product, so Shopify handles checkout, billing and renewals.

## What problem this solves

Without a plan structure, you'd manage perks customer by customer. Plans standardise pricing, billing and benefits, and Yuko issues the benefits automatically to every member.

## When to use

- When you launch paid memberships.
- When you add another plan (for example Silver and Gold).
- When you change a plan's price or benefits.

## Overview

The **Plans** tab has these sections:

- **Membership Status:** turns the whole membership program on or off. Turning it off asks you to confirm; customers can't buy or manage memberships until you turn it back on.
- **Quick Start:** setup checklist.
- **Paid Memberships:** your plans. Click **Add Membership** to create one. Drag plans to reorder them; the order decides what counts as an upgrade or a downgrade (see [Membership Settings](#membership-settings)).
- **Membership Settings:** **Show downgrade option to customers** and **Membership to upsell**.

## Setup guide

### 1. Create a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan editor">
    Go to **Membership → Plans** and click **Add Membership**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in Membership Details">
    - **Membership name** (required), for example `Premium Membership`.
    - **Merchant Code**: an optional internal code saved on the plan's Shopify selling plan group.
    - **Description**: shown to customers in membership widgets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the price and billing interval">
    - **Membership price**: enter the **Amount per month** or **Amount per year** charged each billing period.
    - **Billing interval**: **Monthly** or **Yearly**. You can't change the interval after the plan is created.

    The **Summary** card shows the amount per month or year and the number of benefits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add benefits">
    Click **Add Benefits** and pick a benefit type. Each plan needs at least one benefit. See [Benefit types](#benefit-types).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Yuko creates the Shopify selling plan and the linked membership product.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After saving, the plan editor also shows:

- **Membership Identifier**: the plan's read-only customer tag, in the form `YUKO:Membership:<identifier>`, with a copy button. Yuko adds this tag to members' Shopify customer records, plus the general `YUKO:Membership` tag. Use it to reference the plan in segments and integrations.
- **Membership Status**: switch the plan **Active** or **Inactive**. You can't make a plan inactive while it has active members.
- **Linked product**: the Shopify product customers buy to join. Yuko tags it `YUKO:Membership:Product`.

### 2. Add members manually (optional)

You can enrol customers without them buying the product. Manually added memberships are free: Yuko creates a zero-price subscription contract that tracks the billing cycle but never charges the customer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan">
    Click a plan in **Paid Memberships**. The plan must be **Active**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add one customer">
    In the **Add Member** card, click **Add**, search for a customer and click **Add member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or add by tag">
    Click **Add by tag**, enter a **Customer tag** (or click **Browse** to pick one) and click **Create memberships**. Yuko queues every customer with that tag. Check the **Members** tab for the results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A customer who already has an active or paused membership on the plan isn't added again.

### 3. Set Membership Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Show downgrade option to customers">
    Turn this on to let members move to a lower plan from their customer account. When it's off, members can only upgrade or cancel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Membership to upsell">
    Pick the plan to promote in the cart upsell, checkout upsell, rewards landing page and Floating Rewards launcher widgets.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Benefit types

| Benefit | What members get | Key settings |
|---|---|---|
| **Amount discount** | A fixed amount off. | **Method** (**Automatic discount** or **One-time discount code**), **Discount value**, **Applies to** (all products, specific collections or specific products), **Only apply discount once per order**, **Purchase type**, **Minimum purchase requirements**, **Combine with other discounts**, **Coupon Prefix** (one-time codes only), **Expiry Settings**. |
| **% off discount** | A percentage off. | As Amount discount, without the once-per-order option. The **Discount value** can be up to 50%. |
| **Free shipping** | Free shipping. | **Method** (**Automatic** or **One-time code**), **Purchase type**, minimum requirements, combinations, prefix and expiry. |
| **Free product** | A chosen product for free. | **Method**, **Select Product**, minimum requirements, combinations, prefix and expiry. |
| **Bonus points** | Loyalty points. | **Name**, **Number of points**, optional expiry (**Expire after**). |
| **Store credit** | Shopify store credit. | **Store Credit Type**: **One time**, **Recurring** (with a **Recurring Period** and schedule) or **% cashback after every purchase**. Optional expiry and **Enable Holdings**. Only one store credit benefit per plan, and only on Shopify stores. |
| **Custom benefit** | A perk you deliver yourself, such as early access. | **Name**. It's shown to members but Yuko doesn't issue anything. |

## How it works

1. Saving a plan creates a Shopify selling plan group, a customer segment and a linked membership product.
2. A customer buys the product with the subscription option (or you add them manually). Shopify creates a subscription contract, which appears on the **Members** tab.
3. Yuko tags the customer and schedules the plan's benefits. Scheduled benefits are issued in the background; custom benefits are informational only.
4. Shopify bills renewals at the plan's interval.

### Limitations

- The billing interval is **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
- You can't delete a plan, or make it inactive, while it has active members.
- Members can only be added to an **Active** plan.

## Examples

- *Gold*: $9.99/month with **Free shipping** (automatic) and **% off discount** of 10% (automatic).
- *Annual Club*: $79/year with **Bonus points** (500) and a recurring monthly **Store credit**.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Lead with the most valuable benefit.** Customers scan, and the first benefit gets the click.</Tip>
<Tip>**Order plans from lowest to highest.** The order on the Plans tab decides which moves count as upgrades and downgrades in the profile widget.</Tip>
<Tip>**Test the purchase as a real customer** before launching.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I have more than one plan?">
    Yes. Create a plan for each level and drag them into order.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do manually added members pay?">
    No. Members added with **Add Member** or **Add by tag** get a zero-price contract and are never charged.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if a renewal payment fails?">
    Billing and retries are handled by Shopify. The Members tab reflects the subscription contract's status, so a contract Shopify marks as failed shows as **Failed**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
