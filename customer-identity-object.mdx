---
title: "Customer Object"
description: "Fields returned for a customer by the Yuko REST API, and how customers are identified."
---

Yuko creates customer records automatically from your Shopify store. Each customer is linked to their store account by `platform` and `platform_customer_id`. The customer object is returned by [List customers](/developer/api/list-customers) and [Retrieve a customer](/developer/api/retrieve-a-customer), which require the `read:customers` scope.

<Note>
Whenever an endpoint asks for `customer_id`, pass the customer's ID from your store (the `platform_customer_id` below, for example the Shopify customer ID), not the Yuko `id`.
</Note>

## Properties

<ResponseField name="id" type="string<uuid>">
  Yuko's internal UUID for the customer.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="first_name" type="string | null">
  The customer's first name.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="last_name" type="string | null">
  The customer's last name.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="email" type="string | null">
  The customer's email address.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="phone" type="string | null">
  The customer's phone number.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="platform" type="string">
  The store platform the customer comes from, for example `shopify`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="platform_customer_id" type="string | null">
  The customer's ID in your store. Use this value as `customer_id` in API requests.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="state" type="enum<string>">
  `member` for customers in the loyalty program, or `disabled` for customers you've banned from it. Customers excluded only by a customer tag still show `member`. One of `member`, `disabled`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="date_of_birth" type="string<date> | null">
  The customer's birthday in `YYYY-MM-DD` format, if known.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="referral_code" type="string | null">
  The customer's referral code, if they have one.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="referral_url" type="string | null">
  The customer's referral link, if they have a referral code.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="points" type="object">
  The customer's points summary.

  <Expandable title="points properties">
    <ResponseField name="balance" type="integer">
      Spendable points. Always `0` for a banned customer or one excluded by a customer tag.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="earned" type="integer">
      Total points earned.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="redeemed" type="integer">
      Total points redeemed.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="tier" type="object | null">
  Returned by Retrieve a customer only. The customer's current VIP tier, with the same fields as `current_tier` in the [Membership Object](/membership-object). `null` if VIP tiers are turned off or the customer hasn't reached a tier.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="membership" type="object">
  Returned by Retrieve a customer only. The customer's loyalty membership summary.

  <Expandable title="membership properties">
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">
      Always `active`.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="member_since" type="string<date-time>">
      The date the customer was added to Yuko.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="string<date-time>">
  When the customer was added to Yuko (ISO 8601).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="updated_at" type="string<date-time>">
  When the customer record was last updated (ISO 8601).
</ResponseField>
