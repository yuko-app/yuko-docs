---
title: "Customer Object"
description: "Fields returned for a customer by the Yuko REST API, and how customers are identified."
---

Yuko creates customer records automatically from your Shopify store. Each customer is linked to their store account by `platform` and `platform_customer_id`. The customer object is returned by [List customers](/developer/api/list-customers) and [Retrieve a customer](/developer/api/retrieve-a-customer) (scope `customers` read).

<Note>
Whenever an endpoint asks for `customer_id`, pass the customer's ID from your store (the `platform_customer_id` below, for example the Shopify customer ID), not the Yuko `id`.
</Note>

**id**

**string\<uuid\>**

Yuko's internal UUID for the customer.

Example:

`"63a2c89a-9936-4bc0-9d5f-93db46110b76"`

**first_name**

**string | null**

The customer's first name.

Example:

`"Jane"`

**last_name**

**string | null**

The customer's last name.

Example:

`"Doe"`

**email**

**string | null**

The customer's email address.

Example:

`"jane@doe.com"`

**phone**

**string | null**

The customer's phone number.

Example:

`"+15555550123"`

**platform**

**string**

The store platform the customer comes from.

Example:

`"shopify"`

**platform_customer_id**

**string**

The customer's ID in your store. Use this value as `customer_id` in API requests.

Example:

`"7012345678901"`

**state**

**enum\<string\>**

`member` for customers in the loyalty program, or `disabled` for customers you've excluded from it.

Available options:

`member`,

`disabled`

Example:

`"member"`

**date_of_birth**

**string\<date\> | null**

The customer's birthday, if known (`YYYY-MM-DD`).

Example:

`"1990-04-21"`

**referral_code**

**string | null**

The customer's referral code, if they have one.

Example:

`"JANE10"`

**referral_url**

**string | null**

The customer's referral link, if they have a referral code.

Example:

`"https://yourstore.com?ref=JANE10"`

**points**

**object**

The customer's points summary: `balance` (spendable points, always `0` for a `disabled` customer), `earned` (total earned) and `redeemed` (total redeemed). All integers.

Example:

`{ "balance": 650, "earned": 1200, "redeemed": 550 }`

**tier** (Retrieve a customer only)

**object | null**

The customer's current VIP tier, with the same fields as `current_tier` in the [Membership Object](/membership-object). `null` if VIP tiers are turned off or the customer hasn't reached a tier.

**membership** (Retrieve a customer only)

**object**

`status` (always `active`) and `member_since` (the date the customer was added to Yuko).

**created_at**

**string\<date-time\>**

When the customer was added to Yuko (ISO 8601).

Example:

`"2026-03-09T14:02:06+00:00"`

**updated_at**

**string\<date-time\>**

When the customer record was last updated (ISO 8601).

Example:

`"2026-03-09T14:02:06+00:00"`
