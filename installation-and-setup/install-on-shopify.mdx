## Introduction

Yuko is a public Shopify app. Install it from the Shopify App Store, approve its permissions, and it opens inside your Shopify admin under **Apps → Yuko Loyalty**.

## What problem this solves

Yuko installs through Shopify's standard app flow. Storefront widgets load through a theme app embed and Shopify extensions, so you don't need to edit theme code.

## When to use

- You're setting up Yuko for the first time.
- You're reinstalling Yuko after uninstalling it.

## Before you start

<Check>You're the store owner, or a staff member with permission to install apps.</Check>

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Yuko App Store listing">
    Visit [apps.shopify.com/yuko](https://apps.shopify.com/yuko) and click **Install**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve permissions">
    Shopify lists the access Yuko needs, including customers, orders, products, discounts, themes and store credit. Review it and click **Install**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your contact details">
    Yuko opens on **Set up your loyalty rewards**. Enter your **Contact email** (and optionally a **Phone number**) and click **Get Started**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the Setup Guide">
    On the **Dashboard**, the Setup Guide walks you through enabling the Yuko theme app embed. In **Loyalty → Programs**, the **Quick Start** checklist covers the steps to get your loyalty program running.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the Yuko App embed">
    Turn on the **Yuko App** embed in your theme so storefront widgets can load. See [Theme App Embed](/installation-and-setup/theme-app-embed).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

When you install Yuko, Shopify grants it access to your store. Yuko uses that access to:

- Read orders and customers so it can award points.
- Create discount codes and store credit when customers redeem rewards.
- Listen for Shopify events such as new orders, fulfillments, refunds and cancellations.
- Make its theme app embed, theme blocks, checkout blocks and customer account extensions available in your Shopify editors.

### Limitations

- Headless storefronts (Hydrogen or custom) can't use the theme app embed or theme blocks. Use the [REST API](/integrations/rest-api) instead.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Open Yuko from your Shopify admin.** Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty**. The app runs inside Shopify.</Tip>
<Tip>**Don't uninstall to start over.** To clear test data, use **Reset customer activities** in [Loyalty → Settings](/loyalty/program-settings).</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I don't approve the permissions?">
    Shopify cancels the install. Start again from the App Store listing and approve the permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to edit my theme code?">
    No. Turn on the **Yuko App** embed and add Yuko blocks in the Theme Editor.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I uninstall?">
    Open **Shopify admin → Settings → Apps**, find Yuko, and click **Uninstall**. This removes Yuko's access to your store.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
