<Note>
**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Dashboard
</Note>

## Introduction

Run through this checklist after installing Yuko, and again after any big change such as publishing a new theme or switching to new customer accounts. Each check takes a moment and catches the most common reasons a widget doesn't show.

## What problem this solves

Most "widget not showing" problems come from one of three things: the **Yuko App** embed is off on the published theme, the widget is switched off in Yuko, or the block was never added in the Shopify editor. This checklist covers all three.

## When to use

- Right after installation.
- After publishing a new theme.
- After turning on new customer accounts or adding checkout blocks.
- Before launching a big loyalty campaign.

## Verification checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the loyalty program is enabled">
    Open **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Programs** and check that **Loyalty Program Status** shows the program as enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Yuko App embed is on">
    On the **Dashboard**, the Setup Guide item **Enable Yuko Theme App Extension** should be complete. Click **Refresh status** if you've just turned it on. You can also check in the Theme Editor of your **published** theme under **App embeds → Yuko App**. See [Theme App Embed](/installation-and-setup/theme-app-embed).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm you have an active earn rule and reward">
    In **Loyalty → Programs**, make sure at least one way to earn (for example **Place an order**) and one way to redeem are active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your widgets are switched on">
    Open **Loyalty → Widgets** and check that each widget you want is switched on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the blocks are placed">
    Theme blocks (product, cart, landing page) go in the Theme Editor. Checkout blocks go in the Checkout Editor. Customer account blocks go in the Customer Accounts editor. See [Checkout UI Extension](/installation-and-setup/checkout-ui-extension) and [Customer Account Extension](/installation-and-setup/customer-account-extension).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test on the live storefront">
    Open your store in a private window. Check that Floating Rewards appears, then log in as a test customer and add a product to the cart to see the cart messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place a test order">
    Complete a low-value order as the test customer. Once it reaches the **Earning Status** set in [Loyalty → Settings](/loyalty/program-settings) (Paid, Fulfilled or Delivered), open the customer in **Customers** and check that the points were added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Widget not appearing on the live storefront">
    1. Check that **Yuko App** is on in the **published** theme, not only a preview theme.
    2. Check that the widget is switched on in **Loyalty → Widgets**.
    3. For theme blocks, check that the block was added to that template in the Theme Editor.
    4. For Floating Rewards and Loyalty Nudges, check their visibility settings (device and page rules).
    5. Hard-refresh the page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Points not added after a test order">
    1. Has the order reached the **Earning Status** set in Loyalty → Settings? Points aren't added before then.
    2. Does the Place an order rule have a holding period? Points are held until it ends.
    3. Is the customer excluded, for example by a customer tag excluded in Loyalty → Settings or in the earn rule?
    4. Open the customer in **Customers** to see their activity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Checkout blocks not visible">
    1. Checkout blocks need **Shopify Plus**.
    2. Check that the block was added in the Checkout Editor and saved.
    3. Check that **Checkout: Earn points** or **Checkout: Redeem points** is switched on in Loyalty → Widgets.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Customer account blocks or page missing">
    1. Check that new customer accounts are turned on in Shopify.
    2. Check that the Yuko block was added in the Customer Accounts editor, or that the Yuko Loyalty account page is linked from your customer account menu.
    3. Sign out and back in to refresh the session.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
