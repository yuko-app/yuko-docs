## Introduction

The **Customer Account Extension** adds a Yuko Loyalty hub inside Shopify's new customer accounts surface. Customers see their balance, rewards, VIP status and activity log without leaving Shopify.

## What problem this solves

Many loyalty programs send customers to a separate page to manage rewards. With this extension, the loyalty hub lives natively where customers already manage their orders and profile, dramatically improving discovery.

## Where it appears

- The new Shopify Customer Account experience.
- Both order pages (point balance) and the profile page (full loyalty hub).

## When to use

- You've enabled **New Customer Accounts** in Shopify.
- You want a loyalty hub without building a separate landing page.

## Overview

- Shopify-native extension surface.
- Adds the Yuko Loyalty hub block to customer accounts.
- Works on any paid Shopify plan with new accounts enabled.

## Before you start

<Check>Yuko is installed on your Shopify store.</Check>
<Check>**New Customer Accounts** are enabled in **Settings → Customer accounts**.</Check>

## How it works

When a logged-in customer visits their account, Shopify mounts the Yuko Loyalty extension. It fetches the customer's points, rewards, tier and activity feed and renders them in a single hub.

### Decision guidance

- **Enable both blocks** (orders + profile) so customers see their balance everywhere they expect to.
- **Use the Rewards Landing Page** as a fallback for stores still on legacy customer accounts.

### Limitations

- Requires Shopify's New Customer Accounts. Legacy accounts are not supported.
- Visual customisation is constrained by Shopify's extension surface.

## Examples

- A growth-stage brand replaces a separate `/account/rewards` page with the embedded loyalty hub, simplifying their customer experience.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Add it on day one**. Customers expect to find loyalty inside their account; making them hunt for it costs redemption rate.</Tip>
<Tip>**Pair with a points-balance reminder email** so customers click into the new hub.</Tip>
