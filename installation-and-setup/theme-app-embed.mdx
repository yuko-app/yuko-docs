description: Turn on the "Yuko App" app embed so Yuko's storefront widgets can load on your Shopify theme.

<Note>
**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds → Yuko App
</Note>

## Introduction

Yuko adds one app embed to your theme, called **Yuko App**. It loads Yuko's storefront script and styles on every page of your online store. The floating and pinned widgets render directly through it, and every Yuko theme block gets its content from it.

## What problem this solves

You don't need to edit theme code. One toggle in the Theme Editor turns Yuko's storefront runtime on or off.

## What depends on the embed

| Surface | Needs the Yuko App embed? |
|---|---|
| [Floating Rewards](/onsite-widgets/floating-launcher) | Yes. It renders through the embed, so you don't add a block. |
| [Loyalty Nudges](/onsite-widgets/loyalty-nudges) | Yes. It renders through the embed, so you don't add a block. |
| [Cart Earn](/onsite-widgets/cart-earn) and [Cart Redeem](/onsite-widgets/cart-redeem) messages | Yes. With the embed on, they can be placed in the cart automatically, or with the **Yuko: Cart Earn Message** / **Yuko: Cart Redeem Message** blocks. |
| [Product Page Earn](/onsite-widgets/product-page-earn) (**Yuko:Product Earn Message** block) | Yes. The block is an empty container that the embed's script fills. |
| [Rewards Landing Page](/onsite-widgets/rewards-landing-page) blocks | Yes. The earn, redeem, VIP and membership content is loaded by the embed's script. |
| Referral and membership theme blocks such as **Yuko: Referral Signup** and **Yuko: Member Cart Upsell** | Yes. Their content is filled by the embed's script. |
| [Checkout widgets](/installation-and-setup/checkout-ui-extension) | No. Shopify renders them in checkout. |
| [Customer account blocks and the account page](/installation-and-setup/customer-account-extension) | No. Shopify renders them in customer accounts. |

## When to use

- Always enable the embed on your published theme.
- Enable it on any unpublished theme you're using to preview or test widgets.

## Before you start

<Check>Yuko is installed on your Shopify store.</Check>
<Check>You know which theme is published, plus any theme you're preparing to publish.</Check>

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Theme Editor">
    In your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store → Themes**, find your theme, and click **Customize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open App embeds">
    In the Theme Editor's left sidebar, click the **App embeds** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Yuko App">
    Find **Yuko App** in the list and switch it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the theme">
    Click **Save** in the top right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
You can also start from inside Yuko. On the **Dashboard**, the Setup Guide item **Enable Yuko Theme App Extension** has an **Activate** button that opens the Theme Editor's App embeds with **Yuko App** switched on, ready for you to save. Click **Refresh status** once you've saved. The Cart Earn and Cart Redeem widget pages show an **Enable Theme Extension** button while the embed is off.
</Tip>

## How it works

With the embed on, Shopify adds Yuko's script and stylesheet to every storefront page. The script:

- Reads your widget settings from Yuko and the logged-in customer from Shopify.
- Renders Floating Rewards and Loyalty Nudges if they're enabled in **Loyalty → Widgets**.
- Fills every Yuko theme block it finds on the page.
- Inserts the cart messages into the cart automatically, using your theme's cart area or the **Custom placement** selector you set on the widget.

A widget appears only when it's also switched on in **Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Widgets**.

### Limitations

- The embed is per theme. Turning it on for one theme doesn't turn it on for others, including duplicates.
- App embeds work on any theme, but theme **blocks** (product, cart and landing page) need an Online Store 2.0 theme that supports app blocks.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Check the embed whenever you publish a new theme.** A newly published theme that never had the embed turned on is the most common cause of missing widgets.</Tip>
<Tip>**Turn it on before you add blocks.** Blocks added without the embed show up in the editor but stay empty on the storefront.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My widget shows in the preview but not on the live store">
    The embed is per theme. Make sure **Yuko App** is on in the **published** theme, not just the one you previewed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I show Yuko on only some pages?">
    The embed itself loads on every page. Control where widgets appear from each widget: add theme blocks only to the templates you want, and use the visibility settings on [Floating Rewards](/onsite-widgets/floating-launcher) and [Loyalty Nudges](/onsite-widgets/loyalty-nudges).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need the embed for checkout or customer account widgets?">
    No. Those are Shopify extensions that you add in the Checkout and Customer Accounts editors.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
