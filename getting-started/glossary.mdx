title: Glossary
description: Plain-language definitions of the Yuko terms used in this documentation.
---

Keep this page open in a tab. Terms are grouped by the area of Yuko they belong to, and the names match the labels in the app.

## Loyalty

**Loyalty program**
: The set of rules for how customers earn and spend points in your store. Managed under **Loyalty**, with the tabs **Programs**, **Widgets**, **Emails** and **Settings**.

**Points**
: The currency customers collect. The display name is set by **Point Label** in **Loyalty → Settings**, with singular and plural forms (for example, *point* and *points*).

**Points to Cash Conversion Rate**
: What 100 points are worth in your store currency. New stores start at 100 points = $1. Yuko uses it to show customers what their balance is worth.

**Way to Earn** (also: **campaign**, **earning rule**)
: An action that awards points, such as *Place an order*, *Sign Up*, *Birthday* or *Write a review*.

**Way to Redeem** (also: **reward**)
: Something customers can spend points on, such as an amount discount, a free product or store credit.

**Customer reward**
: A reward issued to a specific customer, such as a discount code generated for them.

**Store credit**
: A balance customers can spend in your store. It can be a reward, a VIP or membership benefit, or a referral reward.

**Activity**
: An entry in a customer's history, created whenever points are earned, redeemed, adjusted or expire.

**Earning Status**
: The order status at which points are credited: Paid, Fulfilled or Delivered.

**Expiry**
: An optional setting on a Way to Earn or reward that makes points or rewards expire after a set period.

**Booster campaign**
: A time-limited or always-on campaign that adds extra rewards on top of your Ways to Earn, such as a Multiplier, Spending Goal, **Order Booster**, Streak or Multi-Action Challenge.

**Order Booster**
: A booster campaign that rewards customers for reaching a number of orders.

**One-time campaign**
: A single drop of points to a group of customers, sent immediately or scheduled.

**Backfill points**
: A one-off award of points to customers for paid orders they placed before Yuko was installed. Found under **Loyalty → Settings**.

## Customers

**Customers**
: The top-level sidebar item where you see every customer's balance, tier and activity, and adjust their points.

**Member**
: A customer who has joined your loyalty program.

**Guest**
: A customer Yuko knows about who hasn't joined the loyalty program.

**Excluded**
: A customer removed from the loyalty program with **Exclude from Program**.

**Adjust balance**
: Manually adding (**Credit**) or removing (**Debit**) points for a customer, with an **Update reason**.

## VIP

**VIP tier**
: A level, such as *Bronze*, *Silver* or *Gold*, that customers enter based on points earned, amount spent or order count. Managed under **VIP** in the sidebar.

**Default tier**
: The entry-level tier. It can't be deleted and its threshold can't be changed.

**Default point multiplier**
: The multiplier applied to points earned by customers in a tier, for example 2x.

**Campaign reward**
: A per-tier override for one Way to Earn: a different multiplier, fixed bonus points, or both.

**Entry benefits**
: Benefits customers receive when they enter a tier, such as discounts, free shipping, free products, bonus points or store credit.

**Tier Identifier**
: A read-only identifier for a tier. Yuko adds it as a Shopify customer tag and uses it for a Shopify customer segment.

**Qualification period**
: The window used to measure tier progress: *Lifetime*, *Rolling year* or *Calendar year*.

**Retention window**
: The time a customer keeps a tier after entering it, even if their activity drops.

## Referrals

**Referrer** (also: **advocate**)
: The existing customer who shares a referral link.

**Friend**
: The new customer who arrives through a referral link.

**Referrer Reward** and **Friend Reward**
: The rewards given to each side of a successful referral.

## Memberships

**Membership plan**
: A paid plan that gives customers recurring benefits. Managed under **Membership → Plans**.

**Members**
: Customers with a membership, listed under **Membership → Members** with statuses such as Active, Paused and Cancelled.

**Benefit**
: Something a VIP tier or membership plan gives customers, such as a discount, free shipping or store credit.

## Widgets and extensions

**Widget**
: Any Yuko element shown on your storefront, in checkout or in customer accounts.

**Floating Rewards**
: The floating rewards button and panel on your storefront, where customers see their points, rewards, tier and ways to earn.

**Loyalty Nudges**
: A strip pinned to the top or bottom of your storefront pages that opens a loyalty drawer with the customer's points, tier and rewards.

**Rewards Landing Page**
: A page on your storefront explaining your program, built from Yuko theme blocks.

**Yuko App embed**
: The theme app embed that loads Yuko on your storefront. It must be on for storefront widgets to work.

**App block**
: A Yuko block you add to a theme section in the Shopify Theme Editor.

**Checkout UI extension**
: Yuko blocks inside Shopify Checkout for showing and redeeming points.

**Customer account extension**
: Yuko blocks and pages inside Shopify's customer accounts.

**Shopify POS tile**
: The Yuko tile on the Shopify POS smart grid, where staff redeem points and apply loyalty discounts in store.

## Emails

**Emails**
: The built-in emails Yuko sends for loyalty, referral and membership events, such as *Points earned* or *Tier unlocked*. Each module has its own **Emails** tab.

**Shortcode**
: A placeholder such as `{{ points_balance }}` that's replaced with real data when the email is sent.

**Email Blacklist**
: A list of addresses or domains that don't receive Yuko emails. Found under **Account → Email Settings**.

## Workflows

**Workflow**
: An automation built from nodes that runs when an event happens.

**Trigger**
: The event that starts a workflow, such as *Earn Points* or *VIP Tier Upgrade*.

**Action node**
: A node that does something, such as **Send Email**, **Coupon**, **Points Credit** or **Points Debit**.

**If / Else**
: A node that splits a workflow based on a condition.

**Time Delay**
: A node that pauses a workflow for a set time.

## Integrations

**Integration**
: A connection to another app, such as Klaviyo, Omnisend, Shopify Flow or a WhatsApp provider. Managed under **Integrations** in the sidebar.

**API key**
: A secret token used to authenticate calls to Yuko's REST API.

## Account

**Account Settings**
: Store-wide settings under **Account**: General, Platforms, Email Settings and Translations.

**Translations**
: Your translations of Yuko's customer-facing labels and content for each store language. Found under **Account → Translations**.
