Yuko's widgets ship as Shopify **app blocks**, **theme app embeds** and **checkout extensions**. To render them, your store needs to meet a few baseline requirements.

## What you need

<Check>An **Online Store 2.0** theme (any modern free or paid theme on the Shopify Theme Store).</Check>
<Check>The **Yuko App** embed enabled for your active theme. See [Enable the Yuko App Embed](/installation-and-setup/theme-app-embed).</Check>
<Check>For Checkout widgets: a **Shopify Plus** store. Shopify only allows checkout UI extension blocks on Shopify Plus.</Check>
<Check>For Yuko blocks in account pages: Shopify's **customer accounts** (new customer accounts) enabled.</Check>

## Theme requirements

### Online Store 2.0

If your theme is an Online Store 2.0 theme it supports app blocks. Practically every theme published on the Shopify Theme Store in the last few years qualifies. Older custom themes may need to be upgraded by your developer.

Quick check:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Theme Editor">
    **Online Store → Themes → Customize** on your active theme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look for Add block">
    Open any section. If you see an **Add block** option that lets you choose from **Apps**, you're on Online Store 2.0.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
If your theme doesn't expose **Add block** for apps, your theme is vintage (Online Store 1.0). You'll need to upgrade to an Online Store 2.0 theme. Contact support if you need a workaround.
</Warning>

### Cart drawer vs cart page

Some themes use a **cart drawer** (slide-out) instead of a full cart page. Yuko's Cart Earn and Cart Redeem widgets support both, but the placement options vary by theme. See each widget's docs for theme-specific placement notes.

## Plan requirements

| Surface | Shopify plan needed |
| --- | --- |
| Theme widgets (product, cart, account, landing, launcher) | Any paid plan |
| Checkout UI Extension (Checkout Earn / Redeem) | Shopify Plus |
| Customer Account Extension (blocks in customer accounts) | Any paid plan with Shopify's **customer accounts** enabled |
| Shopify Flow connector | Plans where Shopify Flow is available |

## What if my store doesn't qualify

- **Older theme** → upgrade to an Online Store 2.0 theme, or contact support.
- **Not on Shopify Plus but want points around checkout** → show earned points on the order status page with the [Order Status Earn](/onsite-widgets/order-status-earn) widget (needs Shopify's customer accounts), and let customers redeem in the cart with [Cart Redeem](/onsite-widgets/cart-redeem).
- **Legacy customer accounts** → either enable Shopify's customer accounts or use the Yuko [Rewards Landing Page](/onsite-widgets/rewards-landing-page) as the loyalty hub.
