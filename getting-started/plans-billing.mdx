## How Yuko billing works

Yuko bills through your Shopify subscription. When you choose a plan inside the Yuko app, Shopify shows you an approval screen for the recurring charge. Once approved, the charge appears on your Shopify invoice along with your other Shopify fees.

- Plans are **monthly** by default.
- You can switch plans at any time. Shopify prorates the charge.
- Cancelling Yuko in the Shopify admin cancels the subscription instantly.

## Decision guidance

- Pick the plan that covers the **next** feature you'll launch, not the current one. Upgrades are a few clicks; downgrades are easier when you've already proven ROI.
- If a single feature is the reason you'd upgrade (e.g., Workflows), validate it on a free trial first.
- Enterprise is the right choice when you need contractual SLAs, custom data residency, or integrations not in the standard catalogue.

## Managing your subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Billing page">
    Login to your Shopify admin, open **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change plan">
    Click **Change plan**, pick the new tier, and confirm the new Shopify charge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View invoices">
    Yuko charges appear on your **Shopify admin → Settings → Billing** invoices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel">
    Uninstall the Yuko app from your Shopify admin to cancel. Your data is retained for a recovery window in case you reinstall.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I trial a plan?">
    Yuko ships with a free trial on most plans. Trial length and conditions are shown on the billing approval screen at install time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel?">
    Loyalty balances, customers, rewards and configuration are retained for a recovery window after uninstall. Reinstall during that window restores your store with no data loss.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there annual plans?">
    Annual billing is available on Pro and Enterprise. Contact [support](https://yuko.so/support/) to switch.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I get charged for development stores?">
    Shopify development stores can install Yuko free for testing. Charges only start when the store is on a paid Shopify plan.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
