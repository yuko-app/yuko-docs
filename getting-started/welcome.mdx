## What is Yuko

Yuko is an all-in-one customer retention platform for Shopify. It bundles the tools modern brands stitch together — loyalty points, referrals, VIP tiers, paid memberships, product reviews, email notifications and workflow automation — into a single app, dashboard and codebase.

This documentation focuses on the **Shopify** experience. WooCommerce-specific guides are out of scope here.

## Install on Shopify

Yuko is a public Shopify app. Installation takes under 5 minutes, and most plans include a free trial.

Already installed? Open Yuko from your **Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty**.

## Who Yuko is for

- **DTC brands** that want repeat purchases driven by an in-house loyalty program.
- **Subscription stores** that need tier-based perks and member-only experiences.
- **High-AOV stores** where word-of-mouth and referrals move the needle.
- **Marketing teams** that want one tool instead of four for retention, reviews and lifecycle email.

## How Yuko fits with Shopify

Yuko plugs into your store via:

- **Theme App Embed** for storefront widgets (product page, cart, floating launcher, landing page).
- **Checkout UI Extension** for points and redemption inside Shopify Checkout (Plus / eligible plans).
- **Customer Account Extension** for the loyalty hub inside the new customer accounts.
- **Webhooks** for orders, customers, carts and refunds.
- **Shopify Flow connector** so Yuko events can trigger Shopify Flow workflows.

No theme code edits are needed. Everything is configured from inside the Yuko app and dropped into your theme via Shopify's app blocks.
