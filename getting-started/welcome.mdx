## What is Yuko

Yuko is a customer retention platform for Shopify. It brings loyalty points, VIP tiers, referrals, paid memberships, email notifications and workflow automation together in one app, with one set of customers and one dashboard.

This documentation covers the **Shopify** version of Yuko.

## Install on Shopify

Yuko is a public Shopify app, and installation takes a few minutes.

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="Install Yuko on Shopify" icon="shop" href="https://apps.shopify.com/yuko">
    Add Yuko to your Shopify store from the Shopify App Store.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Already installed? Open Yuko from your **Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty**.

## Who Yuko is for

- **DTC brands** that want repeat purchases driven by an in-house loyalty program.
- **Subscription stores** that need tier-based perks and member-only experiences.
- **High-AOV stores** where word-of-mouth and referrals move the needle.
- **Marketing teams** that want one tool instead of several for retention and lifecycle email.
- **Omnichannel stores** that want the same loyalty program online and in-store with Shopify POS.

## How Yuko fits with Shopify

Yuko plugs into your store through:

- The **Yuko App** theme app embed and theme blocks for storefront widgets, such as Floating Rewards, product and cart messages and the Rewards Landing Page.
- **Checkout UI extensions** for showing and redeeming points in Shopify Checkout.
- **Customer account extensions** for points, rewards and memberships in Shopify's customer accounts.
- A **Shopify POS** tile for in-store redemption.
- **Shopify Flow** triggers and actions, so Yuko events can drive your Flow automations.

You don't need to edit theme code. You configure everything in Yuko and add widgets with Shopify's theme editor and extension settings.
