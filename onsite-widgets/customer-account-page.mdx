## Introduction

The **Loyalty Rewards: Customer Account Page** widget is a full loyalty page inside Shopify customer accounts. It's the **Yuko Loyalty account page** extension, a full-page extension on the `customer-account.page.render` target, not a block you drop into an existing page.

Customers see their balance and activity, their VIP tier, ways to earn and redeem, their referral link and their membership, all on one page.

## What problem this solves

Customers expect to find their loyalty status where they manage their orders and profile. A dedicated page in the customer account removes the need to hunt for it on the storefront.

## Where it appears

- A dedicated page in Shopify customer accounts.

## When to use

- You use Shopify's customer accounts.
- You want a complete loyalty hub without building a storefront page.

## Overview

- Six sections you can drag into any order: **Header**, **VIP Tiers**, **Ways to Earn**, **Ways to Redeem**, **Referrals** and **Membership**.
- Every heading, label, button and empty state is editable.
- **Ways to earn — order** lets you show booster campaigns or standard campaigns first.
- Membership management modals (manage, update payment, cancel, upgrade, downgrade).
- VIP Tiers, Referrals and Membership sections show only when those features are active for your store.
- This widget is available on select plans.

## Setup guide

### 1. Configure the page in Yuko

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the widget settings">
    Go to **Loyalty → Widgets**, find **Loyalty Rewards: Customer Account Page** under **Post-Purchase**, and click **Customize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order the sections">
    Drag sections by their handle to change the order they appear on the page. The default order is Header, VIP Tiers, Ways to Earn, Ways to Redeem, Referrals, Membership.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit each section">
    Click a section to expand it and edit its text. See [Sections and fields](#sections-and-fields) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and enable">
    Click **Save**. Back on **Loyalty → Widgets**, switch the widget's toggle on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 2. Make the page available in customer accounts

import SetupCustomerAccount from '/snippets/setup-customer-account-extension.mdx';

<SetupCustomerAccount />

The **Yuko Loyalty account page** is a full page, so instead of adding a block, link to it from your customer account menu so customers can open it. See [Customer Account Extension](/installation-and-setup/customer-account-extension) for details.

### 3. Verify

Log in as a customer and open the loyalty page from the customer account. Check each section and the order you set.

## How it works

1. A logged-in customer opens the loyalty page in their customer account.
2. The extension loads their balance, tier, rewards, ways to earn, referral link and membership from Yuko.
3. Sections render in the order you set. VIP Tiers, Referrals and Membership are skipped when those features aren't active.
4. Customers can redeem rewards, copy codes, set their birthday, get their referral link and manage their membership without leaving the page.

### Limitations

- Works only with Shopify customer accounts.
- The page's look follows Shopify's customer accounts styling; there's no custom CSS.
- You can reorder sections but not hide individual ones; VIP Tiers, Referrals and Membership hide automatically when the feature isn't active.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Put what matters most first.** If your program is VIP-led, drag **VIP Tiers** above **Ways to Earn**.</Tip>
<Tip>**Show boosters first** during promotions so time-limited campaigns get noticed.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use this with legacy customer accounts?">
    No. Use the [Rewards Landing Page](/onsite-widgets/rewards-landing-page) instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't the VIP Tiers section appear?">
    The section is skipped when VIP tiers aren't active for your store. If they are, check the customer has been placed in a tier.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is this different from the Order List and Profile widgets?">
    [Order List: Point Balance](/onsite-widgets/order-list-balance) and [Customer Profile: Point Balance](/onsite-widgets/customer-profile-balance) are small blocks on existing account pages. This widget is a full page of its own.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
