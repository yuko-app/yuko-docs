**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Widgets → Customer Profile: Point Balance
</Note>

## Introduction

The **Customer Profile: Point Balance** widget shows a loyalty program card with the customer's points balance on the Profile page of their customer account. It's the **Yuko Profile loyalty program** customer account UI extension block, not a theme app block.

## What problem this solves

The Profile page is where customers manage their details. A loyalty card there makes the program part of their account, not something they have to look for.

## Where it appears

- The Profile page in Shopify customer accounts (`customer-account.profile.block.render`), at the top of the page by default.

## When to use

- You use Shopify's customer accounts.
- You want the balance visible on the Profile page.

## Overview

- Two text fields: a title and a point title.
- No Styles tab; the block uses Shopify customer accounts styling.
- Shows only for customers with a points balance.

## Setup guide

### 1. Configure the widget in Yuko

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the widget settings">
    Go to **Loyalty → Widgets**, find **Customer Profile: Point Balance** under **Post-Purchase**, and click **Customize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the text">
    - **Title**: default `Loyalty program`
    - **Point title**: default `You have earned [point_balance] [points_label]`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and enable">
    Click **Save**. Back on **Loyalty → Widgets**, switch the widget's toggle on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 2. Add the block in the customer accounts editor

import SetupCustomerAccount from '/snippets/setup-customer-account-extension.mdx';

<SetupCustomerAccount />

On the **Profile** page, add the **Yuko Profile loyalty program** block. See [Customer Account Extension](/installation-and-setup/customer-account-extension) for more detail.

### 3. Verify

Log in as a customer with points and open their Profile page.

## Available placeholders

| Placeholder | Replaced with |
|---|---|
| `[point_balance]` | Customer's points balance. |
| `[points_label]` | Your loyalty points label. |
| `[points_cash_value]` | Cash value of the customer's points. |

## How it works

1. A logged-in customer opens their Profile page.
2. The block fetches their balance from Yuko.
3. If they have a balance, the block shows your title and point title. Customers with no points don't see it.

### Limitations

- Works only with Shopify customer accounts; it isn't a theme block.
- The block's look follows Shopify's customer accounts styling.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't a customer see the block?">
    The block is hidden when the customer's balance is zero. Also check that the widget toggle is on and the block is added to the **Profile** page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I want a full loyalty page in the account">
    Use the [Loyalty Rewards: Customer Account Page](/onsite-widgets/customer-account-page) widget.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
