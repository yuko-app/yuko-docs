**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Widgets → Loyalty Nudges
</Note>

## Introduction

Loyalty Nudges is a slim strip pinned to the top or bottom of every storefront page. When a customer taps it, a drawer slides open with their points balance, VIP tier, earned rewards, ways to earn (including active **booster campaigns**) and ways to redeem, without leaving the page.

It's a natural home for [Booster Campaigns](/loyalty/booster-campaigns/overview): once a booster is live, shoppers see it on every page without you sending an email.

## What problem this solves

A loyalty program that only lives in the customer account is invisible to someone browsing products. Loyalty Nudges keeps the program in view on every page with one short line of text, and opens the full detail only when the customer asks for it.

## Where it appears

- A **strip** pinned to the **Bottom of page** (default) or **Top of page**.
- A **drawer** that opens when the customer taps the strip.
- Every storefront page, rendered through the **Yuko App** theme app embed. There's no block to place.

## When to use

- You run booster campaigns and want them visible on every page.
- You want a persistent loyalty surface that carries a short personalised message.
- You want points, rewards, VIP and boosters together in one place on the storefront.

## Overview

- A strip with separate **Logged-out text** and **Logged-in text**.
- A drawer with four sections (**Header banner**, **Your rewards**, **Ways to earn** and **Ways to redeem**) that you can reorder by dragging and switch on or off.
- The header banner has a **Member** version (points and tier) and a **Visitor** version (a "join our loyalty program" card). The rest of the drawer is the same for both, but visitors don't get action buttons until they join.
- Booster campaigns can show all active boosters or a hand-picked list.
- **Visibility** controls for top or bottom placement, device and URL exclusions.
- Colours, **Drawer Height** and **Custom CSS** on the **Styles** tab.
- Each language has its own copy of the text. Use the **Editing language** picker.

## Setup guide

### 1. Configure in Yuko

All content settings are on the **Configuration** tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the widget">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Widgets** and click **Customize** on **Loyalty Nudges**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the strip text">
    In **Widget content → Trigger Strip**:
    - **Logged-out text**, shown to visitors. Default: *Earn `[points_label]` & rewards every time you shop*. Keep it inviting: visitors can still open the drawer and browse.
    - **Logged-in text**, shown to members. Supports `[first_name]`, `[customer_name]`, `[point_balance]` and `[points_label]`, for example *Hey `[first_name]`, you have `[point_balance]` `[points_label]`!*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the drawer content">
    In **Widget content → Drawer Content**:
    - **Drawer title**, shown in the drawer's header bar. Default: *MY LOYALTY PROGRAM*.
    - **Greeting**, shown at the top of the drawer. Supports `[first_name]` and `[customer_name]`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrange the drawer sections">
    In **Drawer sections**, drag **Header banner**, **Your rewards**, **Ways to earn** and **Ways to redeem** into order, tick the ones to show, and expand each one to edit its text (see the table below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose booster campaigns">
    In **Booster campaigns**, keep **Show booster campaigns** on to show boosters inside Ways to earn. Under **Campaigns to show**, leave everything unselected to show every active booster, or select specific campaigns. The **Ways to earn** section must be on for boosters to appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set visibility">
    In **Visibility**:
    - **Placement:** **Bottom of page** (default) or **Top of page**.
    - **Where to display the loyalty strip:** **Desktop and mobile** (default), **Desktop** or **Mobile**.
    - **Hide loyalty strip in URLs that contain:** click **Add new condition** and enter text such as `/blog`. The strip is hidden on every URL that contains it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Style it">
    On the **Styles** tab, set **Primary color**, **Secondary color**, **Text color** and **Body description color**, the **Drawer Height** (30–100 vh; leave empty for the default) and any **Custom CSS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and turn it on">
    Click **Save** and switch the widget on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

#### Drawer section settings

| Section | Settings |
|---|---|
| **Header banner** (Member) | **Points section heading** (default *Your Balance*), **Points balance text** (`[point_balance] [points_label]`), **Points worth text** (*Worth `[points_cash_value]`*), **Points multiplier benefit text** (`[multiplier_value]x multiplier`, shown only when the multiplier is greater than 1), and the **Points balance**, **Points worth value** and **VIP tier** toggles. |
| **Header banner** (Visitor) | **Title** (default *Join Our Loyalty Program*), **Description**, **Create account button** (default *Join Now*) and **Sign in**. In **Sign in**, `[signin:Sign in]` becomes a sign-in link and the text inside becomes the link text. |
| **Your rewards** | **Your Rewards heading**, **Apply button text (Your Rewards)** and **Expiration label (Your Rewards)** (*Expires on `[expiration_date]`*, hidden automatically when a reward doesn't expire). |
| **Ways to earn** | **Ways to earn heading**, **Ways to earn — order** (**Booster campaigns first**, the default, or **Standard campaigns first**), **Edit date button text**, **Save date button text** and **Cancel button text** for birthday campaigns, and **Like**, **Follow**, **Share** and **Visit** button text for social and visit campaigns. |
| **Ways to redeem** | **Ways to redeem heading**, **Redeem button text (Ways to Redeem)**, **Insufficient points text** (*Needs `[points_needed]` more `[points_label]`*) and **Load more button**. |

At least one of **Points balance**, **VIP tier** or **Your rewards** must stay on.

### 2. Add to your storefront

Loyalty Nudges renders on every page through the **Yuko App** embed, so there's no block to add. If the embed is on and the widget is active, the strip appears on your storefront.

### 3. Verify

<Steps>
  <Step title="Test as a visitor">
    Open your store in a private window. The strip shows your logged-out text, and the drawer opens with the visitor header card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test as a member">
    Log in as a customer. The strip switches to your logged-in text, and the drawer shows their balance, tier and rewards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test boosters">
    With at least one active booster campaign, open the drawer and check that it appears under Ways to earn.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test exclusions">
    Open a URL that matches one of your conditions and confirm the strip is hidden.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available placeholders

| Placeholder | Replaced with |
|---|---|
| `[first_name]` | The customer's first name (logged-in only). |
| `[customer_name]` | The customer's full name (logged-in only). |
| `[point_balance]` | The customer's current points balance. |
| `[points_label]` | Your points label from Loyalty → Settings. |
| `[points_cash_value]` | What the balance is worth in your store currency. |
| `[multiplier_value]` | The customer's VIP tier multiplier. |
| `[expiration_date]` | A reward's expiry date. |
| `[points_needed]` | How many more points a reward needs. |

See [all widget placeholders](/onsite-widgets/overview#placeholders).

## How it works

1. The **Yuko App** embed loads Loyalty Nudges on every page, unless the device or URL is excluded.
2. Visitors see the logged-out strip. Members see the logged-in strip, personalised with their name and balance.
3. Tapping the strip opens the drawer with the sections you enabled, in your order.
4. Booster campaigns load live, filtered by your **Campaigns to show** selection, and show progress where the campaign type supports it.
5. Members can apply rewards and redeem points straight from the drawer. Visitors can browse, then create an account or sign in.

### Custom login trigger

By default, the visitor **Create account button** and sign-in link go to the Shopify account login page. If your store uses its own login (a popup or a third-party login app), set a **Custom login trigger** in [Loyalty → Settings](/loyalty/program-settings). Choose **Use a CSS element** and enter a **Login element selector** (such as your theme's login button), or **Use a link** and enter a **Login link**. The same setting applies to Floating Rewards and Cart redeem.

### Decision guidance

- **Top or bottom:** bottom is easier to reach on mobile and stays out of the way of sticky headers. Top is more prominent.
- **All boosters or a hand-picked list:** start with all. Narrow the list if you run many campaigns at once.
- **Boosters first or standard campaigns first:** boosters first puts time-limited offers at the top.

### Limitations

- Loyalty Nudges doesn't render in Shopify Checkout. Use [Checkout Earn](/onsite-widgets/checkout-earn) and [Checkout Redeem](/onsite-widgets/checkout-redeem) there.
- It reaches only customers who are on your store. Use [emails](/emails/overview) for everyone else.

## Examples

- **Double-points weekend:** the drawer opens with your Multiplier booster at the top of Ways to earn on every page.
- **Spending-goal progress:** a Spending Goal booster shows each member how close they are to the bonus.
- **Personal strip:** *Hey Sarah, you have 240 points!* on every page reminds members of their balance.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Turn on Loyalty Nudges before you launch a booster.** It's the simplest way to show the campaign on every page.</Tip>
<Tip>**Personalise the logged-in text.** Use `[first_name]` and `[point_balance]` so the strip reads as personal.</Tip>
<Tip>**Exclude pages where the strip competes.** Blog posts and dedicated landing pages are common choices.</Tip>
<Tip>**Test on mobile first.** Most shoppers will open the drawer on a phone, so check the **Drawer Height** there.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is this different from Floating Rewards?">
    [Floating Rewards](/onsite-widgets/floating-launcher) is a corner button that opens a full panel with VIP, referrals, membership and activity. Loyalty Nudges is a full-width strip with a message, which opens a drawer focused on points, rewards, earning and redeeming. You can use either or both.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why don't I see my booster in the drawer?">
    Check that the booster is active, **Show booster campaigns** is on, the **Ways to earn** section is on, and the booster is selected under **Campaigns to show** (or nothing is selected, so all show). The customer must also match the campaign's targeting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I show different text per language?">
    Yes. Use the **Editing language** picker at the top of the editor. Each of your store's published languages keeps its own copy of the text.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I hide it on certain pages?">
    Yes. Add URL conditions under **Visibility → Hide loyalty strip in URLs that contain**. To show it on only one device type, choose **Desktop** or **Mobile**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How tall is the drawer?">
    Set **Drawer Height** on the **Styles** tab, between 30 and 100 (percent of the screen height). Leave it empty to use the default.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
