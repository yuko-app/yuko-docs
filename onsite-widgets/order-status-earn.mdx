## Introduction

The **Order Status: Earn Points Message** widget tells customers how many points an order earned them, on that order's status page. It's the **Yuko Order status earned points** customer account UI extension block, not a theme app block.

## What problem this solves

Right after buying, customers are most engaged. Confirming the points they earned shows the program is working and plants the idea of the next purchase.

## Where it appears

- The order status page in Shopify customer accounts (`customer-account.order-status.block.render`). By default it sits in the order summary; you can move it in the customer accounts editor.

## When to use

- You use Shopify's customer accounts.
- You want a simple confirmation of earned points after every order.

## Overview

- Two text fields: a title and a message.
- No Styles tab; the block uses Shopify customer accounts styling.
- Shows only for orders that earned points.

## Setup guide

### 1. Configure the widget in Yuko

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the widget settings">
    Go to **Loyalty → Widgets**, find **Order Status: Earn Points Message** under **Post-Purchase**, and click **Customize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the text">
    - **Order status earn title**: default `You have earned`
    - **Order status earn message**: default `[points_value] [points_label] for this order.`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and enable">
    Click **Save**. Back on **Loyalty → Widgets**, switch the widget's toggle on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 2. Add the block in the customer accounts editor

import SetupCustomerAccount from '/snippets/setup-customer-account-extension.mdx';

<SetupCustomerAccount />

On the **Order status** page, add the **Yuko Order status earned points** block. See [Customer Account Extension](/installation-and-setup/customer-account-extension) for more detail.

### 3. Verify

Place a test order as a logged-in customer, then open that order in the customer account. Check that the block shows the points earned.

## Available placeholders

| Placeholder | Replaced with |
|---|---|
| `[points_value]` | The points earned on this order. |
| `[points_label]` | Your loyalty points label. |
| `[points_cash_value]` | Cash value of the points earned. |

## How it works

1. A customer opens an order in their customer account.
2. The block reads the points recorded for that order.
3. If the order earned points, the block shows your title and message. Otherwise it stays hidden.

### Limitations

- Works only with Shopify customer accounts; it isn't a theme block.
- The block's look follows Shopify's customer accounts styling.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't the block show for an order?">
    The block is hidden when the order didn't earn points, for example if no earn rule applied or the customer is excluded from the program. Also check that the widget toggle is on and the block is added to the **Order status** page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is this the same as the checkout earn widget?">
    No. [Checkout: Earn points](/onsite-widgets/checkout-earn) shows the points a customer *will* earn during checkout. This widget confirms the points recorded for a placed order.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
