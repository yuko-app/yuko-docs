## Introduction

The **Product page** widget shows the points a customer will earn by buying the product they're looking at, right on the product detail page.

## What problem this solves

Customers who don't realise they're earning won't change their behaviour because of the program. Showing the earn value on the product page, while the customer is deciding, makes the program visible at the right moment.

## Where it appears

- Product detail pages, wherever you place the **Yuko:Product Earn Message** theme app block.

## When to use

- You want loyalty visible while customers consider a purchase.
- Your order values are high enough that the per-product earn is meaningful.

## Overview

- One message field with placeholders.
- Default or custom icon.
- Styles tab with colours, padding, width, height and **Custom CSS**.
- Updates when the customer switches variants.
- Edit the text per language with the **Editing language** picker.

## Setup guide

### 1. Configure the widget in Yuko

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the widget settings">
    Go to **Loyalty → Widgets**, find **Product page** under **Shopping Cart**, and click **Customize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the message">
    **Points earned on product page**. Default: `You'll earn [points_value] [points_label] on this purchase!`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an icon">
    Under **Customize product message icon**, keep **Default** or choose **Custom** to upload your own icon (JPG, PNG, GIF or WEBP, under 1 MB).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust styles (optional)">
    On the **Styles** tab, set:
    - **Color Configuration:** Border color, Background color, Text color and Button background color.
    - **Widget Style:** Padding (px, default 10), Width (%, default 100) and Height (px, leave empty for automatic).
    - **Custom CSS:** add your own CSS rules for this widget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and enable">
    Click **Save**. Back on **Loyalty → Widgets**, switch the widget's toggle on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 2. Add the block to your product template

The widget editor shows an **Add Block** button that opens the theme editor on your product template with the block ready to add. You can also add it manually:

Pick a **Product** template and add the **Yuko:Product Earn Message** block (no space after the colon). Place it near the price or the **Add to cart** button. The block has no theme editor settings; all text and styling come from Yuko. Once the block is added, the widget editor shows a banner confirming it.

### 3. Verify

Open a product page on your live store and check that the message shows the calculated points.

## Available placeholders

| Placeholder | Replaced with |
|---|---|
| `[points_value]` | Total points earned with the purchase. |
| `[points_label]` | Your loyalty points label. |
| `[points_cash_value]` | Cash value of the points earned. |

See the full [placeholder reference](#full-placeholder-reference) below.

## How it works

1. A customer opens a product page that has the block.
2. Yuko reads the selected variant's price and works out the points from your active earn rules.
3. When the customer switches variants, the message updates to the new variant's price.

### Limitations

- The block shows only on templates where you've added it.
- The widget has no **Custom placement** field; position it with the theme editor.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Keep the message short.** Under 10 words is easy to scan on a busy product page.</Tip>
<Tip>**Use Custom CSS sparingly.** Small spacing or font tweaks are usually enough to match your theme.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I enabled the widget but nothing shows on the product page">
    Make sure the **Yuko:Product Earn Message** block is added to the product template you're viewing, and that the widget toggle is on in **Loyalty → Widgets**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does this widget work on collection pages?">
    No. It's built for product detail templates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I translate the message?">
    Yes. Use the **Editing language** picker at the top of the widget editor to set the text for each language published in your store.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
