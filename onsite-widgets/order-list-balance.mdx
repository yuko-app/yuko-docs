**Where to find this:** Shopify admin → Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Widgets → Order List Balance
</Note>

## Introduction

Display the customer's current points balance at the top of their Shopify order history page — a visible reminder of the program every time they check past orders.

## What problem this solves

Returning customers visit their order history more than they visit your loyalty hub. Surfacing the balance here keeps the program top-of-mind without forcing a click.

## Where it appears

- The customer's order history page (`/account` or new customer accounts).

## When to use

- You want a passive reminder of the program in the customer's account flow.
- You want returning customers to see their balance without seeking it out.

## Overview

- Single-line balance display.
- Customisable message.
- Works on legacy and new customer account pages.

## Setup guide

### 1. Configure in Yuko

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the widget settings">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Widgets → Order List Balance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the message">
    Default: *“Your balance: `[point_balance]` `[points_label]`”*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Toggle the widget **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 2. Add to Shopify

import SetupThemeBlock from '/snippets/setup-theme-app-block.mdx';

<SetupThemeBlock />

When choosing the template, pick the **Customer account / order history** page.

### 3. Verify

Log in as a real customer and confirm the balance is shown above the order list.

## How it works

- Reads the logged-in customer's points balance.
- Renders the message with the balance substituted.
- Updates on page reload (not realtime).

### Decision guidance

- **Pair with the Customer Account Page** for a fully integrated account experience.
- **Use this on legacy customer accounts** if you can't use the Customer Account extension.

### Limitations

- Only renders for logged-in customers.
- Hides itself if the customer has zero balance and no activity.

## Examples

- *“Your balance: 480 Stars”* shown above the customer's order history.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Keep it minimal.** A balance line is enough; don't try to fit the full hub here.</Tip>
