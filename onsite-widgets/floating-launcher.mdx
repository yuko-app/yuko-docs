## Introduction

The Floating Launcher is a persistent button (typically in the bottom-right) on every page of your storefront. Clicking it opens a side panel with the customer's balance, rewards and VIP status.

## What problem this solves

Customers shouldn't have to navigate to a specific page to interact with their loyalty status. A persistent launcher means it's always one tap away — the most discoverable surface in the program.

## Where it appears

- Every storefront page (auto-rendered via the Theme App Embed).
- Position is configurable (bottom-right by default).

## When to use

- You want maximum loyalty discoverability.
- You don't want to manage individual widget placements per template.
- You're optimising for mobile, where a floating button beats inline widgets.

## Overview

- Auto-renders via the Theme App Embed — no per-template setup.
- Configurable button shape, color, position.
- Opens a panel with balance, rewards, VIP, activity.
- Optional notification dot when the customer has unspent rewards.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the widget settings">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Widgets → Floating Launcher**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customise the launcher button">
    - Shape: round / square / pill.
    - Position: bottom-right (default), bottom-left.
    - Icon and label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the side panel content">
    Toggle which sections appear in the panel:
    - Balance
    - Earned rewards
    - Ways to redeem
    - VIP status
    - Activity feed
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the notification dot">
    Show a small badge when the customer has unspent rewards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Toggle **Active**. The launcher renders on every page automatically (via the Theme App Embed).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- The Theme App Embed includes the launcher script on every page.
- The launcher button mounts in the configured corner.
- Clicking opens a panel that pulls live data from Yuko.

### Decision guidance

- **Use the launcher as your primary discoverability surface** — it's the easiest way for customers to find their loyalty info.
- **Match the button color to your brand** — too contrast-heavy and it competes with your CTAs.

### Limitations

- Position is corner-based; mid-page anchoring isn't supported.
- May overlap with chat widgets (Intercom, Tidio, etc.). Stagger positions to avoid this.
- The Theme App Embed must be enabled — see [Theme App Embed](/installation-and-setup/theme-app-embed).

## Examples

- A round green launcher in the bottom-right with a star icon, opening a panel with balance + rewards + VIP.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Don't double up.** If you use the Floating Launcher, you usually don't need the Cart Redeem widget on the cart drawer.</Tip>
<Tip>**Stagger from chat widgets.** If you have Intercom, push the loyalty launcher up or use the opposite corner.</Tip>
<Tip>**Show the notification dot.** It's the single biggest driver of redemption rate via this surface.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I hide the launcher on specific pages?">
    Yes — add page exclusions in the launcher configuration.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="It overlaps my chat widget">
    Move the launcher to the opposite corner or adjust its vertical offset.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it appear at checkout?">
    No — Shopify Checkout doesn't allow custom scripts. Use the [Checkout Earn](/onsite-widgets/checkout-earn) and [Checkout Redeem](/onsite-widgets/checkout-redeem) widgets instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
