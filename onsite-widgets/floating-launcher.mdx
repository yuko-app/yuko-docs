</Note>

## Introduction

Floating Rewards is a button that floats in a bottom corner of every storefront page. Tapping it opens a loyalty panel where customers can see their balance, browse ways to earn and redeem, track VIP progress, apply rewards they've earned, share their referral link and manage a paid membership, without leaving the page they're on.

It's marked **Recommended** on the Widgets list because it's the quickest way to make your program visible everywhere.

## What problem this solves

Customers shouldn't have to hunt for a loyalty page to find out what they can earn or redeem. A floating button keeps the whole program one tap away on every page.

## Where it appears

- Every storefront page, rendered through the **Yuko App** theme app embed. There's no block to place.
- In the bottom-left or bottom-right corner, set separately for desktop and mobile. The default is **left**.
- It doesn't appear in Shopify Checkout. Use [Checkout Earn](/onsite-widgets/checkout-earn) and [Checkout Redeem](/onsite-widgets/checkout-redeem) there.

## When to use

- You want loyalty visible on every page with no theme editing.
- You want one panel that covers points, VIP, rewards, referrals and membership.
- You want to link to a specific part of the panel from your menu, emails or ads.

## Overview

- Ten editor tabs: **Customization**, **Home page**, **Floating Rewards button**, **Birthday**, **VIP**, **Your Rewards**, **Activity log**, **Ways to earn**, **Ways to redeem** and **Membership Detail**.
- Most text tabs have two audiences: **Anonymous** (visitors without an account) and **With account** (logged-in members).
- Home page sections can be dragged into a new order.
- A **Deep link** opens the panel straight to a chosen section.
- Each language has its own copy of the text. Use the **Editing language** picker at the top of the editor.
- The widget renders inside a shadow DOM, so your theme's CSS doesn't leak into the panel and the panel's CSS doesn't leak into your theme. Use the **Custom CSS** section to style it.

## Setup guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the widget">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Loyalty → Widgets** and click **Customize** on **Floating Rewards**. The editor opens on the **Home page** tab, with a live preview you can switch between mobile and desktop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the look in Customization">
    Work through the **Customization** accordions: Colors, Shapes, Placement, Visibility, Banner images, Custom CSS and Deep link. See [Customization](#customization) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the button">
    On the **Floating Rewards button** tab, pick a mode, text and icon for desktop and for mobile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the home page">
    On the **Home page** tab, edit each section for **Anonymous** and **With account** visitors, and drag sections into the order you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the inner pages">
    Update the text on the Birthday, VIP, Your Rewards, Activity log, Ways to earn, Ways to redeem and Membership Detail tabs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and turn it on">
    Click **Save**, then switch the widget on with the toggle. The button appears on your storefront through the Yuko App embed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customization

### Colors

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Primary color** | Button backgrounds, headers, icons and borders. |
| **Secondary color** | Card and surface backgrounds across the widget. |
| **Body text color** | General text for labels, descriptions and content. |
| **Body description color** | Default text colour for elements without their own colour. |

To set these colours across every widget at once, use [Branding & Locale](/onsite-widgets/branding-and-locale).

### Shapes

Each element can be **Square**, **Shaved**, **Rounded** or **Circular**:

- **Panel:** Container, Cards, Buttons and Inputs. Each defaults to **Rounded**.
- **Floating Rewards:** Container, which is the button itself. It defaults to **Circular**.

### Placement

Set on separate **Desktop** and **Mobile** tabs:

- **Placement:** **Left** (default) or **Right**.
- **Side spacing:** 0–100 px. The default is 20 px on desktop and 16 px on mobile.
- **Bottom spacing:** 0–100 px. The default is 20 px on desktop and 16 px on mobile.

Placement moves both the button and the panel.

### Visibility

- **Where to display the Floating Rewards:** **Desktop and mobile** (default), **Desktop**, **Mobile** or **None**.
- **Hide on homepage:** hides the button on your home page.
- **Hide Floating Rewards in URLs that contain:** click **Add new condition**, enter text such as `/blog` and click **Add**. The button is hidden on any URL that contains that text. Add as many conditions as you need.

### Banner images

- **Banner image:** optional. It appears in the panel header.
- **Brand icon:** optional. A small icon shown near the banner area.

Images must be JPG, PNG, GIF or WEBP and smaller than 1 MB.

### Custom CSS

Add CSS to fine-tune the panel and button. Because the widget renders in a shadow DOM, write your CSS here rather than in your theme stylesheet.

### Deep link

Open the panel directly on one section from a menu link, button, email or ad:

1. Under **Choose which part of the panel you want to open**, pick **Ways to earn** (default), **Ways to redeem**, **Points**, **VIP**, **Your rewards** or **Activity**.
2. Copy the link from **Your link**. It looks like `#yuko-ways-to-earn`.
3. Paste it into your navigation as a menu item link. To link from an email or another website, add it to the end of your store URL, for example `https://yourstore.com/#yuko-ways-to-redeem`.

| Section | Link |
|---|---|
| Ways to earn | `#yuko-ways-to-earn` |
| Ways to redeem | `#yuko-ways-to-redeem` |
| Points | `#yuko-points` |
| VIP | `#yuko-vip` |
| Your rewards | `#yuko-your-rewards` |
| Activity | `#yuko-activity` |

## Floating Rewards button

Set on separate **Desktop** and **Mobile** tabs:

- **Mode:** **Icon with text**, **Text only** or **Icon only**.
- **Text:** the button label, shown in the two modes that include text. The default is *Rewards*.
- **Icon:** **Default** or **Upload your own icon**.

## How it works

1. The **Yuko App** embed loads the Floating Rewards script on every storefront page.
2. The button mounts in the corner and at the spacing you set, unless a Visibility rule hides it on that device or URL.
3. Visitors see the **Anonymous** home page, with prompts to create an account or sign in. Logged-in customers see the **With account** version, with their balance, rewards and activity.
4. A URL ending in a `#yuko-<section>` deep link opens the panel straight to that section.
5. Customers can apply earned rewards, redeem points, set their birthday and share their referral link from inside the panel.

### Custom login trigger

By default, a logged-out customer who taps a sign-in link in the panel goes to the Shopify account login page. If your store uses its own login (a popup or a third-party login app), set a **Custom login trigger** in [Loyalty → Settings](/loyalty/program-settings):

- **Use a CSS element:** Yuko clicks an existing element on the page, such as your theme's login button. Enter its **Login element selector**, for example `#login-button` or `a[href='/account']`.
- **Use a link:** Yuko sends the customer to the **Login link** you enter.

The same setting applies to Loyalty Nudges and the Cart redeem widget.

### Limitations

- The button sits in a bottom corner. Mid-page positions aren't supported.
- It doesn't render in Shopify Checkout.
- The **Yuko App** embed must be on. See [Theme App Embed](/installation-and-setup/theme-app-embed).

## Examples

- **Menu link to rewards:** add a "Rewards" item to your main menu that links to `#yuko-ways-to-redeem`, so the panel opens on what customers can spend their points on.
- **Booster spotlight:** turn on **Featured booster** during a double-points weekend so every panel opens with that offer at the top of the home page.
- **Clear of your chat app:** place Floating Rewards on the left and keep your support chat on the right, or raise **Bottom spacing** so the two don't overlap.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Stay clear of chat widgets.** Use the opposite corner, or raise **Bottom spacing**, so Floating Rewards doesn't sit on top of your support chat.</Tip>
<Tip>**Add a deep link to your navigation.** A menu item that opens the panel is easier to find than the floating button alone.</Tip>
<Tip>**Use Icon only on mobile.** On small screens a compact button covers less of your product content.</Tip>
<Tip>**Write both audiences.** Visitors and members see different home pages, so check both in the preview.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I hide the button on specific pages?">
    Yes. In **Customization → Visibility**, tick **Hide on homepage** or add URL conditions under **Hide Floating Rewards in URLs that contain**. To hide it on one device type, set **Where to display the Floating Rewards** to **Desktop** or **Mobile**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I open the panel from my own button or menu?">
    Yes. Copy the link from **Customization → Deep link** (for example `#yuko-vip`) and use it as the link on any menu item or button.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why doesn't my theme CSS affect the panel?">
    Floating Rewards renders inside a shadow DOM to keep its styles separate from your theme. Add styling in **Customization → Custom CSS** instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Customers are sent to the Shopify login page, but my store uses a different login">
    Set a **Custom login trigger** in [Loyalty → Settings](/loyalty/program-settings) so Yuko starts your store's own login flow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I translate the panel?">
    Use the **Editing language** picker at the top of the editor. Each of your store's published languages keeps its own copy of the text. Campaign and reward names are translated in [Translations](/account/translations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it appear at checkout?">
    No. Use [Checkout Earn](/onsite-widgets/checkout-earn) and [Checkout Redeem](/onsite-widgets/checkout-redeem) in Shopify Checkout.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
