## Introduction

Referral widgets are the storefront surfaces of your referral program. They let customers get and share their referral link, and let friends claim their welcome reward when they open a link.

## The widgets

The **Widgets** tab lists three widgets. Each card has a toggle to turn it on or off, and opens its own editor.

| Widget | What it does | Where it appears | Storefront block |
|---|---|---|---|
| [**Post-Purchase Widget**](/referrals/widgets/post-purchase) | Invites customers to get their referral link right after they buy. | Thank-you page and order status page. | **Yuko Referral Signup** (checkout and customer account UI extension). |
| [**Referral Landing Page**](/referrals/widgets/landing-page) | A dedicated page where visitors sign up for a referral link and logged-in customers copy theirs. | A Shopify page, created for you at `/pages/referral-info-page`. | **Yuko: Referral Signup** (theme app block). |
| [**Friend Welcome Popup**](/referrals/widgets/friend-welcome-popup) | The popup friends see when they open a referral link. It collects their details and reveals the friend discount code. | Any storefront page opened from a referral link. | No block. It loads through the **Yuko App** embed. |

Referral links can also be shared from the referral section of the [Floating Rewards launcher](/onsite-widgets/floating-launcher) and the [customer account page](/onsite-widgets/customer-account-page). Those are configured with the loyalty widgets.

## Storefront blocks

### Yuko: Referral Signup (theme app block)

The theme app block behind the **Referral Landing Page**. Add it to any page template in the theme editor. It has its own theme-editor settings:

- **Content:** **Show Banner Image**, **Banner Image**, **Image Position (Desktop)** (Top, Left, Right; default Left), **Text Alignment** (Left, Center, Right; default Center).
- **Colors:** **Background**, **Text**, **Button Background**, **Button Text**, **Border**, **Error Text**.
- **Typography:** **Heading Size** (16 to 48, default 28) and **Text Size** (12 to 24, default 16).
- **Spacing & Shape:** **Border Width** (0 to 5, default 1) and **Corner Radius** (0 to 24, default 8).
- **Custom CSS**.

The text lives in Yuko, under **Referral → Widgets → Referral Landing Page**.

### Yuko Referral Signup (checkout and customer account block)

The extension block behind the **Post-Purchase Widget**. It has two targets:

- The **Thank you** page in the checkout editor.
- The **Order status** page in the customer accounts editor.

Its text lives in Yuko, under **Referral → Widgets → Post-Purchase Widget**. Its look follows Shopify's checkout and customer account styling.

## Before you start

<Check>The referral program is enabled and both rewards are set in **Referral → Program**. Widgets stay hidden until a friend reward exists.</Check>
<Check>The **Yuko App** embed is turned on in your theme. See [Theme App Embed](/installation-and-setup/theme-app-embed).</Check>

## Common editor features

- **Editing language:** each widget editor has a language picker, so you can enter text for every language published in your Shopify store.
- **Reward shortcodes:** text fields accept `{{friend_reward}}` and `{{referrer_reward}}`. They're replaced with the **Title** of the friend and referrer rewards.
- **Form fields:** the Referral Landing Page and Friend Welcome Popup let you choose which fields the form collects (**First Name**, **Last Name**, **Phone**, **Marketing Consent**; **Email** is always included).
- **Preview:** each editor shows a live preview of the widget's states.

## Recommended setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the Friend Welcome Popup">
    Without it, friends who open a referral link can't claim their reward. It's the **Enable referral popup** step in the Program tab's Quick Start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give customers a way to get their link">
    Turn on the **Post-Purchase Widget**, the **Referral Landing Page**, or both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test as a customer and as a friend">
    Get a link as a customer, open it in a private window, and claim the friend reward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why isn't a referral widget showing?">
    Check that the referral program is enabled, a friend reward is set, the widget's toggle is on, and its block (or the Yuko App embed) is added to your theme, checkout or customer accounts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the widget colours?">
    The Friend Welcome Popup has a **Styles** tab in Yuko. The Referral Landing Page is styled in the theme editor through the **Yuko: Referral Signup** block. The Post-Purchase Widget follows Shopify's checkout styling.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
