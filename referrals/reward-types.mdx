## Introduction

The referral program has two independent rewards: the **Referrer Reward** (for the customer who shares) and the **Friend Reward** (for the new customer who opens the link). You can give each side a different kind of reward.

## What problem this solves

Referrers and friends want different things. A referrer may value points or store credit they can use later. A friend needs a clear reason to place a first order. Setting the two rewards separately lets you match each side.

## Available reward types

| Reward type | Referrer | Friend | What the customer gets |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Amount Discount** | Yes | Yes | A discount code for a fixed amount off. |
| **Percentage Off** | Yes | Yes | A discount code for a percentage off. |
| **Free Shipping** | Yes | Yes | A free-shipping discount code. |
| **Free Product** | Yes | Yes | A discount code that makes a chosen product free. |
| **Earn Points** | Yes | No | Loyalty points added to the referrer's balance. |
| **Store Credit** | Yes | No | Shopify store credit added to the referrer's account. |

Friend rewards are always discount codes. The friend sees the code in the Friend Welcome Popup and can apply it at checkout.

<Note>
**Earn Points** needs your loyalty program to be enabled. If it isn't, no points are issued.
</Note>

## Configuration options

When you click **Add Reward** (or **Change Reward**) on a reward card, you pick a type and then fill in its configuration page. Which settings appear depends on the type.

| Setting | Shown for | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Title** | All types | The reward name customers see, for example `$5 off coupon`. Referral widgets show it through the `{{friend_reward}}` and `{{referrer_reward}}` shortcodes. |
| **Value** / **Reward value** | All types except Free Shipping and Free Product | The amount, percentage or number of points. |
| **Applies to** | Amount Discount, Percentage Off | **All products**, **Specific collections** or **Specific products**. |
| **Only apply discount once per order** | Amount Discount applied to specific collections or products | If not selected, the amount comes off each eligible item. |
| **Select product** | Free Product | The product given as the reward. |
| **Shipping restrictions** | Free Shipping | **Countries** (**All countries** or **Selected countries**) and **Exclude shipping rates over a certain amount** with a **Maximum shipping rate**. |
| **Coupon prefix** | Discount-code types | Text added to the start of each generated code (letters and numbers, up to 8 characters). |
| **Coupon expiry** | Discount-code types | Turn on to make the code expire after a **Duration** in Days, Weeks, Months or Years. |
| **Reward expires** | Earn Points, Store Credit | Turn on to make the points or store credit expire after a set duration. |
| **Who can use this reward** | Referrer discount-code types | **Limit the reward usage only to the recipient** restricts the code to the referrer's Shopify customer account. |
| **Purchase type** | Amount Discount, Percentage Off, Free Shipping | **One-time purchase**, **Subscription** or **Both**. Subscription options only take effect if your store sells subscriptions. |
| **Minimum purchase requirements** | Discount-code types | **No minimum requirements**, **Minimum purchase amount** or **Minimum quantity of items**. |
| **Combinations** | Discount-code types | Whether the code combines with **Product discounts**, **Order discounts** and **Shipping discounts** (the shipping option isn't shown for Free Shipping rewards). |

<Info>
Friend discount codes are always limited to the friend's customer account.
</Info>

## Decision guidance

### Picking the referrer reward

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Earn Points">
    Good for engagement-led programs. It ties the referrer back into your loyalty program and lets them redeem on any reward.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Store Credit">
    Good for premium brands. It feels like money on file rather than a coupon.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Amount Discount or Percentage Off">
    Good when you want the referrer to come back and buy again soon.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Free Product or Free Shipping">
    Good when you want a non-cash reward that reads like a gift.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Picking the friend reward

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Amount Discount">
    Easy to explain: "Get $10 off your first order." Add a minimum purchase amount to protect margin.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Percentage Off">
    Works well for higher-AOV stores, where 10 to 20% is a meaningful amount.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Free Shipping">
    Works where shipping cost is a known reason for abandoned carts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Free Product">
    Works for product launches or sample-size items, putting a specific product in the friend's hands.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How it works

- **Friend reward:** Yuko creates the friend's discount code when they claim it in the Friend Welcome Popup. The friend must be a new customer (no previous orders), and each customer can receive only one friend reward.
- **Referrer reward:** Yuko issues it when the friend places a paid order that uses the friend's referral code:
  - Discount-code types create a Shopify discount code for the referrer.
  - **Earn Points** credits the referrer's points balance.
  - **Store Credit** adds Shopify store credit to the referrer's account.
- Expiry is counted from the moment the reward is issued.

### Limitations

- Friends can't receive points or store credit; friend rewards are discount codes only.
- There is one referrer reward and one friend reward per program.
- There's no cap on how many successful referrals one referrer can be rewarded for.

## Examples

| Referrer reward | Friend reward | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| 500 points (**Earn Points**) | $10 off (**Amount Discount**) with a $50 minimum | Pulls the referrer back into the loyalty program and protects margin on the friend's order. |
| $20 **Store Credit** | 15% off (**Percentage Off**) | Discount-led, with a premium feel for the referrer. |
| **Free Product** | **Free Shipping** | Non-cash on both sides; feels like a gift. |

## Best practices

<Tip>**Don't make either side feel cheap.** A $1 discount looks like an afterthought.</Tip>
<Tip>**Keep the two rewards in proportion.** A big friend reward with a tiny referrer reward reduces the referrer's motivation to share.</Tip>
<Tip>**Give each reward a clear Title.** The title is what customers see in the referral widgets.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I give the friend points?">
    No. Friend rewards are discount codes only. Friends earn points the usual way after their first order.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I run several reward configurations at once?">
    No. Each program has one referrer reward and one friend reward. To change one, click **Change Reward** on its card.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I limit how many referrals one customer is rewarded for?">
    No. There's no per-referrer cap.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
