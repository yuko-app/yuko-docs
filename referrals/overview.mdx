## What is the Referral Program

The Referral Program lets your existing customers refer friends. Both sides get rewarded — the **referrer** for sharing, and the **friend** for completing a qualifying action (typically their first order).

It's not a separate app or page; it sits alongside your loyalty program and shares the same customer base, points balance and storefront widgets.

## Who it's for

- Brands with positive word-of-mouth that want to systematise it.
- High-AOV or considered-purchase stores where personal recommendations carry weight.
- Stores looking to lower customer acquisition cost via existing customers.

## How it fits together

```
Customer  ─→  Shares referral link  ─→  Friend clicks link
                                                 │
                                                 ▼
Friend gets a discount on first order  ←──  Friend places order
                                                 │
                                                 ▼
Referrer gets a reward (points / discount / store credit)
```

The referrer reward and friend reward are independent — pick what suits each side.

## Get started

1. Read **[Program Setup](/referrals/program-setup)** end-to-end.
2. Decide rewards in **[Reward Types](/referrals/reward-types)**.
3. Add the **[Storefront Widget](/referrals/widget)** so customers can find their referral link.
