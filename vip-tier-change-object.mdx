---
title: "VIP Tier Change Object"
description: "How VIP tier changes appear in a customer's activities in the Yuko REST API."
---

When a customer enters, moves up or down, or keeps a VIP tier, Yuko records it in the customer's activity log. These records are returned by [List customer activities](/developer/api/list-customer-activities) (`GET /public/customers/activities`, scope `activities` read) as [activities](/activity-object) with `source_type` set to `loyalty_tiers`. To get only tier changes, pass `source_type=loyalty_tiers`.

To read a customer's current tier instead of the history, use [Get customer tier](/developer/api/get-customer-tier).

**id**

**string\<uuid\>**

Unique identifier for the tier change activity.

Example:

`"a1b2c3d4-e5f6-7890-abcd-ef1234567890"`

**customer_id**

**string\<uuid\>**

Yuko's internal UUID for the customer whose tier changed.

Example:

`"63a2c89a-9936-4bc0-9d5f-93db46110b76"`

**activity_type**

**enum\<string\>**

The kind of tier change.

Available options:

`vip_tier_entry` (the customer reached their first tier),

`vip_tier_upgrade`,

`vip_tier_downgrade`,

`vip_tier_retained` (the customer kept their tier at re-evaluation)

Example:

`"vip_tier_upgrade"`

**source_type**

**string**

Always `loyalty_tiers` for tier changes.

Example:

`"loyalty_tiers"`

**source_id**

**string\<uuid\>**

For tier changes, this is the customer's Yuko UUID.

Example:

`"63a2c89a-9936-4bc0-9d5f-93db46110b76"`

**entry_type**

**null**

Always `null`; tier changes don't change the points balance.

**points**

**integer**

Always `0` for tier changes.

Example:

`0`

**earning_type**

**null**

Always `null` for tier changes.

**metadata**

**object**

The tier details of the change.

**metadata.previous_tier**

**object | null**

The tier the customer was in before the change, with `uuid` and `name`. `null` when the customer had no tier (for `vip_tier_entry`).

Example:

`{ "uuid": "3fa85f64-5717-4562-b3fc-2c963f66afa6", "name": "Silver" }`

**metadata.current_tier**

**object | null**

The tier the customer is in after the change, with `uuid` and `name`.

Example:

`{ "uuid": "7fb39c85-3a3f-4247-8333-2e05a9c8ae28", "name": "Gold" }`

**created_at**

**string\<date-time\>**

When the tier change was recorded (ISO 8601).

Example:

`"2025-12-17T12:36:33+00:00"`
