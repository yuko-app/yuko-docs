---
title: "VIP Tier Change Object"
description: "How VIP tier changes appear in a customer's activities in the Yuko REST API."
---

When a customer enters, moves up or down, or keeps a VIP tier, Yuko records it in the customer's activity log. These records are returned by [List customer activities](/developer/api/list-customer-activities) (`GET /public/customers/activities`, requires the `read:activities` scope) as [activities](/activity-object) with `source_type` set to `loyalty_tiers`. To get only tier changes, pass `source_type=loyalty_tiers`.

To read a customer's current tier instead of the history, use [Get customer tier](/developer/api/get-customer-tier).

## Properties

<ResponseField name="id" type="string<uuid>">
  Unique identifier for the tier change activity.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="customer_id" type="string<uuid>">
  Yuko's internal UUID for the customer whose tier changed.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="activity_type" type="enum<string>">
  The kind of tier change. One of `vip_tier_entry` (the customer reached their first tier), `vip_tier_upgrade`, `vip_tier_downgrade`, `vip_tier_retained` (the customer kept their tier at re-evaluation).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="source_type" type="string">
  Always `loyalty_tiers` for tier changes.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="source_id" type="string<uuid>">
  For tier changes, this is the customer's Yuko UUID.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="entry_type" type="null">
  Always `null`; tier changes don't change the points balance.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="points" type="integer">
  Always `0` for tier changes.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="earning_type" type="null">
  Always `null` for tier changes.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="metadata" type="object">
  The tier details of the change.

  <Expandable title="metadata properties">
    <ResponseField name="previous_tier" type="object | null">
      The tier the customer was in before the change. `null` when the customer had no tier (always for `vip_tier_entry`, and for a manual tier assignment to a customer with no tier).

      <Expandable title="previous_tier properties">
        <ResponseField name="uuid" type="string<uuid>">
          The tier's unique identifier.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="name" type="string">
          The tier's name. An empty string if the tier has since been deleted.
        </ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="current_tier" type="object | null">
      The tier the customer is in after the change. `null` for a `vip_tier_downgrade` where the customer dropped out of all tiers.

      <Expandable title="current_tier properties">
        <ResponseField name="uuid" type="string<uuid>">
          The tier's unique identifier.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="name" type="string">
          The tier's name.
        </ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="created_at" type="string<date-time>">
  When the tier change was recorded (ISO 8601).
</ResponseField>
