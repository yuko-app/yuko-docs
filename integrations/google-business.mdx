## Introduction

The Google Business connector authorises Yuko against your Google Business Profile — used in review-related features (more in v1.1 when Reviews documentation lands).

## When to use

- You manage a Google Business Profile and want Yuko to interact with it.
- You're preparing for the Reviews module rollout (v1.1).

## Prerequisites

<Check>You administer the Google Business Profile.</Check>

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the integration">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Integrations → Google Business**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Authorise Yuko via Google's OAuth dialog. Pick the business profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Connection is active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limitations

- Google Business APIs are subject to Google's review and approval cadence — some features may be limited or rate-restricted.
