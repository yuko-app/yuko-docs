## Introduction

[Judge.me](https://judge.me) is a widely used Shopify reviews app. The Judge.me integration lets customers earn **Yuko loyalty points** for the reviews they write on Judge.me.

When connected, Yuko registers webhooks on your Judge.me shop. Each time Judge.me sends a review event, Yuko checks the review and awards points based on your [Write a review](/loyalty/ways-to-earn/write-a-review) earn rule.

## When to use it

- You collect product reviews with Judge.me.
- You want customers who write Judge.me reviews to earn loyalty points.
- You want an extra bonus for reviews that include photos.

## Before you start

<Check>You have an active Judge.me account connected to your Shopify store.</Check>
<Check>You have your Judge.me API token, or you can sign in to Judge.me to connect with OAuth.</Check>
<Check>Your **Write a review** earn rule is enabled and set to the points you want to award.</Check>

## Connect Judge.me to Yuko

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Judge.me in Yuko">
    In Shopify admin, go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Integrations** and click **Judge.me**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an authentication type">
    Under **Authentication Type**, pick **OAuth** or **API Key**.
    - **OAuth**: click **Connect with OAuth**. Yuko redirects you to Judge.me to authorise the connection, then brings you back.
    - **API Key**: paste your Judge.me API token into **API Key** and click **Connect Integration**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the webhook events">
    Under **Webhook Events**, tick **review/created**, **review/updated** or both. See [Which events to enable](#which-events-to-enable).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save settings">
    Click **Save Settings**. Yuko registers the selected webhooks on your Judge.me shop and starts awarding points.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Which events to enable

| Event | Fires when |
|---|---|
| **review/created** | A new review is created in Judge.me. |
| **review/updated** | An existing review is updated, for example when a moderator publishes it. |

Yuko awards points only for **published** reviews. A review that is still pending moderation when Judge.me sends the event doesn't earn anything yet.

- **If Judge.me auto-publishes reviews**, enabling **review/created** is enough.
- **If you moderate reviews before publishing**, also enable **review/updated**, so the customer earns when the review is published.

<Tip>Enabling both events doesn't double-award. Yuko awards the base points only once per customer per product, and the photo bonus only once.</Tip>

## How the reward works

1. A customer writes a review on your store using Judge.me.
2. Judge.me sends the review event to Yuko.
3. Yuko matches the reviewer to a Yuko customer, first by Shopify customer ID and then by email, and matches the reviewed product.
4. If the review is published and the **Write a review** rule is active, Yuko awards the rule's points.
5. If the review includes photos and **Enable Media Earning** is on in the **Write a review** rule, Yuko also awards the **Media earning value** as a bonus.

The customer sees the points in their loyalty balance, and any points-earned email you have turned on is sent as usual.

## Example use case

**Moderated reviews, 50 points plus a 25-point photo bonus**

1. In Yuko, open the **Write a review** earn rule, set it to 50 points, turn on **Enable Media Earning** with a **Media earning value** of 25, and save.
2. Connect Judge.me in **Integrations**.
3. Tick **review/created** and **review/updated**, then click **Save Settings**.
4. When a moderator publishes a review with a photo, the customer earns 50 + 25 points. Later edits to the same review don't award again.

## Limitations

- **The reviewer must match a Yuko customer.** Reviews from people Yuko can't match by Shopify customer ID or email are skipped.
- **The product must exist in Yuko.** Reviews for products Yuko hasn't synced are skipped.
- **Only published reviews earn.**
- **Points are set in the earn rule.** There's no per-review override.
- **Points aren't reversed.** If a review is later deleted or unpublished in Judge.me, the points stay with the customer.
- **No historical reviews.** Yuko only receives new and updated reviews after you connect. Reviews written before that don't earn points.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What if a review is deleted in Judge.me after points were awarded?">
    Yuko doesn't revoke the points automatically. If you need to reverse an award, adjust the customer's points manually from [Customers](/loyalty/customers).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I award extra points for photo reviews?">
    Yes. Turn on **Enable Media Earning** in the **Write a review** earn rule and set a **Media earning value**. Judge.me reviews with photos earn that bonus on top of the base points.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can customers earn for reviews they wrote before I connected Judge.me?">
    No. Yuko only receives review events sent after the webhooks are registered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I use a different reviews app. Can it award points?">
    Yes, if it can trigger Shopify Flow. Use the **Yuko Add Points for Review** action in [Shopify Flow](/integrations/shopify-flow) to send reviews from other apps to your **Write a review** earn rule.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I switch Judge.me accounts or rotate the token?">
    Disconnect Judge.me in Yuko, which also removes the webhooks Yuko registered. Then connect again with the new token or account and save your webhook events.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
