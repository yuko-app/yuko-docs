## Introduction

The General tab holds a few store-wide preferences used across Yuko. Set them once during setup; they rarely need to change.

## What's configured here

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Timezone** | Your preferred timezone for the app. Yuko uses it for date-based schedules, such as birthday and reminder timing. |
| **Date Format** | How dates are shown: MM/DD/YYYY, DD/MM/YYYY, YYYY-MM-DD, January 1, 2023, MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY. The default is month-day-year. |
| **Time Format** | 12-hour format (1:30 PM), 24-hour format (13:30), 12-hour with seconds (1:30:00 PM) or 24-hour with seconds (13:30:00). The default is 12-hour. |
| **Coupon Prefix** | The prefix used for generated coupon codes. The default is `YK_`. |
| **Logo** | The logo displayed in emails and other communications. |

Your store name, currency and languages come from Shopify and aren't edited here. To translate customer-facing text, use [Translations](/account/translations).

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open General">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Account → General**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the timezone">
    Search for and pick your store's primary timezone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose date and time formats">
    Pick the formats your team and customers expect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the coupon prefix">
    Enter 2–6 characters using letters, numbers and underscores only, for example `YK_` or `VIP`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    Click **Customise** next to **Logo**, upload an image and check the preview. Images must be under 1 MB; around 200 × 50 pixels works best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** in the save bar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

<Tip>**Use your store's primary timezone**, not the timezone of whoever set up the app, so date-based emails go out at sensible times.</Tip>
<Tip>**Keep the coupon prefix short and recognisable.** It makes Yuko codes easy to spot in Shopify's discount reports.</Tip>
<Tip>**Use a wide, transparent logo.** It reads better in email headers than a square icon.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I change the store currency here?">
    Yuko uses your Shopify store currency for points value, discounts and store credit, so it always follows Shopify.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the coupon prefix change existing codes?">
    No. Codes that were already issued keep their current prefix. The new prefix applies to codes generated after you save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my coupon prefix rejected?">
    It must be 2–6 characters long and contain only letters, numbers and underscores.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
