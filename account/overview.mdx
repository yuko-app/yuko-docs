## What's in Account Settings

Account Settings hold store-wide preferences that apply across loyalty, referrals and memberships. On Shopify stores, there are four tabs:

| Tab | What you configure |
| --- | --- |
| [General](/account/general) | Timezone, date and time format, the default coupon prefix and your logo for emails |
| [Platforms](/account/platforms) | Your Shopify connection details and customer and product sync |
| **Email Settings** | **General**: your sender identity (**From Name** and **Reply Email**), the **Default From Address** and custom sending domains. See [Sender and branding](/emails/sender-and-branding). **Email Blacklist**: addresses that should never receive Yuko emails. See [Email Blacklist](/account/email-blacklist). |
| [Translations](/account/translations) | Translations of storefront labels and your campaign and reward content for each store language |

Integrations such as Klaviyo, Shopify Flow, Shopify POS and the REST API have their own **Integrations** item in the sidebar. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview).

## Decision guidance

- Set **General** during initial setup. Your timezone controls when date-based events, such as birthdays and anniversaries, happen.
- Use **Platforms** to sync existing Shopify customers and products into Yuko, or to re-sync after a large import.
- Set up **Email Settings** before you turn on emails, so customers see your brand name and reply address.
- Use **Translations** if your Shopify store publishes more than one language.
