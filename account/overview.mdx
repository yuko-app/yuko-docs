## Decision guidance

- Configure **General** during initial setup and revisit if your store currency or timezone changes.
- Configure **Email Blacklist** before launching your referral program — disposable-email domains are the most common referral fraud vector.
- Configure **Members** as soon as you bring teammates into the loyalty program.
