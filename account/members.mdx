## Introduction

Members are teammates who have access to the Yuko dashboard. Each member has a role that determines what they can see and do.

## What's configured here

- The list of members.
- Each member's role (Admin, Editor, Viewer — or as configured by your plan).
- Pending invites.

## When to use

- Onboarding teammates to Yuko.
- Restricting access to sensitive actions (e.g., manual point adjustments).
- Removing access when someone leaves.

## Roles

- **Admin** — full access, including billing, member management and program reset.
- **Editor** — can edit campaigns, rewards, widgets, emails and workflows. Cannot manage members or billing.
- **Viewer** — read-only across the dashboard. Useful for stakeholders.

<Note>
Role names and capabilities depend on your Yuko plan. Check the in-dashboard role descriptions for the canonical list.
</Note>

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Account → Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a member">
    Click **Invite member**. Enter the email and pick a role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    The invitee receives an email with a sign-up link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify access">
    Once they accept, they appear in the list with their role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Removing a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the member">
    Click their row in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Remove">
    Confirm the removal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Audit">
    The member's recent activity remains in audit logs but they can no longer access the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

<Tip>**Use Editor by default** — reserve Admin for genuine admins.</Tip>
<Tip>**Audit members quarterly.** Stale access is a real risk.</Tip>
<Tip>**Pair Members with role-restricted actions** — e.g., Editors can adjust campaigns but only Admins can run **Reset activities**.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does this relate to Shopify staff accounts?">
    Yuko members are independent of Shopify staff. A Shopify staff account doesn't automatically grant Yuko access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I see what a member did?">
    Activity feeds in **Customers** record the member name on every manual action (credit, debit, redeem revoke, etc.).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
