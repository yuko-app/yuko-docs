## Introduction

The Platforms tab shows the commerce platforms connected to your Yuko organisation — typically just your Shopify store, but if you operate multiple Shopify stores under one brand, this is where you manage the connections.

## What's configured here

- The list of connected Shopify stores.
- The primary store (used as the source of truth for shared loyalty data).
- Connection status for each.

## When to use

- During initial setup, to confirm the Shopify connection is active.
- When adding a second Shopify store under the same Yuko organisation.
- When troubleshooting a sync issue.

## How multi-store works

- Each Shopify store has its own Yuko install.
- A single Yuko organisation can group multiple stores.
- Data sharing across stores depends on your plan — contact support for multi-store coordination.

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Platforms">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Account → Platforms**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the Shopify connection">
    The store should show as **Connected**. If not, reinstall the Yuko app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add another store (optional)">
    Multi-store setup is plan-gated. Contact [Support](https://yuko.so/support/) to enable it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best practices

<Tip>**Don't disconnect the platform** unless you intend to uninstall — disconnection breaks all loyalty syncing.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I run Yuko on Shopify and WooCommerce simultaneously?">
    Yes, but it's an Enterprise configuration. Contact support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my Shopify store shows as Disconnected?">
    Reinstall Yuko from the Shopify App Store. Existing customer data is retained.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
