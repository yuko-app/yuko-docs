## Introduction

The Platforms tab shows your Shopify connection and lets you sync your store's existing customers and products into Yuko. New customers and products are picked up automatically after install. A manual sync is for bringing in your existing data or refreshing it later.

## What's configured here

- **Connection status**: a badge next to the **Shopify** title shows **Connected** when Yuko can reach your store.
- **Connection Details**: your read-only **Store URL**, with a button to open your storefront.
- **Data Synchronisation**: a three-step sync of customers and products, plus the status of your most recent sync.

## When to use

- Right after installing Yuko, to bring existing customers and products into Yuko.
- Before importing customer points from a CSV in **Customers**. The import needs a completed sync.
- When customers or products in Yuko look out of date compared with Shopify.

## Sync your store data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Platforms">
    Go to **Apps → Yuko Loyalty → Account → Platforms**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fetch Data">
    Under **Sync Progress**, click **Fetch Data**. Yuko connects to your Shopify store and counts the available data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Store Data step">
    Yuko shows how many customers and products it found and that they're ready to synchronize.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sync Now">
    Yuko imports the customers and products. The **Complete** step shows the overall status: **In progress**, **Completed** or **Failed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Recent Sync status">
    **Recent Sync status** shows the latest result for **Customers** and **Products** separately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To run another sync later, click **Start Sync Again** and repeat the steps.

## Best practices

<Tip>**Sync before launch** so existing customers can see their balance and tier as soon as your program goes live.</Tip>
<Tip>**Sync before a points import.** The customer points import in [Customers](/loyalty/customers) needs a completed sync first.</Tip>
<Tip>**Re-sync after bulk changes** in Shopify, such as a large customer import, if the data doesn't look right in Yuko.</Tip>

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to sync to get new customers into Yuko?">
    No. New customers and products are picked up automatically. The sync is for existing data and for refreshing it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The sync shows Failed. What should I do?">
    Click **Start Sync Again** and run it again. If it keeps failing, [contact support](https://yuko.so/support/).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I connect more than one store?">
    Each Shopify store installs Yuko separately and has its own settings, customers and program.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if Yuko can't reach my store?">
    Reinstall Yuko from the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/yuko). If the connection still doesn't show as **Connected**, contact support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
