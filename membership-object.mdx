---
title: "Membership Object"
description: "Fields returned for a customer's loyalty membership summary by the Yuko REST API."
---

The membership object summarizes a customer's standing in your loyalty program: points balance, current VIP tier, next tier and progress toward it. It's returned by [Get customer membership](/developer/api/list-memberships) (`GET /public/customers/memberships`, requires the `read:customers` scope).

<Note>
This object describes loyalty program membership. Paid membership plans (Membership → Plans) aren't available through the REST API.
</Note>

## Properties

<ResponseField name="status" type="string">
  The customer's membership status. Always `active` for a customer returned by this endpoint.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="member_since" type="string<date-time>">
  When the customer was added to Yuko (ISO 8601).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="points" type="object">
  The customer's points summary.

  <Expandable title="points properties">
    <ResponseField name="balance" type="integer">
      The customer's spendable points balance. Always `0` for a customer who is banned or excluded from the loyalty program by a customer tag.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="earned" type="integer">
      Total points the customer has earned.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="redeemed" type="integer">
      Total points the customer has redeemed.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="current_tier" type="object | null">
  The customer's current VIP tier, or `null` if VIP tiers are turned off or the customer hasn't reached a tier.

  <Expandable title="tier properties">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string<uuid>">
      The tier's unique identifier.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="name" type="string">
      The tier's name.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="threshold_value" type="integer">
      The value needed to reach the tier.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="multiplier" type="number">
      The tier's points multiplier, `1` when none is set.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="icon" type="object | null">
      The tier's icon.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="benefits" type="object[]">
      The tier's benefits.

      <Expandable title="benefit properties">
        <ResponseField name="name" type="string">
          The benefit's name.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="benefit_type" type="enum<string>">
          One of `fixed`, `percentage`, `free_product`, `free_shipping`, `bonus_points`, `store_credit`, `custom`.
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="benefit_value" type="object">
          The benefit's value configuration. Its structure depends on `benefit_type`; see [List VIP tiers](/developer/api/list-vip-tiers).
        </ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="created_at" type="string<date-time>">
      When the tier was created.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="next_tier" type="object | null">
  The next VIP tier the customer can reach, with the same fields as `current_tier`. `null` if VIP tiers are turned off, the customer hasn't been evaluated for tiers yet, or the customer is already at the highest tier.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="progress_to_next_tier" type="object | null">
  The customer's progress toward `next_tier`, or `null` if VIP tiers are turned off or there's no next tier.

  <Expandable title="progress properties">
    <ResponseField name="current_value" type="integer">
      The customer's current qualifying value, rounded down.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="required_value" type="integer">
      The value needed for the next tier.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="remaining_value" type="integer">
      How much more is needed.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="progress_percentage" type="number">
      Progress as a percentage, rounded to 2 decimal places.
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="threshold_type" type="string | null">
      The metric used for tier progression (`total_earned_points`, `total_amount_spents` or `total_orders`). Can be `null` even when VIP tiers are set up.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
